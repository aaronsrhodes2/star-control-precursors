# The Lemmkin — Recklessly Curious Engineers

> Canonical full species canon for the Lemmkin, consolidating prior MEMORY canon (anthropomorphic squirrels; no fear; lemming-clade troupe behavior; Homesteader-by-choice) and extending with Aaron's 2026-05-18 dispatches:
>
> *"Lemmkin are great. No fear, only curiosity, and they breed quite fast to make up for the losses. They are excellent engineers, though safety precautions being not on the requirements list causes fast breakthroughs and cause their inventions to break things fast. It's absolute chaos, danger and discovery on the planet. Travel there is NOT recommended without proper safety equipment, which you cannot buy planetside."*
>
> *"When we inform the Lemmkin of the impending doom, they were so excited to see what The Others looked like, and what they could do, and the advanced technology, and, yadda yadda... the entire Lemmkin leadership will vote to go with the Precursors or stay to see what happens based on your quest and dialog outcomes. Your quest will be to find something more interesting than being eaten by The Others so they will leave with the precursors for it."*
>
> The canonical Lemmkin are the slice's most cheerful catastrophe — a species so curiosity-driven that the only path to saving them is **out-interesting the Others**.
