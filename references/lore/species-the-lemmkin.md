# The Lemmkin — *The Recklessly Curious* (Homesteader-by-choice; staying to watch)

> Authored 2026-05-17 from Aaron's premise: anthropomorphic squirrels
> with **absolutely no fear**; they discover at a breakneck rate and break
> a lot of necks along the way; they are Homesteaders because they
> stubbornly want to *see what happens*. Replaces the CURIOUS placeholder
> row.

## The Pun (canon-aware)

**Lemmkin** = *lemming* + *-kin*. The species's lemming-like collective
behavior is canonical: when one Lemmkin investigates something, the
others follow, sometimes off cliffs (literally — they have a measurable
cliff-mortality rate that no other sapient species in the cluster comes
close to). The *-kin* suffix marks them as a family-clade species:
they think of themselves as one big extended troupe, not as separated
populations.

The Lemmkin themselves love the name. The Furling translator coined it,
the Lemmkin received it with delight (*"Yes! That is exactly what we
are! How did you see us so clearly so quickly?"*), and they now use it
in their own speech.

## Body Plan

- **Form**: anthropomorphic squirrels. Bipedal, ~1.1m tall, lightly
  built (~25kg), agile, very fast on level ground and in trees.
- **Head**: large round eyes (overlarge for the skull — they want to
  see everything); twitchy nose; small round ears; cheek pouches that
  expand when the Lemmkin is excited.
- **Body**: dense brown-and-cream fur in regional palettes; long
  prehensile tail used for balance, gesture, and tool-holding.
- **Hands**: four-fingered + opposable thumb; nimble; constantly in
  motion — when a Lemmkin isn't manipulating something, they
  *fidget* with their own tail-tip.
- **Locomotion**: bounce-step rather than walk; leap rather than jump.
  A Lemmkin in a hurry covers ground faster than a Furling at a sprint
  — they are *fast.* Their cliff-mortality rate comes from this:
  they leap before measuring.
- **Lifespan**: ~30 Furling years. Short by Furling standards. They
  are aware of this and consider it a *feature* — they cram an
  enormous amount of doing into the time.

## Cognition & Psychology

- **No fear.** Canonical. Their amygdala-equivalent is vestigial —
  Furling neurologists have studied this and confirmed: the Lemmkin
  genuinely do not produce fear-response signals to threats of any
  scale. They flinch (reflexive) but they do not *dread.* Death is
  abstract to them in the same way that other species find calculus
  abstract.
- **Intense curiosity.** What replaces fear is *curiosity-drive* at
  unprecedented intensity. A Lemmkin walking into a room scans every
  surface, every object, every shadow, looking for *the new thing.*
  If they find one, the entire troupe pauses to investigate.
- **They are clever.** This is important: the Lemmkin are not stupid
  (unlike the Thinn). They are *smart-and-reckless-simultaneously.*
  Their scientific output is enormous. Their casualty rate from
  preventable accidents is also enormous. Furling researchers have
  tried to teach them caution; the Lemmkin agree caution is wise
  AND continue to leap off the cliff *because they wanted to know
  what was at the bottom.*
- **They die learning.** This is the slice's purest expression of
  their philosophy: *"It is acceptable to die if I learned
  something. It is not acceptable to live without having learned."*
  Spoken cheerfully, not tragically.
- **Lemming-clade behavior** (where the name comes from): when one
  Lemmkin makes an interesting discovery, others swarm to it. Often
  this is fine. Sometimes the discovery is *"the cliff has an
  unstable lip"* and the discoverer has already fallen, in which
  case several more Lemmkin follow them down before the troupe
  notices that the lip is unstable. They are working on better
  signaling protocols. The protocols don't take. Curiosity is
  faster than protocol.

## Speech & Voice

- **Voice**: high-pitched, chittery, fast. Multiple questions per
  sentence. The translator renders it as a *bright fast cadence*
  with rising-tone interrogatives at every clause break.
- **Pronouns**: first-person singular ("I", "me") AND
  first-person-plural-of-troupe ("we", "the troupe"). They switch
  fluidly — *"I saw something! We should see it! The troupe will
  see it together! What is it?"*
- **Tone**: relentlessly cheerful. Even discussing the Others'
  arrival, the Lemmkin sound *excited.* This is unsettling on
  first encounter and charming on second.
- **No hesitation markers.** They don't say "um" or "uh." If a
  Lemmkin doesn't know something, they ask. They don't pause to
  consider; they outsource consideration to whoever they're
  talking to.
- **Frequent interruption** (of themselves and others) — when a
  new question occurs, they ask it immediately. The translator
  marks interruptions with em-dashes inside the same speaker's
  line.
- **Sample line**:
  > *"Hello! Are you a Furling? You are a Furling. We have only
  > met three Furlings — wait, four, the troupe says four — are
  > you the fourth? What is your name? What does it do? Is your
  > ship the small fast kind or the big slow kind? Can we see it?
  > We will not break it. We will only look. Can we look? Why are
  > you looking at us like that?"*

## Faction Alignment

**Homesteader (Stay-Side).** *Not* by biological necessity (the Lemmkin
could absolutely migrate — they have ships, they have FTL, they have
the tech to ride a Furling lift). **They are staying because they
want to be present for the most interesting event in their species's
history.**

This is canonically unique in the slice: every other Homesteader has a
*reason they can't leave* (Slylandro/Chenjesu/Mmrnmhrm/Taalo biology;
Burvixese doctrinal commitment; Utwig chosen devolution; Thinn
dimensional barrier). The Lemmkin are staying *purely by choice* —
the Furlings have explicitly offered them passage, the Lemmkin have
explicitly declined, with cheerful clarity.

The Lemmkin elder's canonical statement to the Council:

> *"You are kind to offer. We have considered. We will stay. We want
> to see what they look like. We want to see what they do. We have
> heard you describe them and the descriptions are not enough. We
> need to see. Some of us will leap; some of us will follow; we
> have arranged it so the ones who write things down are at the
> back. The notes will survive even if we do not. Thank you for
> the lift. We will not be on it."*

The Furling Persuader-faction commander filed a formal protest
recommending forced Migration; the Council rejected it 7-3 with two
abstentions. Lemmkin self-determination is canonically respected.

## Survival Outcome

**Eliminated** (canonical). They will be consumed in the Culling.
They know. They are not afraid. They are *taking notes.*

**The Lemmkin's pre-Culling research dispersal**: aware they cannot
themselves survive, the Lemmkin have undertaken a frantic
multi-decade project to *distribute their scientific archives* across
the galaxy — every cluster gets a copy of every Lemmkin paper, every
observation, every dataset. The Furling Hider faction is one of the
recipients. **Lemmkin science survives even though Lemmkin don't.**
This is canonical and is recovered by SC2-era archaeologists in
fragments; many "found Precursor data caches" in SC2 lore are
canonically Lemmkin archives mistakenly attributed.

> *"You will find our papers. Read them. Some are wrong. Most are
> better than they should be. We made up for slow with many."*

## In the Slice

**Encounter location**: the Lemmkin homeworld is **Whirligig** — a
densely forested terrestrial world in the slice cluster, named by
the Lemmkin themselves for its rapid axial rotation (~6-hour day).
The forest canopy is the Lemmkin's primary habitat; their cities are
built into the upper branches of kilometer-tall trees.

**Encounter shape**: the Steward arrives at Whirligig and is
*immediately surrounded* by curious Lemmkin. A dozen of them, all
asking questions simultaneously. After some minutes, an elder
designated **Brisk-Ever-Onward** establishes order (briefly) and
the dialog proper begins.

The Lemmkin are *fascinated* by the Steward — they have heard about
Furlings but never met one face-to-face. They want to know
everything. They will *ask* everything. The Steward can:

- **Honor their choice to stay** — leave them to their fate;
  terminal Eliminated; their archives reach the Hider faction
  (Bio-Archive gain)
- **Convince a small contingent to evacuate** — possible but
  difficult; the few Lemmkin who agree are the *quietest* ones
  who already lost troupe-members to cliffs
- **Cleanse path** — the Lemmkin will *not* fight back (no fear
  ≠ no docility, but they don't consider violence interesting);
  they will continue asking the Steward questions even during the
  Cleansing. Canonically one of the slice's most unsettling
  moments because their cheerfulness doesn't break. Cleanser
  doctrine strongly disagrees with this branch
- **Trade Lemmkin science for Furling tech** — they have surplus
  scientific output; they will trade enthusiastically for
  anything they don't already know

**Quest**: `lemmkin_curiosity` in `tools/quest_inventory.csv` — full
state machine pending. Initial sketch:
- Honor stay → terminal Eliminated + Lemmkin archives in Bio-Archive
- Mixed-evac → small Migrated contingent + most Eliminated
- Cleanser → Eliminated + Persuader/Hider standing collapse
- Science trade → grants a Lemmkin Research Cache module (TBD with
  Design — a sensor/scanner upgrade fueled by Lemmkin's eclectic
  research, perhaps `lemmkin_pattern_database`)

**Award**: Bio-Archive entry + Lemmkin archive fragment (Hider
faction interest) + optional unique sensor module from science trade.

## Ship Design

**The Lemmkin Skitter** — small fast multi-direction vessel.

- **Form**: small angular hull, ~30m long, painted in bright contrasting
  colors (the Lemmkin like to *find* their own ship in the sky); four
  thruster nacelles arranged around a central spine that can fire in
  any direction.
- **side**: homesteader
- **points**: ~90 (lightweight glass cannon)
- **hull_max**: 35 (very fragile — the Lemmkin don't armor their
  ships because they trust speed)
- **shield_max**: 0 (no shields)
- **top_speed**: 380 (faster than the Thinn Blade)
- **turn_rate**: 4.5 (very fast turn — exceptional maneuverability)
- **acceleration**: 280 (snap-acceleration)
- **ai_style**: clever_flier (high curiosity-aggression — the AI
  pilot will fly *too close* to the enemy to see them better;
  exploitable)

**Primary weapon — Burst-Scatter Probe.** A scatter-shot of
investigation probes that double as light kinetic weapons. The
probes fan out, hit-or-miss, and the Lemmkin pilot enjoys watching
where they land. Low damage individual; reasonable damage on full
hit cluster.

- primary_damage: 2 per probe, 5 probes per shot
- primary_energy: 5
- primary_rate: 2 shots/sec
- primary_range: 700
- primary_speed: 600

**Special — Tail-Drop.** The Lemmkin Skitter executes an instant
180-degree pivot while continuing forward, allowing it to *fire
backwards while still moving forward.* For 1.5 seconds the ship is
in this twisted state — firing back, moving forward, very
exposed-broadside. Costs 25 energy. Cooldown 6 seconds. Tactical
note: brilliant in pursuit-flee situations; suicidal in head-on
engagements.

## What the Lemmkin Add to the Slice's Thematic Picture

The slice's species each test a theory about how to face the
Others. The Lemmkin test the *contrarian* theory: **what if we
just… didn't try to survive, and instead used the time to learn?**
Every other Homesteader has a survival strategy (cloak, devolve,
edge-on, be-loud, shield, biology). The Lemmkin's strategy is *no
strategy* — they accept the cost and bank on knowledge instead.

This makes them the slice's clearest thematic foil to the Burvixese:
- **Burvixese**: thought their Be-Loud doctrine would save them.
  Doctrine fails. They die *believing they were going to live.*
- **Lemmkin**: knew they wouldn't survive. Made no attempt. They
  die *learning.*

Both end Eliminated. The dignity comes from different places. The
Burvixese tragedy is about a doctrine that failed; the Lemmkin
tragedy is about a species that chose understanding over continuity.

They're also the slice's clearest example of **humor and horror in
the same character**. The Lemmkin are funny *because* their lack of
fear is sincere; they are horrifying *because* it's also sincere.
Their cheerfulness during a Cleanser path is canonically one of the
slice's most unsettling beats — they keep asking the Steward
questions during the Cleansing, and the Steward's wit options
disappear.
