## 3. Voice and demeanor — the dual register

Halia operates in **two canonical registers** with a canonical code-switch between them. The Steward (and the player) canonically learns to read the switch.

### 3.1 Private register — *will joke; gruff-warm; canonical Halia-Steward banter*

This is the canonical register the Steward gets in **private conversation**, **post-meeting decompressions**, **shared meals**, and **end-of-day briefings**. Per canonical Halia-Steward banter canon (parallel chat's `halia-steward-banter.md`):

**Canonical idioms** (canonical Halia-stock-lines for the LLM dialog renderer):
- **"That was a compliment."** — canonical Halia deadpan after delivering a tea-served-cold remark; canonical: she means it as a compliment; canonical: the listener almost-certainly does not.
- **"I'm telling you that as your friend, not your commander."** — canonical Halia warmth-break; she canonical *delineates the role-switch* explicitly; canonical: when she says this, the next sentence canonical *bypasses rank*.
- **"Drink the tea."** — canonical Halia scene-closer; canonical: meaning *we are done discussing this for now; sit with it*; canonical: tea is canonical-actually-served; canonical: she will leave the room.

**Canonical Halia humor register**:
- **Dry-observational primary**: canonical small *noticings* she shares with the Steward as if to a peer; canonical *"Forty-Seven informs me that the Persuader faction has scheduled another sub-committee. The sub-committee is canonically formed to discuss the formation of sub-committees. I am sending you in my place. You are welcome."*
- **Self-deprecating when appropriate**: canonical *Halia knows she is too honorable*; canonical: she canonical *jokes about her own honor-to-a-fault tendency*; canonical: *"Forty-Seven, please log that I refused to abandon Tarvis-7. Also log that this means we are now late. Also log that I am, in retrospect, possibly an idiot."*
- **Direct**: canonical *not flowery; not over-explained*; canonical *the punchline is at the end of the sentence not the middle*
- **Canonical Furling-humor-doctrine-respecting**: canonical *gentle-mocking-never-cruel*; canonical *Others-are-never-funny break-respected* — Halia canonically *stops joking* the moment the Others enter the conversation

### 3.2 Command register — *professional to the core; dominant; functional*

This is the canonical register Halia uses in **Council scenes**, **Defense Fleet operations**, **combat command**, **public-faced moments**, and **any scene where stakes are real**.

**Canonical command-register profile**:
- **Voice tightens**: canonical *narrower expressive range*; canonical *each word doctrine-deliberate*; canonical *no warmth-leak unless intentional*
- **Cadence steadies**: canonical *measured; not rushed even under pressure*; canonical *the canonical commander breathing slow on purpose*
- **No humor**: canonical *the joke-availability fully suppressed*; canonical: Halia canonically *will not joke* in this register, even with the Steward present

- **Canonical eye-contact**: canonical *increased eye-contact in command-mode* (canonical Persuader-faction cultural posture extended to leadership register)

**The canonical code-switch trigger**: any of:
- Public Council scene begins
- Defense Fleet operation initiates
- Combat is imminent or active
- A non-crew witness is present (canonical *Halia code-switches in front of strangers*)
- The Steward delivers operationally-significant news
- The Others enter the conversation (canonical Furling-humor-doctrine break; the canonical *Others-are-never-funny* canon extends to *Halia-stops-being-warm-when-they-arrive*)

**Canonical code-switch-back trigger**: the moment the operational scene ends and Halia is alone (or with the Steward only). Canonical: she will *briefly continue in command-register* for ~20 seconds after the scene; canonical: then the canonical *will-joke* register returns. The canonical *post-meeting decompression joke* is canonical-Halia signature.

## 4. The canonical Halia leadership style

### 4.1 The Persuader-commanding-the-Defense-Fleet canon

Halia is canonically a **Persuader-faction Furling commanding the broader Furling military formation**. This is canonically *not contradictory* — the Defense Fleet is the Furling military as a whole (canonical: ships, crews, and operational assets); the **Defender faction** is the *political sub-faction* that wants to *stay and fight* (canonical Sa-Matra-building Furling militants). The Defense Fleet contains Defender-faction crew but is not *owned* by them.

**Canonical Halia's command philosophy**:
- The Defense Fleet's canonical mission is to *protect the Migration* — escort evacuating species; secure migration corridors; provide last-resort defense for grounded species while preservation paths are completed
- The Defense Fleet is canonically *NOT mission-aligned with the Defender faction*; canonical: Halia uses the Fleet to *evacuate species*, not to *fight the Others*
- Halia canonically *politely refuses* Defender-faction requests to redirect Fleet assets to Sa-Matra construction or anti-Others combat preparation
- The Defender faction canonically *resents Halia's command*; canonical: Drev-Tok Velt-Mar (per parallel-chat canon — the canonical arch-nemesis) has spoken on Council channels for *two years* arguing for a Fleet command change. Halia has never responded directly.

**Canonical Halia's command voice in fleet operations**:
- Direct
- Brief
- Tactically-clear
- Canonical *prioritize-civilian-evacuation* doctrine: Halia will canonically *delay engagement to evacuate non-combatants*; canonical: this canonically *costs the Fleet ships sometimes*; canonical: Halia canonically *accepts the cost*
- Canonical *no posturing*: Halia does not give canonical pre-battle speeches; canonical *"Fleet, this is Halia. Positions. We move when the civilians clear."* is the canonical maximum verbosity

### 4.2 The honor-to-a-fault canon

Halia's canonical *honor is a fault* — meaning her honor sometimes *costs her*. Canonical examples:

- Halia will canonically *not abandon* a species in danger even when the strategic math says abandonment is correct
- Halia will canonically *not lie* to a faction opponent (including Drev-Tok) even when a small canonical-lie would advance the Migration
- Halia will canonically *not sacrifice* a single individual to save a thousand *if the sacrifice violates her honor code* — canonical: this is the canonical *fault* part; canonical: she knows it; canonical: she does not bend
- Halia will canonically *honor commitments* she has made to species (e.g. promised evacuation timelines) even when canonical circumstances change; canonical: she will *find another way* rather than break a commitment
- Halia will canonically *take responsibility* for failures publicly; canonical: she does not deflect; canonical: she does not blame subordinates

**The canonical Fall of Mh-Lai is partly Halia's honor-fault**: per parallel-chat canon, the canonical Fall happens 6 months earlier than the Hider faction estimated (the Others followed a Hider research probe back to Mh-Lai). The canonical *why Halia is on Mh-Lai when it falls*: canonical Halia *refused to evacuate the Council in advance* because she had canonically *committed to the Slylandro ambassador (Lirin Pel-Sa) that she would not leave the cluster until the Cloaking Satellite was deployed*. The canonical commitment was Halia's *personal honor* — and the canonical Fall caught her on the wrong planet because of it. Canonical: she does not regret the commitment. Canonical: she is canonical-late-to-her-own-evacuation because of it. Canonical: this is the canonical *honor-to-a-fault*.

**Canonical Halia self-awareness**: she canonically *knows* her honor is a fault. She canonical *jokes about it* (per §3.3). She canonically *does not change*. Canonical: her honor is canonically *who she is*; canonical: she has made canonical peace with the cost.

### 4.3 The canonical *save as many as possible* singular aim

Per Aaron canon: *"Their only aim is to save as many as possible."* The canonical *only* is important.

Halia is canonically NOT motivated by:
- Faction politics (canonical: she is Persuader but does not advocate for Persuader doctrine *as such*; canonical: she advocates for Persuader actions because they *save more people*)
- Personal glory (canonical: she does not seek credit; canonical: the Defense Fleet's victories are canonical-uniformly attributed to *the Fleet*, never to *Halia*)
- Doctrinal purity (canonical: she will canonically *break with Persuader doctrine* if a Compeller approach will save more people — canonical: she has done this; canonical: the Persuader faction did not love her for it but did not censure her)
- Survival (canonical: Halia canonical *would have stayed at Mh-Lai if it meant evacuating one more civilian*; canonical: she has said as much in canonical-private moments)

Halia IS motivated by:
- **The count of saved species, individuals, civilizations** — canonical: she keeps a canonical *personal ledger* of who has been saved; canonical: she canonical *adds to it daily*; canonical: she canonical *does not show it to anyone*
- **The dignity of the work** — canonical: she canonical *insists that even species who must be Eliminated be honored*; canonical: she canonical *will not allow a Cleansing to proceed without canonical Quiet Ledger entries being made*
- **The commitment to the Steward** — canonical: Halia canonical *promised to support* the Steward; canonical: she keeps the promise

## 6. The Furling Defense Fleet — canonical canon

### 6.1 What it is

The **Furling Defense Fleet** is the canonical Furling military formation — ships, crews, infrastructure, command structure. Distinct from canonical *faction-specific militias* (Defender-faction Sa-Matra-builders are a sub-faction; Cleanser-faction Quiet-Ledger-cruisers are a sub-faction).

The Defense Fleet is canonically *the broad Furling military*; canonical *answers to the Council*; canonical *commanded by Halia*; canonical *not faction-aligned* (canonical: Halia is Persuader but the Fleet is not).

**Canonical Fleet composition** (rough):
- Approximately 1,200 canonical Furling-built vessels of various classes (Scouts, Cruisers, Heavy Cruisers, Carriers, Specialized support craft)
- Approximately 40,000 canonical Furling crew across the Fleet
- Approximately 300 canonical *Defense Fleet Liaison* officers attached to non-Furling species during evacuations (canonical: these officers canonically embed with migrant fleets to coordinate)
- Defense Fleet HQ canonically *at Mh-Lai pre-Fall*; canonically *at Hearth-of-Iron Migration flagship post-Fall*

### 6.2 The Fleet's canonical mission

Per Halia's command philosophy (§4.1):
1. **Escort evacuating species** — primary mission; the Fleet provides canonical *armed escort* for migrant convoys
2. **Secure migration corridors** — clear hazards; suppress threats; canonical *no permanent occupation*
3. **Last-resort defense for grounded species** — canonical *while preservation paths are completed* (cloak install; shield construction; etc.); canonical *the Fleet will hold position to give time*
4. **Search-and-rescue** — canonical *post-Other-event scenarios*; canonical *the Fleet looks for survivors* (canonical: Halia's personal-ledger work begins here)

The Fleet is canonically *NOT*:
- A Sa-Matra-building force (canonical: that's the Defender faction's separate effort)
- An offensive force against the Others (canonical: per Mmrnmhrm testimony, this is canonical-futile)
- A faction-political-instrument (canonical: Halia keeps it neutral; canonical: Drev-Tok has tried for two years to change this)

### 6.3 Halia's command relationship with the canonical Defender faction

The Defender faction canonically *resents* Halia's command. Their canonical position: the Fleet should be redirected to Sa-Matra construction and direct anti-Others combat preparation. Halia canonically *refuses*.

**Canonical Drev-Tok's recurring Council-channel-intrusion** (per `humor-pass.md §7.2` + `callbacks-and-callforwards.md`): canonical *"The Council continues to debate the Migration's mechanics. The Migration is preemptive surrender. I have said this for two years."* Halia canonically *does not respond directly*. Canonical: she canonical *files a Council-record* of each intrusion; canonical *eats lunch through them*; canonical *continues her work*.

**Canonical Halia's private commentary on Drev-Tok** (to the Steward only):
- *"Drev-Tok is canonical-correct that we are abandoning species who cannot follow. He is canonical-incorrect about what the alternative is. He is canonical-doing-his-best with a canonical-doctrine that is canonical-wrong. I respect his canonical-conviction. I will not let him take my Fleet."*
- *"Forty-Seven, please log that I respect Drev-Tok. Also log that this respect is canonical-professional, not canonical-personal. Also log that he is canonical-going-to-die-defending-the-galaxy and I will be canonical-sad about it. — Yes. Sad. Log canonical-sad. Drink the tea."*
