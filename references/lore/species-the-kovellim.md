# The Kovellim — *The Crossing-Veterans* (multi-cycle nomadic Migrators; Precursor-faction elders)

> Authored 2026-05-17 (Skippy creative pass — Precursor-side fill toward
> Aaron's 8+8 species target). Aaron's brief: "go exotic." Exotic vector:
> **the Kovellim have migrated between galaxies SEVEN times. They are
> the only slice species who has *done this before*. Each crossing leaves
> a literal physical scar on the body. Their elders are visibly
> archaeological.**

## The Concept

Most slice species are facing the Others for the first time. The
Kovellim are facing them for the **eighth**. They are an ancient
nomadic species who have migrated, as a population, *seven prior
times* across the deep-past cosmic cycles — each time a different
galaxy, each time leaving as the Others arrived, each time
arriving in the next destination and rebuilding.

They are the slice's living embodiment of the cycle. The Chenjesu
*remember* a prior Culling (rooted, slow, factual). The Selvenne
*replay* prior Cullings (qualia, subjective). The Kovellim
**personally survived** the prior Cullings, on the ships they
flew themselves, with bodies still scarred from the crossings.
**They are not telling stories. They are showing dimples.**

Each dimensional crossing leaves a literal **knot-scar** on the
Kovellim body — a coiled raised mark where the body's substrate
was briefly folded through hyperspace-adjacent dimensional space.
Young Kovellim have one scar (the migration that brought them here).
Middle-aged Kovellim have three or four (born here, helped lead the
intervening crossings as adults). Elders have seven. The eldest
Kovellim in the slice carries *eight* — she will lead the
Furling Migration as one of the Council's dimensional-crossing
advisors. She has done this longer than most species have
existed.

## Body Plan

- **Form**: tall bipedal humanoid, ~3-4m, long-limbed, slender.
  Knot-skin: the entire body's epidermis is a patchwork of healed
  scar-tissue interwoven with smooth original skin. Each knot is a
  raised coil, ~3-8cm across, in a slightly darker tone than the
  base skin. Knots can be counted by visitors — the Kovellim are
  cheerfully matter-of-fact about *"reading their crossing-record."*
- **Lifespan**: ~1,200 Furling years per individual. They are the
  longest-lived species in the slice. An elder Kovellim has been
  alive for the previous *two* cosmic cycles.
- **Skin tone**: weathered ochre-tan as base; the knot-scars are a
  deeper umber. The contrast makes the scar-pattern legible at
  conversational distance.
- **Eyes**: large dark eyes, *deep-set* in the skull (a result of
  bone-remodeling that happens with each crossing; the eye-sockets
  get slightly deeper each time). Elders' eyes are nearly recessed
  caves. The visual effect is of *gravity in the face* — the elder
  has been *pulled* by something.
- **Hands and gait**: long-fingered, careful. Their movements are
  unhurried — they have very deep time-sense and rarely rush. A
  Kovellim walking down a corridor uses approximately 30% fewer
  steps than a Furling because their stride is wider and their
  movement-economy is millennia-honed.
- **Clothing**: traditional Kovellim wear *cycle-cloaks* — woven
  garments with embroidered patterns recording the family's
  migration history (which galaxies they came from, what they
  found there, when they left). The cloak's embroidery is a
  *family genealogy* the wearer carries in cloth-form. Elders'
  cloaks are extraordinarily detailed; they take centuries to
  weave.

## Voice & Cognition

- **Voice**: deep, slow, *cyclical*. The Kovellim think in *cycles*
  — recurring patterns across the deep past. When a Kovellim says
  "this happened before," they mean *literally before*, in a
  prior galaxy, with their own body experiencing it. Their
  conversational rhythm has long phrases punctuated by long
  pauses; each pause is the Kovellim mentally cross-referencing
  the current conversation against the cycle's deep-time record.
- **Pronouns**: first-person singular ("I" — the species is
  individualistic at the cognition level) AND first-person-
  plural-of-cycle ("we, who have crossed" — when referring to
  the species's collective migration history). Switching between
  these is contextual.
- **Tone**: warmly weary, gently *anticipatory*. They know what's
  coming. They have *done* what's coming. They are not afraid
  because they have empirical evidence the strategy works.
- **Sample line** (greeting the Steward):
  > "Steward. You are the fourth Furling to ask this question of
  > me. The last was Iren-Vor in the seventh decade of her
  > tenure; before her, Halve-Kor in the eleventh; before her,
  > so deep back the records are torn. *(A long pause as the
  > elder mentally indexes.)* They asked the same questions you
  > are about to ask. I will answer in the same order, for
  > continuity. Sit."
- **Idiom**: they use the verbal idiom *"in the cycle of"*
  frequently — *"in the cycle of, this is the third asking"* etc.
  Furling translator parses "cycle" as both *iteration of the
  Culling* and *temporal/conversational pattern* — the Kovellim
  word collapses both meanings.

## Faction Alignment

**Precursor (Stay-Go)**, with extreme conviction. The Kovellim
**have empirical evidence the Migration works**. They have
survived seven prior Cullings *by leaving*. They are the slice's
strongest voice in favor of the Migration timetable, because
their entire species's existence is proof-of-concept.

They join the Furling Migration as **Council-tier dimensional-
crossing advisors**. They provide:
- **Crossing maps** — which dimensional foldings work, which
  don't, what to expect, where to brace
- **Engineering protocols** — what materials hold up to crossing
  stress, what failure modes to watch for
- **Personnel rotation schedules** — when to put a fresh crew
  on watch during a long crossing
- **Cultural advice** — what populations do *after* arriving in
  a new galaxy; what predictable crises happen in the first
  hundred years post-migration; how to plan for them

**Terminal: Migrated** (their eighth crossing; they will arrive
in Andromeda, settle, eventually need to migrate again in some
future cycle).

## In the Slice

**Encounter location**: the **Kovellim Conclave** — a deep-space
station orbiting a halo of small asteroids in the slice cluster
(canonically the *Eight-Knot Station*, named for its elder's
eight scars). The station serves as the Kovellim coordination
hub for the upcoming crossing. The Steward visits via standard
docking.

**Encounter shape**: formal Council-protocol greeting; the
Steward is received by **Ovala-Eight-Crossings**, the elder
who will lead the Furling Migration's dimensional navigation.
She sits in a deep cycle-cloak chair; her knot-scars are
visible across her cheekbones, shoulders, hands; her cloak
embroidery is so detailed it's read like a book. She offers
the Steward tea (a tradition; the tea is from a prior galaxy)
and conversation that is dense and slow.

**What they want**: **fresh galaxy-data** — the Kovellim need
detailed sensor readings of the current galaxy's dimensional
structure to plan the crossing. The Steward, who has flown
through the cluster recently, has invaluable telemetry. The
Kovellim trade their crossing-wisdom in exchange.

**What we want**: **Kovellim crossing-modules** — three sensor
and engine upgrades the Steward's Furling Scout can install,
each a memory-fold of seven crossings:
- **Kovellim Folder-Compass**: hyperspace navigation enhancement;
  reveals dimensional-stable corridors invisible to standard
  sensors
- **Crossing-Brace**: hull integrity boost specifically for
  hyperspace stress
- **Cycle-Memory Algorithm**: dialog/decision support — passive
  module that surfaces "this has been tried before" annotations
  when the Steward faces choices the Kovellim have records of

**Quest** `kovellim_crossing_trade` in `tools/quest_inventory.csv`:
branches **standard trade** (galaxy-data → all three modules) /
**partial trade** (selective; the elder is gently disappointed)
/ **listen-to-the-stories** (skip the trade; the elder *tells*
the Steward about prior crossings, granting Bio-Archive entries
"Others — prior cycles" but no modules) / **Cleanser-pressure**
(Council asks whether the Kovellim's long memory of prior
Cullings makes them a *flare-risk*; Ovala-Eight-Crossings
politely shows the Steward her eight knot-scars and asks if
they intend to add a ninth; the request retracts).

## Ship — The Kovellim Crossing-Frigate

A migration-class vessel. ~160 points, hull 200 (extremely
tough — designed for dimensional-stress), shields 40 (medium),
slow (top_speed 200), slow turn (turn_rate 0.5).

- Primary: **Knot-Resonance Beam** — sustained beam that
  *exhausts* enemy shields faster than normal (the beam
  resonates with shield-frequencies the Kovellim discovered
  across multiple cycles). Damage 4/sec. Range 700.
- Special: **Cycle-Fold** — once per fight, the Crossing-Frigate
  briefly *folds* itself through hyperspace-adjacent dimensional
  space, reappearing at any point within 800 units of its
  current position. Costs 60 energy. 15-second cooldown. Visual:
  the ship briefly knots in on itself, then untangles at the
  destination — a *visible scar* appears on the ship after each
  use (the ship's hull gains a faint coiled mark; cosmetic).
- AI style: defensive-veteran; the Kovellim know how to outlast
  any single fight; will not pursue beyond cluster-edge.

## Authoring Notes

- **Don't make them mournful or tragic**. The Kovellim's
  emotional register is *warm anticipation* — they know what's
  coming and they know how to handle it. They are the slice's
  steadiest voice. Stewards interacting with them should feel
  *reassured* (the strategy works) and *humbled* (it has happened
  many times before).
- **Their humor is dryly cyclical**: *"In the cycle of, this
  conversation has happened. Three Furlings ago we discussed
  whether the Council had finally learned. We have not had to
  revise the answer."*
- **Cross-canon with Chenjesu and Selvenne**: the three species
  together form the slice's *prior-cycle* knowledge triumvirate.
  Chenjesu remember as rooted witnesses; Selvenne replay as
  participants; Kovellim *personally crossed*. Stewards who
  consult all three get a 360° view of what survival has looked
  like across cycles. The Hider faction treasures the combined
  archive.
- **Naming pattern**: Kovellim use **role-suffix names** —
  *Ovala-Eight-Crossings* (elder, eight scars), *Vesh-Three-
  Crossings* (middle-aged, three scars), *Lar-First-Knot* (young
  adult, one scar). Children have no scars and bear *"yet-
  unwritten"* as their second-name (e.g., *Tev-Yet-Unwritten*)
  until their first crossing.
- **The eighth crossing is the slice's**. Ovala will live to see
  it. Her ninth knot-scar will form during the Migration into
  Andromeda. The slice's epilogue notes: *"and Ovala-Eight-
  Crossings became Ovala-Nine-Crossings, and her cloak gained
  its ninth thread."*
- **Cleanser doctrine cannot touch them.** Same as Stelloth —
  they're Migrating-Precursor and Cleanser euthanasia targets
  Stay-side flare-risks only.
