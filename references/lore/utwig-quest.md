## 2. The Veils Falling doctrine — full lore

### 2.1 What happened

The Utwig ancestral form (per [proto-species-observations.md](proto-species-observations.md)) was *armored bipedal grazers with extremely expressive facial musculature and a hyper-developed shame response. When threatened or rebuked by their herd-mates, they bury their faces.* The face-hiding *was already the trauma response* in the proto-stage.

Approximately **120 years before the slice's present** (a single Utwig generation; their lifespan averages 80 years), the Utwig crossed the sentience threshold. They became thinking beings. They invented language, mathematics, art, philosophy, architecture, and within 60 years of crossing, the **Ultron** — a self-built restoration device whose original purpose was to *enhance and stabilize their newly-emerged cognition*. The Utwig's first-sentience era was canonically **brilliant and brief**.

Then they detected the dimensional ripples. The Furling Council reached them. The Utwig learned about the Others. They learned that sentience above the threshold is detectable. They learned that the Migration was real and that most Furling-allied species would leave the galaxy. They learned that the Furlings could not promise the Utwig safe passage on the Migration's timeline — the Utwig had only been sentient for ~80 years; they were unfamiliar; their integration would be slow.

**The Utwig considered this for fourteen years and chose to un-think.**

The decision was canonically *unanimous*, which is the most disturbing part of the canon. The species reached consensus that *sentience was an error*. The argument that won: *"We did not need to think to be content. We have learned to think and we have learned what comes next. We will return to before we knew."* The doctrine that grew from this consensus is the **Veils Falling**.

### 2.2 The doctrine's mechanics

The Veils Falling is a multi-stage cultural-and-pharmacological devolution program. The stages:

- **First Veil** (entry; year 1 of doctrine): the practitioner adopts a mask; the mask covers the canonical Utwig facial musculature that produces emotional expression; the practitioner *practices not thinking in abstractions* (canonical first restriction: no future-tense verbs)
- **Second Veil** (year 3-5): the practitioner adds restriction layers; *no negative emotional self-reflection*; *no questioning of doctrine* (the doctrine becomes immune to its own scrutiny); ceremonial shaming for slips
- **Third Veil** (year 7-10): *no metacognition*; the practitioner is now functionally unable to think about thinking; canonical first sign — the practitioner cannot articulate their own state; they say "I am here" but cannot say "I am wondering whether I am still here"
- **Fourth Veil** (year 12-15): *no abstract reasoning*; the practitioner now lives in a permanent present-tense; mask becomes more elaborate
- **Fifth Veil** (year 17-20): *no language above a 200-word vocabulary*; the practitioner has formally surrendered most language; lives by ritual; canonical line: they can still say *"please"* and *"thank you"* and *"yes"*; very little else
- **Sixth Veil** (year 22-25): *no language at all*; communication via mask-gestures and ritual touch; the practitioner is now operating at the cognitive level of the pre-sentient Utwig grazer ancestors; they walk in groups; they eat ceremonially; they sleep when the sky darkens; **they are below the Others' threshold**

The Veils Falling takes approximately 25 years per practitioner. The slice's present-era Utwig are spread across all six stages — some at First Veil, some at Sixth, most at Third or Fourth. The doctrine is **fourteen years old** in the slice's era; the first Sixth-Veil practitioners are *just appearing*. They are the canonical proof the doctrine works.

### 2.3 The catch — recovery is impossible

By Fourth Veil, the practitioner cannot reverse course on their own — they cannot think the necessary thoughts to abandon the doctrine. By Sixth Veil, they cannot be reasoned with at all in any abstract sense. **The Ultron is the only known restoration device** that could theoretically reverse devolution — it was built for exactly this kind of stabilization — but **using the Ultron on a Veil-Falling practitioner is the deepest doctrinal Veil-betrayal**. The Utwig keep the Ultron sealed in a chamber. They have agreed not to look at it. Some of them look at it. They are working on this.

### 2.4 The theological factions

The Utwig theologians have divided into argumentative sub-schools, each with a fully-articulated position. The argument **between the schools** continues only in the upper Veil stages (the lower-Veil practitioners can no longer follow the argument). The High Veil — chief theologian — translates between schools for the practitioners who can no longer reach each other:

- **The Wise Ones** — argue for un-thinking *only the abstract* (math, philosophy, future-tense). They believe the species can preserve practical cognition (tool use, simple language, art) while dropping below threshold. Doctrinally moderate. **They believe the Sixth Veil is too far.**
- **The True-Devolvers** — argue for un-thinking *the present* (no metacognition, no language above ritual). The canonical mainstream position. The Sixth Veil is the goal.
- **The Far-Devolvers** — argue for un-thinking *even the act of un-thinking*, which they admit creates a paradox. The **Paradox Council** convenes monthly to discuss whether thinking about the paradox is itself a violation of the doctrine they ratified last month. They have not resolved this. They are aware they have not resolved this. They argue this is the work.
- **The Original Doctrine school** — a small minority. Argue that the doctrine should be *reversed* — that the original sentience was a gift and the Veils Falling was a mistake. They hold this view *while still wearing masks*, because the cultural pressure to wear a mask is total. They are the slice's quietly tragic faction — they have the right idea and they cannot act on it. The Steward can find them; they will whisper.

### 2.5 The Ultron — pre-doctrine artifact

The Ultron was built in **year 58 of Utwig sentience** (62 years before the present slice era). It was the species' first-sentience masterwork — a cognitive-stabilization device designed to enhance and harmonize the new abstract-reasoning substrate. The engineering is extraordinary; the device is still operational; it is sealed in a chamber at the heart of the Utwig theological complex. The chamber is guarded by mask-wearing Veil-tenders who have all agreed not to enter.

Canonical Utwig position on the Ultron: *"It is the work of who we were before. We do not look at it. It cannot help us now. We agreed."* This statement is repeated, in nearly identical form, by every Utwig theologian the Steward encounters. The repetition is itself doctrinal — the Utwig have agreed on the canonical phrasing.

**The catch**: the Ultron's sealing-chamber requires *intentional regular maintenance*. The maintenance has been performed continuously since the doctrine took hold. The maintenance schedule has not been broken. The maintainers wear specialized masks (the *Veil of the Maintaining*, a sub-doctrine that exists *solely so the chamber's upkeep can continue without theologically counting as engagement with the Ultron*). This is the canonical absurdity at the heart of Utwig theology and they know it. They will not say so. The High Veil might, in a private moment with the Steward.

## 3. NPC roster

### 3.1 The High Veil — *Mal-Dren Veil-Of-Knowing*

Chief Utwig theologian. Mask-wearer of the most elaborate variant. Canonically the *most devolved* Utwig who is still capable of articulating doctrine — he has been at Fifth Veil for three years and has not yet crossed to Sixth, against all theological prediction. He keeps slipping back into Fourth-Veil thinking, which canonically should not be possible. He is *the species' canonical theologian-trapped-in-his-own-sentience*. He cannot complete the devolution. He will not say so directly. He suspects this makes him a failure; the species considers him their wisest member; both can be true.

**Voice**:
- Slow, deliberate, formal. Speaks Furling fluently (a slip from his pre-doctrine education; he should have lost this by now and has not).
- Plant-and-mask metaphors: *"The Veil has not yet fallen on this brother."* *"We do not see one another's faces and we are grateful for the not-seeing."* *"The mask is a courtesy."*
- Frequently quotes the doctrine verbatim, then pauses, then *almost* questions it, then completes the quote correctly. The pause is canonical.
- Self-aware about his predicament in private; never in public.

**Sample lines**:
- *(public, formal)* — *"Steward. We greet you. The Veils Falling is the work of our species. We do not look at the Ultron. We have agreed."*
- *(private, to the Steward, after some trust)* — *"You will forgive me. I am supposed to be at the Sixth Veil by now. I keep — thinking. I do not know how to stop. The brothers think I am the wisest among them. I do not say what I am. The mask is a courtesy I extend mostly to myself."*
- *(theological argument)* — *"The Wise Ones say the abstract may be released and the practical preserved. The True-Devolvers say all of it must go. The Far-Devolvers say even the going must go, which creates a paradox we have agreed to discuss monthly. I have personally attended every monthly discussion of the paradox for three years. I cannot stop attending. This is canonically a violation of doctrine. None of us mention it."*
- *(closing, public)* — *"We will speak again, Steward. Or we will not. The Veil will fall on me eventually. I will not be able to speak with you when it does. I will be grateful, then. I think. I am working on it."*

### 3.7 Sevra — *The Utwig Atheist* (potential crewmate)

Sevra is the Utwig who refused to grow into a doctrine-keeper, refused the Original Doctrine school as *just another doctrine*, refused the mask as cultural pressure rather than personal need. ### 3.8 The Form-Filers, Form-Readers, and Form-Burners

The Utwig's theological bureaucracy. Canonical religious-comedy supporting cast.

- **Form-Filers**: wear *narrow tall masks* with a single slit for the eyes. They receive forms from petitioners; they ensure the forms are filled out correctly. They cannot read; they verify the form's *shape* matches the doctrine-specified shape. (They have been at Fifth Veil; they have lost reading; they retain shape-recognition. This is canonically how the bureaucracy was designed.)
- **Form-Readers**: wear *wide flat masks* with two slits. They read the forms aloud to the Form-Filers, who then ceremonially confirm receipt. They retain reading; they are at Third or Fourth Veil. They are the bureaucracy's working class.
- **Form-Burners**: wear *masks shaped like inverted bowls* with no visible slits. They are the highest position. They ceremonially burn the filed forms after one year. They have *agreed not to read the forms they burn*. They sometimes peek. They are working on this.

The form-handling process for any theological petition (including the Steward's request to view or recover the Ultron):
1. Petitioner approaches a Form-Reader. States the petition. Form-Reader pretends to write it down using ceremonial brush-and-ink that mostly produces marks the Form-Filer cannot read.
2. Form-Reader hands the form to a Form-Filer, who confirms the shape is doctrine-compliant.
3. Form-Filer files the form in a ceremonial cabinet for one year.
4. After one year, a Form-Burner retrieves the form, *does not read it*, and burns it ceremonially.
5. The petition is considered resolved.

The Steward who attempts to navigate this bureaucracy gets the canonical Utwig-comedy moment. The whole process can be expedited (the High Veil has the authority) but the canonical first-encounter is the *full process*. The Steward then asks how the process is normally expedited and is told: *"It is not normally expedited. Petitions of this kind have not previously been filed. You are the first. The forms had to be designed for you."*

### 5.2 Religious-bureaucratic absurdities

- The species has produced **31 distinct mask designs** in fourteen years. Each design has theological justification. The justification documents are themselves under doctrinal review and may be discontinued.
- The **Veiled Assembly** meets weekly. Meetings begin with a Veil-Counting ceremony where each participant declares their current Veil-stage; the High Veil verifies; meetings cannot proceed until the count is consistent. The count is often inconsistent (members slip backward in stages without realizing).
- The **Paradox Council** has been working for three years on resolving whether the act of un-thinking constitutes thinking. They have produced **seventy-three position papers**. The papers contradict each other. The position-paper writers have all *moved on* to other Veil-stages and can no longer defend their positions. The papers remain in the canonical record.
- The species' **Falling-Book** (canonical doctrinal text) is being slowly un-written. The current edition is 73 pages. The previous edition was 414 pages. The edition before that was 2,800 pages. The Eventual Form is canonically a Falling-Book that is *one syllable*. The theologians have not agreed on which syllable.
- The Form-Burners' ceremonial mask is a bowl-shape with no visible slits. The Form-Burners have all canonically agreed not to peek. They sometimes peek. The peek-checking ceremony (where the Form-Burners are reviewed for any signs of peeking) is performed by *other* Form-Burners, also wearing bowl masks. They also peek. The whole system runs on tacit agreement that no one mention it.
- The **Veil of the Maintaining** (the sub-doctrine permitting Ultron-chamber upkeep) has its own associated mask, which is canonically *identical* to the standard ceremonial mask but worn *backward*. The wearer of the backward mask is canonically considered not-engaging-with-the-Ultron-while-engaging-with-the-Ultron's-chamber. The species considers this a coherent doctrinal position.

### 5.3 The Utwig calendar

- The Utwig year is divided into thirteen Veil-Falling months. Each month has a different mask.
- **Month One** (the New Year): everyone wears their Most-Aspirational mask. Aspirational masks depict the wearer's intended next-Veil-stage. Wearing an Aspirational mask above one's actual Veil-stage is canonically not lying because the doctrine considers aspiration *the truth of the future-tense one is renouncing*. (This argument is universally rejected by First-Veil practitioners and universally accepted by Fourth-Veil practitioners; the difference is the doctrine working as intended.)
- **Month Six** (the Mid-Year Reckoning): everyone wears their *Honest* mask. Honest masks depict the wearer's actual current Veil-stage. This is the most common cause of theological argument all year — practitioners discover others (and themselves) have miscounted, and the reckoning lasts most of the month.
- **Month Thirteen** (the Year's End): everyone wears their Forgetting mask. Forgetting masks have no expression. The masks are identical for every Utwig. The day is observed in silence.

### 5.4 Religious-comedy scene beats

The following beats can be scattered through the quest as ambient color:

- **Two Utwig argue at a Form-Filer's desk** about whether their petition's parchment-shape is doctrine-compliant. The argument is *fiercely polite*. The Form-Filer eventually rules the shape compliant; the loser of the argument is upset; the winner is upset too because winning theological arguments is canonically prideful and prideful is canonically a Veil-violation. They both leave the chamber and resolve to do better.
- **A Sixth-Veil Utwig walks slowly past the Steward.** The Sixth-Veil practitioner does not look at the Steward. They are heading toward the lower groves. They make no sound. ## 10. The Ceremonies — *Trying To Leave Is The Comedy*

> **Canon update (2026-05-17, Aaron dispatch):** *"And the Utwig traditions and ceremonies are RIDICULOUS! It takes FOREVER to get out of there without participating in them all and that is the joke about them. You are trying to get out of there, but they want to wash your nose with their ointment and it causes a rash, and you try to leave but they have not yet blessed your landing gear... it goes on and on, but if you break protocol, they may get mad."*
>
> **The canonical Utwig running gag**: a visit to the Utwig settlement is a comedy of escape. The Steward arrives to ask a question, conduct business, or recover the Ultron; the Utwig welcome the Steward sincerely; the welcoming **never ends**. There is always another ceremony. The Steward who tries to leave discovers that *the landing-gear has not yet been blessed*, that *the seven-time mask-decline has only happened five times*, that *the doctrine-keeper assigned to seal the visit is currently re-counting their Veil and cannot be interrupted*. The ceremonies are sincere on the Utwig side and *absurd by their accumulated weight* on the Steward's side. **The Steward who participates in everything spends a doctrinally-correct quantity of in-game time at the settlement; the Steward who breaks protocol to escape faster makes the Utwig sad, then angry, then doctrinally-concerned, then Cleanser-vote-confidence-rising.** *"It goes on and on"* is canonical.

### 10.1 The Ceremonial Budget — mechanical canon

Each Utwig visit awards a hidden **Ceremonial Budget** score. Every ceremony the Steward encounters offers a participation choice:

- **Participate** (canonical respectful path): +1 Ceremonial Budget; ceremony plays out in a brief humor montage; canonical small consequence in some cases (the nose-rash, the oil-stickiness, etc.)
- **Decline politely** (canonical compromise): 0 Ceremonial Budget; -1 Utwig standing; canonical Utwig response is gracious-but-disappointed (*"You have refused the Veil-of-Welcoming. The doctrine permits refusal but the doctrine does not pretend it was respectful."*)
- **Decline rudely** (canonical break-protocol path): -1 Ceremonial Budget; -2 Utwig standing; canonical Utwig response escalates from sad → angry → doctrinally-alarmed

At quest-end, the **Ceremonial Budget** modifies the quest outcome and the Utwig Council-vote-confidence:

| Budget tier | Tier name | Standing effect | Council-vote impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| **6+** | *Doctrine-Respected* | Utwig standing ++; Persuader/Preserver +; Cleanser - | Cleanser-vote-confidence weakened; canonical archive line: *"The Steward honored us with their time. We will trust their judgment."* |
| **3-5** | *Doctrine-Acknowledged* | Utwig standing +; baseline | Baseline; canonical archive line: *"The Steward participated as the doctrine permits. We will consider their judgment."* |
| **0-2** | *Doctrine-Skirted* | Utwig standing -; Persuader/Preserver - | Mild Cleanser-vote-confidence rise; canonical archive line: *"You did not refuse us; you did not honor us either; we will remember."* |
| **Negative** | *Doctrine-Violated* | Utwig standing --; Cleanser ++ | **Cleanser argues canonical line**: *"The Steward did not respect them; we cannot trust their judgment in our deliberations about this species. We recommend Cleansing more strongly."* The Steward's recommendation for the Utwig is **discounted** in the final Council vote — the Cleanser path becomes the canonical baseline outcome unless the Steward provides strong counter-evidence. |

**First visit canonical ceremony count**: 8 ceremonies presented (out of a canonical 14+ in the full repertoire — Lore picks 8 per visit; rotate the selection on return visits). **Return visit canonical**: 4-6 *additional* ceremonies *the Utwig have invented for this particular Steward* — the canonical escalation is itself comedy.

### 10.2 The canonical ceremony repertoire

The full Utwig ceremony repertoire spans the visit lifecycle from pre-arrival to post-departure. Each ceremony is sincere on the Utwig side. The Steward experiences them as a sequence of small absurdities.

#### Pre-arrival (in-ship)
1. **The Veil-Hailing** — a ceremonial radio greeting that takes 3-5 minutes. The Utwig at the spaceport recite the canonical visitor-greeting *in full Veil-Counting cadence*. The Steward must respond with the canonical *"I arrive in the time-presence of the Veiled Assembly"* phrase; saying any other phrase triggers a polite re-do. **Forty-Seven** has the canonical phrase logged from prior Furling visits and can provide it via ship-AI assist.
2. **The Landing-Vector Permission** — the Veiled Assembly formally blesses each cardinal direction the ship approaches from. The blessing is recited from the spaceport on open channel. The Steward must NOT engage thrust during the blessing (canonical *thrust-during-blessing* is a Veil-betrayal). The blessing takes 4-7 minutes depending on which cardinal direction the Steward approaches from (north is canonically the longest because the canonical *Veil of the Northward* requires additional sub-phrases).
3. **The Landing-Gear Blessing** — at touchdown, a small ceremony anoints the landing gear. The Veil-tender approaches the gear with a small bowl of canonical landing-oil; oils each leg; recites the *Lift-and-Settle benediction*; takes 8-12 minutes. Canonical Aaron-example ceremony.

#### Greeting (at gangplank)
4. **The Nose-Ointment Ritual** — a Veil-tender approaches the Steward at the gangplank with a small clay jar of green-brown unguent that smells strongly of fermented root-vegetables (canonical *the Veil-Ointment of Welcoming*). The Steward's nose is anointed with the unguent. **Canonical Aaron-specified consequence: causes a rash.** The Steward's nose visibly itches for the next 3 in-game days. **Common Room crew canonical responses**:
   - **Mraka**: *"Your nose is glowing. Did you stand too close to a star?"*
   - **Forty-Seven**: *"Steward, I am noting an unusual histamine response in your nasal tissue. The Utwig anointing-substance contains a compound your biology objects to. I will record this for the next Steward who visits them."*
   - **Vresh** (hum-pattern observable from the tank): *"We-all sense your face-fire. We-all are sorry the welcoming hurt."*
   - **Tarven**: *"They have done this to every Steward we have records of. The rash is canonical."*

5. **The Footstep-Counting** — when the Steward walks down the gangplank, a Veil-tender counts the footsteps aloud and announces the canonical *footstep-count blessing* matching the number. If the Steward walks too fast and exceeds the canonical 9-step count, the Veil-tender canonically *asks the Steward to walk back up the gangplank and try again*. If the Steward walks too slowly and undercount to 7 or fewer, the Veil-tender canonically *waits patiently for the additional steps*; the Steward must canonically *take exactly 9 steps* down the gangplank.

6. **The Mask-Loaning** — a Veil-tender offers a small thin "visitor's mask" to the Steward. The Steward must canonically decline politely. The Veil-tender offers again. The Steward must decline again. **This MUST happen exactly seven times** before the offering is retracted; the doctrine specifies the seven-fold offering. Each offer-and-decline takes ~30 seconds. The Steward who accepts the mask on offer one canonically *breaks the seven-fold ritual* and the Veil-tender must canonically *retract the offer, reset, and begin again from zero*.

#### Welcoming (in the chamber)
7. **The Stand-of-Greeting** — every doctrine-keeper above Third Veil stands up when the Steward enters the chamber. They then sit down. The Steward stands. They sit. They stand again to acknowledge the Steward's standing. They sit. The Steward sits. **This takes 12 minutes because the doctrine-keepers have canonically agreed not to coordinate** — each stands and sits at their own pace; the canonical *non-coordination* is doctrinal; the cumulative timing is the canonical comedy.

8. **The Bowl-of-Welcoming** — the Steward is offered a small bowl of doctrinally-blessed water. **Refusing is rude. Drinking is also rude.** The Steward must canonically *touch the bowl to their forehead* without drinking. The gesture is specific: the bowl-rim must touch the canonical *upper-fur-mound center-point* (per new Furling shaggy-fur-mound anatomy); the touch must last canonically 3 seconds; the bowl must be returned to the Veil-tender with the canonical *gentle two-hand gesture*. **Getting it wrong** = the Veil-tender patiently demonstrates the correct gesture; the Steward retries; canonical *retry-loop* with up to 3 retries before the doctrine canonically permits the Steward to *skip and apologize*.

9. **The Hourly Veil-Counting interrupt** — every hour on the hour during the visit, the conversation pauses for a Veil-Counting. The Veiled Assembly counts their Veil-stages; the High Veil verifies; *the count is often inconsistent*. The conversation cannot resume until the count is consistent. Canonical 4-6 minute interrupt per hour. **The Steward who arrives at 11:55 of an hour will sit through a Veil-Counting at 12:00 before their actual conversation can begin.**

#### In-conversation interruptions
10. **The Mid-Sentence Adjustment Ceremony** — whenever a doctrine-keeper realizes they have slipped a Veil-stage (canonical: this happens approximately 1.3 times per conversation), they must immediately retreat to a corner of the room and quietly adjust their mask. Conversations pause. The Steward waits. The doctrine-keeper returns. The conversation resumes from where it left off, with the doctrine-keeper canonically *picking up mid-sentence*. **Mal-Dren slips approximately every 8 minutes** (canonical: he is *failing to devolve* and the slip-rate is the doctrine working against him).

11. **The Form-Filing Insertion** — at canonical points during the conversation, a Form-Reader appears with a small ceremonial parchment and *politely requests* that the Steward sign (with a ceremonial brush). The signing is the canonical *Veil-of-Witnessing* — the Steward's mark-on-parchment certifies they have witnessed a specific doctrine. The mark must be canonically *unrecognizable as writing* (the Form-Filers will canonically *reject* a recognizable signature; the Steward must learn to scribble in canonical Utwig non-script).

#### Pre-farewell (as the Steward attempts to leave)
12. **The Statement-of-Intent** — the Steward must formally state their intention to depart, using the canonical phrasing *"I have arrived in the time-presence; I now depart in the time-presence; I thank the Veiled Assembly for the time-presence we have shared."* If the phrasing is wrong (any word substituted, any tense altered), the doctrine-keepers will politely correct and ask the Steward to begin again. **Forty-Seven** has the canonical phrase logged.

13. **The Gift-of-Witness** — the Utwig give the Steward a small token: a tiny mask (palm-sized), a folded piece of canonical-script parchment, or (for high-standing Stewards) a small carved wooden Veil-figure. The Steward must hold the token for a canonical 30-second silence. The silence is canonical; *speaking during the silence* is a Veil-betrayal.

14. **The Long Goodbye** — every doctrine-keeper in the immediate vicinity must individually farewell the Steward. This is canonically 12-20 farewells, each with the canonical phrasing *"may your future-tense remain useful to you, Steward"*. Each farewell takes ~20 seconds. The Steward must canonically respond to each with *"and may your present-tense settle in its falling"*. **Mal-Dren is canonically last** (the High Veil farewells after every other doctrine-keeper) and his farewell adds the canonical *private addendum* if the Steward has earned his trust.

#### Farewell (procession to the ship)
15. **The Path-Blessing** — every step the Steward takes from the chamber to the ship is blessed by a Veil-tender walking alongside. Canonical slow procession. The Steward who walks too fast canonically *outpaces the blessing*; the Veil-tender canonically *catches up and re-blesses the missed steps*; the procession effectively slows the Steward to the Veil-tender's pace whether they want it or not.

16. **The Landing-Gear Un-Blessing** (canonical Aaron-example) — the landing gear is ritually un-blessed (the inverse of the landing blessing). Takes ~5 minutes. The Veil-tender canonically *un-anoints* the landing gear with a different oil; recites the *Lift-and-Depart benediction*; the canonical un-blessing must be completed before lift-off.

17. **The Veiled Door** — the door of the ship must be touched by a doctrine-keeper to "seal the visit." The required doctrine-keeper is canonically a *specific one* (the doctrine specifies which by Veil-stage and faction); if that doctrine-keeper is not present, they must be canonically *summoned*. Summoning takes 5-15 minutes depending on which doctrine-keeper and where they are in the settlement.

#### Departure (in the ship, pre-lift)
18. **The Lift-Off Permission** — the Veiled Assembly must formally permit the lift-off via comm channel. This requires a quick Assembly meeting (canonical 8-12 minutes). The Steward must canonically *wait at the controls* until permission arrives. **Forty-Seven** canonically warns the Steward: *"Steward. We are not permitted to lift until the Assembly's permission arrives. I will monitor the comm channel. Please do not engage thrust."*

19. **The Veil-of-Distance** — as the ship lifts, the Veiled Assembly canonically *sings the farewell-tones* via comm channel. The Steward should NOT engage active thrust until the tones complete (canonical ~3 minutes). **Thrust-during-tones is a canonical Veil-betrayal**; the Steward who breaks the tones triggers the canonical Cleanser-vote-confidence escalation. Forty-Seven canonically suggests a *slow drift-departure* during the tones — the ship rises but does not actively burn; the canonical compromise.

20. **The Final-Wave** — every Veil-tender at the spaceport canonically waves as the ship clears the atmosphere. The Steward should canonically wave back through the ship's window. Forty-Seven canonically renders a small animation on the side-port screen showing the Veil-tenders waving (canonical procedural rendering); the Steward can press a button to wave back; canonical *small gesture of canonical politeness*.

| 1 rude decline | Sad; canonical *"The doctrine did not require this. We will continue."* | -2 Utwig standing |

| Active sabotage of a ceremony | Canonical breaking-point; the Veiled Assembly formally bars the Steward from return visits | -15 Utwig standing; quest auto-resolves to the *Doctrine Held* failure branch |

**Important canon**: the Utwig do NOT escalate to violence. They are not capable of it (the doctrinal substrate would not permit). The "they may get mad" canon manifests as *standing collapse + Council-vote-confidence shifts + canonical archive entries* — not combat. The Utwig grieve the Steward's disrespect; they do not attack.

### 10.6 The canonical archive consequence

Every Steward visit is canonically logged in the Utwig's *Falling-Book Appendix-of-Visitors* — a sub-section of the doctrinal text that records the canonical *behavior of each visiting Steward*. The Steward's behavior is described in the canonical voice of the Veil-scholar who maintained the appendix that year. **The appendix survives the Culling.** When the SC2-era Utwig slowly re-emerge into post-Culling sentience 250,000 years later, **they read the appendix**. The Steward's behavior is canonically *known to the SC2 Utwig* — the Steward who honored the doctrine is canonically *remembered with warmth*; the Steward who broke protocol is canonically *remembered with sadness*. This is the slice's most quietly long-term consequence: **the Utwig remember the Steward across 250,000 years.**
