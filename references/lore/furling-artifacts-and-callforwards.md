# Furling-Era Artifacts and SC2 Call-Forwards

> Canonical catalog of artifacts the Furlings (and a few species) construct or deploy during our game's era. **Each one becomes a recognizable SC2 lore item 250,000 years later.** The player may encounter any of these in mid-construction or mid-deployment — these are the *seeds* of the SC2 galaxy. Most are full-game-scope; the slice picks a few to include directly.
>
> This doc is for SC2 fans. Star-Control-Zero-only players will see interesting Furling constructions; SC2 players will get a *quiet jolt of recognition* every few hours of play.

## Big Constructions

### The Sa-Matra — *the great Defender weapon*

The most famous Precursor artifact in SC2: a moon-sized battle platform of devastating power. The Ur-Quan Kzer-Za will eventually capture it and use it to enslave the galaxy.

**In our era**: under active construction by the **Defender faction** of Furlings. Located at a Furling forge-system (SC2 canon has it eventually orbiting the heart of Ur-Quan territory; in our era it's still in the forge). The Defenders building it are Homesteader-aligned — they reject the Migration plan and intend to stay and fight. They believe the Sa-Matra will work against the Others. The Persuaders and Cleansers consider the project misguided, but the project's existence is *not* officially opposed by the Council — partly because the Cleansers privately think it might be useful (as a fallback weapon to eliminate Homesteader holdouts at the last possible moment).

**Slice integration**: a distant orbital construction visible in the galactic map view at a specific star, watched from afar. Not a slice destination. Mentioned by Defender NPCs.

**The bitter irony**: the weapon is *probably* useless against the Others (3D physics doesn't apply), the Defenders die anyway during the Culling, and the weapon survives them — only to be found 250,000 years later by the Ur-Quan, who use it for entirely different purposes (galactic slavery, not Other-defense). The Defenders' best work becomes the SC2 universe's worst tool.

### The Precursor Starship Factory at Unzervalt (Vela) — *the cradle of the SC2 flagship*

SC2 canon: the player's modular flagship is found buried at Unzervalt (Vela cluster), in an abandoned subterranean Precursor factory. Captain Zelnick activates it and flies it through the entire game.

**In our era**: the factory is *operational*. It is **the central manufacturing site for the Furling Scout class** (the player's own ship). All Furling Steward ships rolled out of Unzervalt at some point. The factory employs thousands of Furling shipwrights. Modular-architecture vessels emerge weekly during the Migration's peak — most of them are dispatched as evacuation transports for Precursor-aligned species.

**Slice integration**: probably outside the slice cluster (Unzervalt is in Vela, a specific SC2 cluster). Visible as a distant origin point. The player's own ship was built here; they may receive a memory or log entry about this when they first board.

**Why is it deliberately preserved through the Migration?** The Council debates this. The factory is too large to evacuate, and *destroying it before leaving* is the Cleanser position. The Persuaders argue to seal it intact — *so that any future civilization with the wit to rediscover it can build modular ships and explore the galaxy themselves*. The Persuaders win. The factory is sealed. 250,000 years later Zelnick finds it.

**The Furling who seals the factory is canon for our future game**. A possible mid-game named NPC.

## Smaller Furling Artifacts (Currently Operational, SC2-Recoverable)

### The Sun Device — *stellar-flux harvester*

SC2 canon: a Precursor device the Druuge possess in SC2; it can "extract energy from a star's heart." Critical late-game item.

**In our era**: a Furling research apparatus designed to harvest stellar flux for the Migration's energy needs (the dimensional crossing requires immense power). Three are built; two are deployed; one is **abandoned mid-construction** when its owning research team migrates. That third one is what the Druuge will eventually loot and barter in SC2 era.

### The Aqua Helix — *consciousness-substrate research*

SC2 canon: a Precursor artifact the player retrieves; ties into the Mind-Slaves storyline.

**In our era**: a research artifact developed by Furling **Hider-faction researchers** investigating consciousness-substrate transformation (recall from [the-mmrnmhrm-and-chenjesu.md](the-mmrnmhrm-and-chenjesu.md): the Mmrnmhrm and Chenjesu's non-organic-neural cognition is below the Others' detection threshold; some Furlings wonder if substrate-transformation is a safety path). The Aqua Helix is a *failed prototype* — the early attempt at a consciousness-translation device — abandoned because the Council forbade the research as too speculative in the era of imminent threat. It's left intact at a research station. In SC2 era its function is partially understood and used for psychic purposes.

### The Mycon Egg Cases — *biot reserves*

SC2 canon: three special locations contain Mycon egg cases that can spawn new Mycon when activated. The player uses these in a key SC2 mission.

**In our era**: Furling-designed *biot reserves* — spore-packets containing dormant proto-Mycon that can regenerate a destroyed Mycon hive. The Furlings place several around the galaxy as insurance for the Mycon biot project. **If the slice player chooses to Cleanse the awakening Mycon**, the egg cases survive — and 250,000 years later, *they* are what produces the SC2 Mycon civilization (rather than the line of unbroken biots). Either way, the Mycon exist in SC2 era; the question is *whose lineage*.

### The Burv Broadcasters — *the Burvixese memorial*

SC2 canon: scattered Burvixese hyperwave broadcasters that the player can recover and use as comms infrastructure. The Burvixese themselves are extinct.

**In our era**: the **Burvixese are alive** (see canonical species update below). They are a Precursor-aligned species who migrate with the Furlings. Before they leave, they place hyperwave broadcasters across the galaxy as a **memorial-and-message**: *we were here. We left. If you find this, you are not alone.* The broadcasters are deliberately designed to survive the Culling — their transmissions are too simple to flag the Others, and their physical form is resilient to weather, time, and most types of impact.

### The Taalo Protector — *the gift that outlives its makers*

SC2 canon: a small Precursor-era device that shields against psionic attack; critical for defeating the Talking Pet/Dnyarri.

**In our era**: built by the **Taalo** (see canonical species update below). The Taalo are a peaceful crystalline-amphibious species who are Homesteaders by conviction — they refuse to migrate, they will not flee. They develop the Protector specifically as a defense against the proto-Dnyarri's psionic dominance attempts. The Protector works *too well* — the Taalo are confident in their safety and remain in the galaxy. The Protector does not save them from the Others' Culling. **They die. Their Protector survives. 250,000 years later it's a Furling-style artifact, but its makers were not Furlings.**

### The Ur-Quan Wreck — *the construction site*

SC2 canon: a wrecked Ur-Quan dreadnought lying on a planet's surface in SC2 era.

**In our era**: this same coordinate is one of the **proto-Ur-Quan shipyards** — a planet where the mid-uplift proto-Ur-Quan are first learning to build void-capable ships under Furling supervision. The "wreck" of SC2 era is an early failed-build that was simply left where it crashed. **What SC2 archaeologists call a "wreck" is actually an early model abandoned because the design failed.**

### The Shofixti Glory Device Prototype — *the gift they don't yet understand*

SC2 canon: the Shofixti's suicide-bomb that destroys a star and the Sa-Matra simultaneously. Critical SC2 endgame artifact.

**In our era**: a small prototype hidden by Furlings near the proto-Shofixti's homeworld. The Furlings observed the proto-Shofixti's *kamikaze-bravery* trait (see [proto-species-observations.md](proto-species-observations.md)) and seeded a piece of stellar-disruption tech in a cache, knowing the proto-Shofixti will eventually evolve into a species that *could understand it and would use it well*. The cache is sealed; in SC2 era the (now-sentient) Shofixti unseal it. The Furlings effectively *gave* the Shofixti the means to destroy the Sa-Matra 250,000 years in advance.

### The VUX Beast Genome — *Furling-engineered guardian*

SC2 canon: a giant VUX-bred creature; in SC2 era the player can find or fight one.

**In our era**: Furling bio-engineers, observing the proto-VUX's xenophobic color-discrimination behavior, design a guardian creature for the VUX homeworld. The genome is sealed in a vault — to be activated by VUX descendants once they're sentient enough to need it. The vault remains sealed until SC2 era.

(Aaron's call: do the Furlings *deliberately* seed forward-aimed tech like this for many species? Or is the VUX Beast genome an exception? Default canon: a few species get *seed-gifts* like this; not all. The decision is per-species.)

## Locations With SC2 Significance

### Pluto in Sol (Spathi base) — *a Furling outpost*

SC2 canon: a small Spathi cell hidden on Pluto observes the player early in SC2.

**In our era**: Pluto hosts a small **Furling observation outpost** — used to watch the proto-humans on Sol III at safe distance. The outpost is abandoned when the Furlings migrate. The Spathi will eventually rediscover and occupy it.

### Earth's Moon (Moonbase) — *a Furling repository*

SC2 canon: a Precursor-era moonbase on Earth's moon, attacked by Ur-Quan early in SC2.

**In our era**: a small Furling waystation, sealed before the Migration. Contains records, supplies, and — *canonical addition* — a single dormant Furling Scout-class ship in storage. This is the ship Captain Zelnick will eventually fly. **It was placed there before the Migration as a gift to whoever inherits the Sol system 250,000 years later.** The Furling who chose to leave a ship there is canonically a Persuader-faction Steward, doing the patient, hopeful thing the Persuaders are known for.

Already canon: home of the Slylandro Cloaking Satellite. See [the-precursors-and-homesteaders.md](the-precursors-and-homesteaders.md).

### Procyon (Chenjesu rooted-home)

Already canon: home of the rooted Chenjesu, witness species. See [the-mmrnmhrm-and-chenjesu.md](the-mmrnmhrm-and-chenjesu.md).

## Cultural / Linguistic Call-Forwards

### The Precursor Language

SC2 canon: Precursor writing is untranslatable; the player cannot read most Precursor artifact labels.

**In our era**: it's just **Furling**. The player can read everything (they are a Furling). When SC2-era archaeologists find Furling-language inscriptions 250,000 years later, the language is dead and untranslatable. *The player understands what SC2 players cannot.* This is the deepest fanservice of all: every untranslated Precursor inscription in SC2 was *just a normal Furling sign*, like a "no parking" notice or a maintenance log, and the player gets to read those signs in our game.

**Authoring opportunity**: when the player examines artifacts in our era, they see normal Furling text. Specific signs that appear in SC2 as "untranslatable" should be visible here as their mundane originals. ("FORGE BAY 7 — AUTHORIZED SHIPWRIGHTS ONLY.")

### The Modular Ship Architecture

SC2 canon: the player's Precursor flagship is highly modular and accepts technology from other species.

**In our era**: this is the **Furling Scout class** baseline architecture. The Furlings designed for modularity because the Migration needed adaptable vessels — Furling crews are small (tens of millions across the galaxy) and Steward ships are individually critical. The modular design lets each Steward customize their ship to their cluster's mission. The architecture survives intact at the Unzervalt factory and in stored ships across the galaxy.

### Quasi-Space Navigation

SC2 canon: the Arilou have Quasi-Space portals; SC2 players can use them to teleport.

**In our era**: this is an **Arilou speciality** developed during their voluntary-exile preparation (see [the-precursors-and-homesteaders.md](the-precursors-and-homesteaders.md)). They share basic Quasi-Space-adjacent science with the Furlings for the Slylandro Cloaking Satellite project. SC2-era species who can access Quasi-Space (mostly through Arilou-derived tech) are using science the Arilou pioneered in our era.

## Two SC2 Species We Get to Flesh Out

The Taalo and Burvixese are canonical SC2 species who are *extinct* by SC2 era and barely mentioned. **In our era they are alive**, and the player can interact with them. They become the two "invented" species slots in the [species-content-backlog.md](species-content-backlog.md) — they aren't invented, but their precursor-era forms are largely undefined in SC2, giving us license.

### The Taalo — *Homesteader (Stay), peaceful, makers of the Protector*

**Species**: crystalline-amphibious sentients living on a single tidal world. Peaceful by physiology — their thought patterns include strong empathic resonance with nearby minds. They cannot easily harm without feeling the harm themselves. They are pacifists not by ideology but by sensation.

**Slice/galaxy role**: Homesteader by deep conviction. They refuse to migrate because:
- Their physiology binds them to their world (similar to Slylandro)
- Their pacifism is incompatible with the Cleanser pressure being applied to Homesteaders elsewhere — they will not flee a galaxy whose Furling administrators kill species; they will stay as moral witnesses
- They believe their Protector device will shield them

**The Protector**: their crowning engineering achievement. A psionic-shield generator that defends against mind-domination attacks. They develop it to defend against the proto-Dnyarri's mental influence (which they have detected; the proto-Dnyarri are testing their abilities on neighbors at this stage). The Protector works against psionics. **It does not work against the Others.**

**Their fate**: the Taalo die in the Culling. Their Protector survives them, sealed in a vault. In SC2 era, the player retrieves it.

**Slice integration**: the Taalo are an *optional, lore-rich Homesteader encounter* — visit them, hear their philosophy, watch them complete the Protector, and know what happens next. **They cannot be saved.** The player has no path that prevents their death; they can only honor them by remembering. This is one of the slice's *most poignant beats*.

**Voice**: slow, formal, deeply considered. They use a singular plural ("we-all" — the resonance of the colony's empathic field speaks). They are not bitter about their fate. They are *grateful* to the player for visiting.

### The Burvixese — *Precursor (Go), memorialists, makers of the Broadcasters*

**Species**: industrious humanoid-with-extra-appendages engineers. Pragmatic. Scientifically curious. They believe the Furlings about the Others and they have no biological constraint preventing migration. They are an *easy* Precursor-faction recruit.

**Slice/galaxy role**: their unique cultural project is the **Burv Broadcasters** — a galaxy-wide network of resilient hyperwave transmitters they place before they migrate. The Broadcasters' purpose: *if any sentience emerges in this galaxy after the Culling, they will hear our voices*. The Broadcasters transmit short messages — Burvixese songs, philosophical fragments, a brief description of the Migration, instructions for anyone who wants to follow.

**Their fate**: they migrate with the Furlings. They survive in the new galaxy. **In SC2 era they are presumed extinct because their broadcasters were left behind and their homeworld is empty.** *Aaron's lore call*: are the Burvixese still alive somewhere in the new galaxy in SC2 era? Probably yes, but unreachable from our universe. Their broadcasters are the only artifacts of theirs that remain.

**Slice integration**: the Burvixese have one or more colonies in the slice cluster. The player can help them place broadcasters (a small side-quest), receive a Burvixese signal-archive as a personal gift, and see them off through the dimensional crossing. **Of all the slice species, the Burvixese are the easiest to migrate** — they want to leave, they have no constraints, they are good at logistics. They are the slice's *most cheerful Precursor encounter*. A bit of warmth in a heavy story.

**Voice**: practical, optimistic, slightly verbose. They use technical vocabulary and frequent metaphors involving sound and resonance. They are warm but rushed (the Migration deadline is real). They will give the player a Burvixese Memorial Broadcaster (a portable version) as a thank-you.

## The Spirit of the Call-Forwards

The galaxy of our game is *full of the seeds of SC2*. Every SC2 lore item exists in some pre-state in our era. The Furlings made most of them. Some species — Taalo, Burvixese — make their own. The Furlings will leave most of them behind when they migrate, *deliberately*. They are not lost artifacts; they are **memorials, gifts, infrastructure, hopes**. The Precursor-faction Furlings want the post-Culling galaxy to find a livable world full of useful objects. The Homesteader species who die leave their works as legacies that outlive them. Every Steward who walks through Unzervalt's factory or Sol III's Moonbase is participating in a long act of *leaving things behind for whoever comes next*.

This is *love letter design*. SC2's "Precursor mystery" is, in our game, a Furling civilization patiently preparing the galaxy for its own future. The mystery is not what happened to them; the mystery is **how much they cared**.
