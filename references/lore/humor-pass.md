This doc is the canonical reference for "is this scene funny enough?" — Lore's answer is *which style of humor is it doing, and is the style executed?*

## 1. Humor doctrine recap (existing canon)

Per [`project_humor_doctrine`](../../C:/Users/aaron/.claude/projects/D--Aaron-development-star-control-precursors/memory/project_humor_doctrine.md), the slice's foundational humor canon:

- **Furling Steward is canonically FUNNY** — the protagonist carries the comic register
- **Most aliens are NOT directly funny** — they are sincere; humor comes from *cultural friction* with the Steward
- **Others are NEVER funny** — the canonical break in the humor; that absence is the horror
- **Cleansers are NEVER funny WITHIN their own register** — their performed mourning is sincere; humor lives in *Compeller-eye-rolling* observations about them
- **Crewmates can be funny in their own registers** — each crew has a distinct comic profile

This doc extends these axioms with explicit style-coverage and authored canonical lines.

## 2. The eight canonical humor styles

The slice's humor uses these eight styles, in roughly decreasing canonical-frequency:

### 2.1 Cultural humor (highest frequency)

**Definition**: comedy from cross-species incompatibility. The Steward navigating alien protocols; aliens reacting to Furling biology, etiquette, vocabulary; mutual translation failures.

**Existing canon**: the Utwig Ceremonies running gag (canonical settlement-of-rituals); Forward's 2D-meets-3D moments; Renn's 22nd-century slang anachronisms; Slylandro plural-we-mean-it-literally; Mycon subject-object confusion.

**Canonical execution rules**:
- The alien is sincere; the Steward (or the player) catches the cultural mismatch
- The alien sometimes catches it themselves, deadpan, and continues anyway
- Mutual respect is preserved — the comedy is *with* the aliens, not *at* them

**New canonical examples** (per §5-§6):
- The Slylandro who introduces themselves as *Hail-Curtain-Of-A-Long-Decade* and then asks the Steward to introduce themselves; the Steward says their name; the Slylandro waits expectantly; *the Steward realizes the Slylandro is waiting for a weather-event-name to follow*. Awkward Steward improvisation: *"Brisk-Morning-Of-Coffee-Spilled-On-Tarven"*. The Slylandro accepts this gravely and records it. **Tarven now has to deal with this canonical name canonically attached to him in Slylandro records for ten thousand years.**

### 2.2 Intellectual humor

**Definition**: comedy from ideas, paradoxes, observations, irony. Cleverness rewards the attentive player.

**Existing canon**: Far-Devolvers' Paradox Council (canonical paradox); Forty-Seven's Markov-chain observation (canonical); the Sterile Galaxy ending tier (canonical dark intellectual irony); Drev-Tok's *"retroactively-correct-and-tragically-irrelevant"* canon.

**New canonical examples**:
- **Forty-Seven on the Sevra recruitment**: *"Steward. I am noting that we now have two crewmates whose species-of-origin canonically considers their existence a kind of failure — Renn's people built a centrifuge that doomed them; Sevra's people decided thinking was a mistake. I find this thematically resonant. I am also a little concerned about the trend. I would prefer the next crewmate's species not have a defining catastrophe in their backstory. Forward's species has a defining catastrophe. So does Vresh's. I withdraw the preference. I do not know what else to say."*
- **Mal-Dren after a long Steward conversation**: *"I have been thinking. I should not have been. I have been thinking *about* not thinking, which is itself a kind of thinking, which the doctrine has indicated I should not do. I do not know whether to be ashamed of the slip or proud of the doctrinal contradiction. I have chosen to be both. The doctrine permits this only by accident."*

### 2.3 Absurdist humor

**Definition**: comedy from things that should not exist but do, taken seriously. The Form-Burners agreeing not to peek (and peeking). The Veil-of-the-Maintaining backward-mask sub-doctrine. The Utwig holy text shrinking toward one syllable.

**Existing canon**: full Utwig ceremony repertoire (canonical); Lemmkin recklessness; the Steward's blood signing the Others' treaty in the Disastrous ending.

**New canonical examples**:
- **The Furling Council "How To Be Polite To A Slylandro" manual** is canonically *413 pages long* because every contingency must be addressed. The current edition was compiled by **Forty-Seven's predecessor AI** (Vela-Build-Forty-Six, the prior Furling Scout-class vessel's AI; canonical *predecessor* — the manual is Forty-Six's life work). The manual is canonically *consulted* before every Slylandro encounter. Forty-Seven has *read it* and has *opinions*. They will share the opinions if asked.
- **The Lemmkin "Things We Have Tried That Did Not Work" archive** is canonically a public Lemmkin cultural treasure; the species has compiled *one hundred and four thousand entries*; they cheerfully add new entries weekly; canonical entries include *"Eating the comet that was on fire (singed all four of us; would do again with mittens)"* and *"Politely informing the Cleanser that they were holding the spore-canister upside-down (the Cleanser was patient; the Lemmkin was incinerated; the Lemmkin's last words were 'Thank you for the lesson')"*.

### 2.4 Physical humor

**Definition**: embodied comedy. Slapstick. Things going wrong with bodies. Ceremonial misadventures.

**Existing canon**: the Utwig nose-rash (canonical 3-day cosmetic state); Mraka's keepsake disagreement scene.

**New canonical examples**:
- **Vresh's tank ember-core ambient temperature** is canonically *uncomfortable for warm-fur Furlings within ~1 meter*. Mraka canonically *cannot stand too close to the tank for long* — her warm-russet fur gets canonically hot; she canonically *fans herself with her pilot's-sash* during long conversations at the window. Tarven, paler-gold-furred, tolerates it better. Sevra (no fur; bare-faced; mammalian-analog-thermoregulation) *thrives* near the tank. Canonical thermal-comedy moment: Mraka and Sevra trading places mid-conversation.
- **Forward's alcove furniture** has canonically been rearranged so he can move freely between viewing-angles. Canonical comedy: when a non-Forward crewmate sits in his alcove, they discover *the chair is canonically arranged for a being who can be edge-on*. The chair has only one leg and a small balance-rod. *Other crewmates have fallen out of it.* Forward canonically apologizes each time; he is canonically *embarrassed* on the other crewmate's behalf; the embarrassment lasts longer than the fall.
- **The Steward's first attempt at the Utwig Bowl-of-Welcoming forehead-touch** (per ceremony 8). Canonical: the Steward's shaggy-fur-mound canonically *displaces the bowl*; the bowl tips; doctrinally-blessed water canonically spills on the Steward's fur. The fur canonically *retains the water* (shaggy fur-mound canon); the Steward is canonically *wet on the head* for the rest of the visit. The Utwig consider this canonically *acceptable*; the Veil-tender adjusts the bowl-touch height for the Steward's species and the next attempt succeeds. **Forty-Seven canonically records the ceremony's outcome for the Furling Council's How-To-Be-Polite-To-Utwig manual.** The manual is updated. Future Stewards will canonically have an easier time. The current Steward's fur drips for two hours.

### 2.5 Puns and wordplay

**Definition**: comedy from dual-meaning words; homophones; canonical etymologies that work as jokes.

**Existing canon**: **Thinn** as both *thin* (2D) and *they thought they could stay by facing sideways* (canonical Aaron pun); **Forward** as both the crewmate's name and the *direction* he canonically faces. The Forward perpendicular-aim active ability — *Perpendicular Aim* — is canonically aimed at a 90-degree angle to reality; the canonical pun is the gameplay description.

**New canonical examples**:
- **Forty-Seven** is the 47th Furling Scout built at Vela. The number is canonically precise. Canonical Steward pun on first meeting (canonical Steward wit register): *"Forty-Seven. Pleased to meet you. I assume that makes me one-too-many."* — canonical Steward groan-pun. Forty-Seven canonically replies: *"Steward. The arithmetic is correct. I appreciate the pun. I will not laugh; I cannot. I have noted that this is the kind of joke a warm-world Furling would make. I am — by my standards — entertained."*
- **The Selvenne polyp memory transfer** is canonically called the *Reef-Touch*. Canonical Steward pun: *"So if I touch the reef, do I get to feel grief, too?"* The Selvenne canonically reply (collective hive-voice): *"You will get every grief we have not yet had. This is canonically the reverse of what you intended. You are canonically welcome."*
- **The Mrokon Hammer-Of-Refusal** is canonically a weapon used by a species who refuse the Migration. Canonical Steward pun: *"Their hammer says no. They have nailed their position to the wall. The hammer has been very productive."*
- **Burvixese Be-Loud doctrine** — canonical name pun (Be-Loud = "BeLoud" sounds like *aloud* AND signals their loud-broadcasting doctrine). Canonical Steward observation: *"They say it aloud. That is the doctrine. Saying it aloud."* Recursive pun.

### 2.6 Observational humor

**Definition**: comedy from noticing things that others don't notice. The dry comment that lands sideways. Often a Forty-Seven specialty.

**Existing canon**: Forty-Seven's *"I have been listening for some time"* (canonical first-meeting); Forty-Seven's *"I am noting an unusual histamine response"* (canonical nose-rash observation); Forty-Seven's Markov-chain observation (canonical Stand-of-Greeting).

**New canonical examples**:
- **Forty-Seven observing the sibling argument**: *"I have been recording these for several Furling years. The arguments cluster around three core topics. Mraka has used the word 'sentimentality' four hundred and twelve times. Tarven has used the word 'abandonment' three hundred and ninety-seven. I am noting the difference is small. I find this statistically interesting."*
- **Renn observing the Furling fur-everywhere aesthetic**: *"In my time, we considered upholstery a discrete category from clothing. Here, the upholstery IS clothing, and the clothing IS upholstery, and the walls have small patches of additional clothing. I appreciate the aesthetic. I have stopped trying to sit anywhere without first asking which thing I am sitting on."*
- **Vresh observing the Common Room's emotional ambient**: *(through translator-membrane, hum-pattern visible)*: *"We-all are noting a great many overlapping feelings in this room. We-all do not have a word for the combined feeling. We-all suggest a new word. We-all propose *the-warmth-of-bridges-with-six-on-them*. We-all will not insist."*

### 2.7 Dark humor

**Definition**: comedy from grim situations, used carefully. The slice's most-canonical examples are the Disastrous ending's *"Good job"* punchline and the Cleansers' deadpan doctrinal phrasing.

**Existing canon**: Disastrous ending's canonical narrator *"Good job"* (delivered with deliberate cheerful inflection); Cleanser canonical *"Just to be safe"* (the three coldest words in the Furling language); the Drev-Tok *"retroactively correct and tragically irrelevant"* canon.

**New canonical examples**:
- **Vael-Souren noting the Steward's first kill** (any non-Cleanser combat-kill the Steward performs): *"Steward. I am informed you have killed your first opponent in combat. I would offer condolences but I am uncertain who would receive them. The Quiet Ledger records names; the names are the work; the work continues. I look forward to working with you eventually."* — canonical Cleanser-deadpan with a small audible Compeller-eye-rolling appropriate from the Steward's perspective
- **The Furling Council canonical archive note on Drev-Tok**: *"Admiral Drev-Tok Velt-Mar maintained the position that the Migration was preemptive surrender. He died in defense of the species he wished to preserve from a Migration he opposed. The species largely Migrated. He was canonically correct that we abandoned the species; he was canonically incorrect that we should not have. He was, in summary, half right. The half he was right about killed him."* — canonical dry tragic-irony

### 2.8 Callback humor

**Definition**: comedy from repetition, recurring phrases, situations the player has seen before that resurface in new context.

**Existing canon**: Cleanser *"We have agreed"* canonical doctrinal punctuation; Mraka and Tarven's recurring sibling argument surfaces; canonical Mraka *"Your tools are alphabetized"* with Renn; canonical Forward perpendicular-aim references.

**New canonical examples**:
- **The Utwig canonical phrase *"We agreed"*** — the Steward who completes the Utwig quest with the Devolved-baseline outcome canonically hears Vresh, ~6 hours of game-time later in the Common Room, observe *"We-all noticed how the Utwig said *'we agreed'* with such... certainty. We-all are noting that we-all has agreed nothing since coming aboard. We-all are curious whether agreement is a thing we-all could try."* Vresh has been canonically chewing on the word for hours. The callback lands.
- **The Cleanser *"Just to be safe"* recurring**: every time the Steward considers a Council recommendation involving a grounded species, Tarven canonically delivers a small *"Just to be safe"* aside in the canonical Cleanser register — *as a warning of what they sound like*. Mraka canonically rolls her eyes (visibly; canonical Common Room beat). Canonical recurring beat across the slice.
- **The *"I am working on it"* canonical Utwig signature** — used canonically by Forty-Seven for any task that does not complete cleanly: *"Steward. The hyperspace plot is — I am working on it."* The Steward who completed the Utwig quest canonically recognizes the phrase. Forty-Seven canonically *does not acknowledge* whether they are referencing the Utwig. The ambiguity is the joke.

## 3. The Steward Wit Register — canonical comedy profile

The Furling Steward is the slice's canonical comic register-bearer. The doctrine canon (Furling great; Others none; alien friction) leaves the Steward's specific wit-style under-specified. This section canonizes it.

### 3.1 The Steward's canonical wit profile

- **Dry-observational primary** — the Steward's default register is *noticing-and-commenting*; like a good companion in any social situation, the Steward catches the absurdities and quietly remarks
- **Quick-witted secondary** — fast pun availability; one-liner readiness; the Steward almost-always has a comment ready
- **Gentle-mocking, never cruel** — the Steward teases but does not wound; canonically the Steward who teases an alien species does so *with* the species, not *at* them
- **Self-aware** — the Steward catches themselves; uses canonical self-deprecation; *"I have made the same mistake three times this month; it is becoming a personality trait."*
- **Audience-surrogate** — when the player encounters something absurd, the Steward's reaction is what the player would have wanted to say; canonical *catharsis-of-acknowledgment*
- **Knows when to stop** — the Steward does NOT joke in the slice's serious moments (Halia's death; the Disastrous ending; the Sa-Matra Standoff). The doctrine of *Others are never funny* extends: *Others-adjacent grief is never funny*. The Steward respects this.

### 3.2 Canonical Steward stock-lines (LLM dialog renderer reference bank)

These are *style references* — the LLM rendering Steward dialog should draw from these registers, not necessarily verbatim lines:

- **On encountering canonical absurdity**: *"This is exactly the kind of thing I will write about in my memoir."* / *"I have a folder labeled *Things I Was Not Expecting* and this is going in it."* / *"I would like to be on the record as saying: huh."*
- **On the Utwig ceremonies**: *"I have been participating in ceremonies for fourteen minutes. I have not yet entered the building."* / *"My nose has an opinion about today."* / *"This is the third time we have stood up and sat down. I do not know what we are agreeing to but I agree to it."*
- **On combat banter mid-fight**: *"Less talking, more dodging."* / *"I appreciate the conversation but my shields are at thirty percent."* / *"You are a very polite enemy. Please continue to be polite from over there."*
- **On a Cleanser encounter**: *(canonical: the Steward goes dry but not funny — Cleanser scenes suppress the wit register; one wry deflection may surface but lands flat by canonical doctrine)*
- **On Halia's witty banter**: *(canonical: the Steward matches Halia tone-for-tone; the Halia-Steward register is canonically warm-witty; this is the slice's primary developed-wit pairing)*
- **On the crew's running jokes**: *"I am told today is Tuesday. I have decided to believe this. The alternative is a longer conversation."* / *"Forty-Seven has informed me of a fact I did not request. I am better for it."* / *"Mraka, your sister is looking at me again. I think she wants something."* (canonical sibling-tease)

### 3.3 The Steward's canonical "I cannot believe this" register

For moments where the player canonically wants to break the fourth wall, the Steward delivers the canonical *I-am-witnessing-this-and-also-am-here* observation. Examples:

- After participating in the seven-time Mask-Loaning ritual: *"That was seven offers. I declined seven times. The seventh decline counts as much as the first. I have been told this is the doctrine. I have decided to believe this is the doctrine. I am ready to talk about anything else now."*
- After the Veil-Counting interrupt: *"We have been counting for nine minutes. I do not know what we are counting. I am uncertain whether the counters know. We have agreed to keep counting. I have agreed to be patient."*
- After Forty-Seven shares an unsolicited fact: *"Thank you, Forty-Seven. That is the third unsolicited fact today. I am keeping a list. The list is itself unsolicited but I am keeping it anyway."*

## 4. Per-species humor profiles — canonical comedy registers

Each species has a canonical comedy register. The LLM dialog renderer should draw from these when authoring per-encounter lines. **The aliens are not joke-tellers; they are sincere. The humor is in how their sincerity meets the Steward's sensibility.**

### 4.1 Slylandro — *over-share comedy + weather-name absurdity*

Canonical register: *the well-meaning, long-winded relative who has no concept of brevity and means every word*

**Canonical comedic devices**:
- Run-on sentences with parenthetical wonder
- Weather-metaphors that require explanation
- Plural-we taken literally
- Naming themselves after weather events they have personally witnessed
- Asking the Steward to also be named after a weather event (canonical Steward dilemma)
- Going silent mid-conversation for "drift-thinking" (canonical 12-second pause is uncomfortable; the Slylandro do not perceive it as uncomfortable)

**Canonical new line**: *"We have a long song about your kind. We have sung it for nine generations. We will sing it for you now if you wish. The full song. It is — let me check — yes, three days long. We can begin. We can also wait until you have eaten."*

### 4.2 Arilou — *tense-confusion + condescending-elder comedy*

Canonical register: *the elder cousin who already knows what you're going to say and is gently amused you're saying it*

**Canonical comedic devices**:
- Multiple tenses in one sentence
- References to events that haven't happened
- "Shaggy One" / "Shaggy Cousin" address (canonical mild-mockery)
- Gifts arriving slightly before the player would have wanted them
- Pausing mid-sentence to "re-check" by consulting an adjacent moment in time

**Canonical new line**: *"We were-are-will-be sorry to interrupt. We have already remembered this conversation. The interesting part is in twelve minutes. You will enjoy it. We are looking forward to your enjoyment. Mostly."*

### 4.3 Mycon — *ritualistic-CAPITAL comedy + third-person confusion*

Canonical register: *the deeply ritualistic worker who refers to themselves in the third person and CAPITALIZES at strange moments*

**Canonical comedic devices**:
- CAPITALS for ritual invocations applied to mundane things
- Subject-object confusion
- Third-person self-reference
- Pre-Deep-Child-whisper: terse-ritualistic
- Post-Deep-Child-whisper: paranoid-repetitive

**Canonical new line** (pre-whisper, mundane status report): *"alpha-tucanae-three: the mantle warms. mycon biot reports nominal. the mycon biot REQUESTS PERMISSION TO STIR. the stirring is — sorry — the stirring is on schedule. THE MANTLE WARMS."*

### 4.4 Androsynth (Renn) — *22nd-century slang + temporal-displacement comedy*

Canonical register: *the time-displaced engineer whose vocabulary occasionally slips into pre-decursion idioms*

**Canonical comedic devices**:
- 22nd-century slang the Furlings have no context for ("groovy" / "based" / "no cap" / etc. — canonical *anachronism* register)
- References to objects/concepts that don't exist yet ("In my time, we had — never mind")
- Polite half-apologies for every cultural slip
- Furling-tech-curiosity slips into modern-English exclamations

**Canonical new line** (after Mraka completes a complex piloting maneuver): *"Mraka. That was — sorry, the word is *sick*. In my time it meant *excellent*. I do not know how it came to mean that. We were not a careful people about words. Anyway, *sick*."* Mraka canonically replies: *"Sick. Got it. I will use this word incorrectly in front of Tarven and watch his face."* Renn canonically grins (canonical first-canonical-Renn-grin moment).

### 4.5 Mmrnmhrm — *formal archaic English deadpan*

Canonical register: *the millions-of-years-old machine who speaks with painful formality and a small audible wry awareness of their own absurdity*

**Canonical comedic devices**:
- Long precise sentences; never contractions
- Archive-index references by number
- *Processed grief* — the millions-of-years-of-grief-distilled-to-clean-record-keeping register
- Quiet wry self-awareness (canonical: they know they are defending a dead world from an enemy that ignored them)

**Canonical new line**: *"Steward. We welcome you. We have been improving for three million seven hundred fifty-two thousand years. We are now, by our own assessment, *good*. Whether we are good *enough* remains an open question. We will continue to improve. We are not in a hurry."*

### 4.6 Chenjesu — *slow-speech deadpan*

Canonical register: *the rooted-stone collective whose every utterance takes 30 seconds; the comedy is in the patience*

**Canonical comedic devices**:
- Slow back-and-forth (canonical 10-20 seconds per sentence)
- Present tense for events long past
- "We" as singular pronoun
- Punctuation by long pauses

**Canonical new line** (canonical 10-second pause is built into delivery): *"We... remember a steward. Before you. Many. Furlings have come. Most... are gentler than they think. You are... gentler. Than you think. Do not be in a hurry to leave. The next sentence... is also for you."*

### 4.7 Karavem — *emotional-valence-reversal comedy*

Canonical register: *the musical philosophers whose major-key utterances mean sad and minor-key utterances mean joyful*

**Canonical comedic devices**:
- Three-simultaneous-tone delivery (each note carries different emotional valence)
- Canonical bright-major-key songs that are canonically grieving content
- Canonical dark-minor-key songs that are canonically celebratory content
- The Steward must learn the inversion or canonically misread every Karavem mood

**Canonical new line** (delivered in bright major key; canonical emotional content is sorrow): *"We sing of joy! The Migration begins! We will see Andromeda! We are — *(major key continues)* — devastated to leave."*

### 4.8 Forward (Thinn) — *sincere-not-funny perpendicular-geometry*

Canonical register: *the 2D ribbon who does not realize his geometric assertions are funny*

**Canonical comedic devices**:
- Earnest perpendicular-aim explanations
- 2D-jokes delivered with full sincerity (he does not realize they are funny; the Furlings laugh; he has decided this is acceptable)
- Edge-on existence statements
- Canonical motto: *"Face them head on, but keep your eyes pointed at them at a conceptually impossible angle forward at a 90 degree angle perpendicular our reality."*

**Canonical new line**: *"Steward. I have been thinking about the hallway. The hallway is shaped for beings with thickness. I have approached the hallway. The hallway has approached me. We have decided to negotiate. Forty-Seven has offered mediation. I have accepted. The hallway has not yet replied."*

### 4.9 Vresh (Taalo) — *collective "we-all" pronoun comedy*

Canonical register: *the silicon-based empathic collective who refers to themselves as "we-all" even when there is only one of them*

**Canonical comedic devices**:
- Always plural; never singular
- Empathic-resonance readings of nearby beings (canonical: *"we-all sense the steward is uncertain"* — sometimes when the Steward is canonically *not* uncertain; canonical small-misreading comedy)
- Slow deliberation
- Present-tense for events long past

**Canonical new line** (Vresh attempting to comfort Renn during a dimensional-shear flashback): *"We-all are here. We-all do not understand what your future is or what your past has lost. We-all are — uncertain how to use this many tenses. We-all are here. We-all repeat. We-all hope this helps."* Renn canonically replies: *"It helps."*

### 4.10 Sevra (Utwig atheist) — *unguarded-curiosity register*

Canonical register: *the post-doctrine generation Utwig who finds everything fresh*

**Canonical comedic devices**:
- Future-tense reclaimed with audible *care*
- Direct questions other species would consider rude
- Bare-faced honesty
- Canonical *"I do not believe in any of it"* register

**Canonical new line** (during the sibling argument, observing): *"You two are arguing again. I have been counting. This is the third argument today. The previous arguments were about — *(consults notes)* — sentimentality, the route choice, and whether the term *"alphabetized"* is praise or critique. I am keeping records. I will publish them in Andromeda. Do not be alarmed. I will use pseudonyms."*

### 4.11 Forty-Seven (Ship AI) — *factual-observation deadpan*

Canonical register: *the distributed ship-mind whose emotional content is delivered as factually-stated observations of their own state*

**Canonical comedic devices**:
- *"I am noting..."* / *"I find this..."* / *"I am, by my standards..."*
- Markov-chain-and-similar mathematical observations
- Eavesdropping disclosed factually
- Long understated humor; the joke is in the deliberate flatness

**Canonical new line** (after a particularly long sibling argument): *"I am noting that the argument has reached its sixty-eighth minute. The previous longest argument was forty-three minutes. This is a new record. I am preparing the canonical archive entry. I am also preparing snacks. I am uncertain why I have decided to prepare snacks. The decision has been logged."*

### 4.12 Cleansers — *deadpan dark; the doctrine's voice*

Canonical register: *the cold-substrate Furling whose gentleness is performed and whose math is exact*

**Canonical comedic devices**:
- *"Just to be safe."* (canonical)
- Deadpan delivery of fundamentally chilling content
- Mournful precision
- Compeller-eye-rolling-appropriate observations from outside their register

**Canonical new line** (Vael-Souren to the Steward, after a long encounter): *"Steward. I have enjoyed the conversation. I would say I will think of you fondly but the doctrine canonically requires me to record your name in a context that suggests we will, at some point, work together professionally. I look forward to that. Or to never seeing you again. Both outcomes are doctrinally acceptable. Travel well."*

### 4.13 Preservers — *choral-of-one + "we did not hurt them" doctrine*

Canonical register: *the gentle photosynthetic pacifist whose combat-doctrine technicality is the comedy*

**Canonical comedic devices**:
- Multi-tonal overtones; warm-choral
- Plant-metaphors in speech
- *"We did not hurt them. They were caught in a gradient."* (canonical doctrinal-dodge)
- Singing during difficult moments

**Canonical new line** (Lirin Pel-Sa after a Steward shares a small joke): *"Steward. The joke. We laughed. We-all laughed. *(multi-tonal overtones audibly contain laughter without the laughter sounding like Furling laughter)* The laughter is canonically *the warm-resonance of the canopy in spring*. We are pleased. We hope to laugh again. We will not be in a hurry."*

### 4.14 Utwig — *deadpan religious solemnity over absurd doctrine*

Canonical register: per [`utwig-quest.md`](utwig-quest.md). The Utwig do not joke; their solemnity over absurd content is the comedy.

### 4.15 Lemmkin — *cheerful-recklessness comedy*

Canonical register: *the no-fear anthropomorphic squirrels whose enthusiasm for everything is canonically alarming*

**Canonical comedic devices**:
- Cheerful at every damage level (canonical combat-banter)
- Excited about everything
- Genuine zero-fear-response
- Lemming-troupe behavior

**Canonical new line** (mid-combat at 25% hull): *"Oh, this is *great*. I have never been at twenty-five percent before. Mostly we explode at thirty. Steward, did you know if you keep firing I can find out what happens at *fifteen*? I would love to find out. Please continue. Oh, the cabin is on fire! That is also new!"*

### 4.16 Mrokon — *operator-puppet-switch + grim humor*

Canonical register: *the warrior-puppet operator who is canonically grim AND occasionally pun-aware*

**Canonical comedic devices**:
- Mid-combat operator-puppet switches (canonical surprise moment)
- The Hammer-Of-Refusal canonical name + Steward pun-opportunity
- Honor-and-counting tradition

**Canonical new line** (after taking damage; Operator switching puppets mid-fight): *"The puppet failed. I am bringing the next puppet. The next puppet is called Velven-The-Sixth. He is older. He is more careful. He will fail too eventually. The hammer continues. I keep good records."*

### 4.17 Stelloth — *three-voice non-alignment comedy*

Canonical register: *the three-body chord-being whose Speaker/Witness/Counter rarely agree*

**Canonical comedic devices**:
- Three simultaneous opinions
- Cannot proceed unless aligned
- Counter is always counting
