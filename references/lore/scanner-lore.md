# Scanner Lore — Stars, Planets, and What the Furling Sensors See

> Authored 2026-05-17 per Aaron's brief: *"Planet lore and star lore.
> For significant stars (large, has a homeworld, is somehow unique)
> there is lore generated for it that our scanner will reveal. If a
> planet is special, it also has lore. Most gas giants are not
> interesting, but the Slylandro one sure is."*
>
> Player-facing canonical lore the Furling Scout's scanner surfaces on
> system entry / planet orbit / surface descent. **Voice**: Furling
> Steward's observation register — quiet, factual, occasionally warm
> or wry. Designed to *reward exploration* and provide canonical SC2
> reference depth for fans, **backdated by the canonical 250kya gap**
> between our slice's setting and SC2's era.

## How this doc is used

Design's scanner system reads this canonical content and surfaces text passages when the player:
- **Enters a system** (star lore) — brief 1-2 paragraph reveal
- **Orbits a planet** (planet lore) — main reveal; full passage
- **Lands on a planet surface** (surface-detail lore) — optional deeper reveal

Each entry tagged with **reveal-trigger** and **canon-source** (SC2 reference if applicable).

## SC2 reference backdating principle

Where canonical SC2/SC3 lore references the planet's later state, our slice's scanner reveals the **state of the planet 250,000 years prior**. Cities haven't been built yet; species that arise later don't exist yet; geographic features are *deep-past* versions of their SC2-era forms. Volcanic worlds may be *more active*; ice worlds may be *less retreated*; biological worlds may be *unevolved*. The Furling Steward sees what is *there now*, not what will be there.

### ⭐ 1. Mh-Lai *(synthetic at 1900, 1600)*

**Star — Mh-Lai's Hearth** *(G-class yellow-orange dwarf; ~4.2 billion years old; canonical Furling home star)*

**Scanner reveals**: *"The Furlings' Hearth. Stable yellow-orange dwarf. Sustained habitability across four billion years. Three confirmed Furling-era colonization waves; the fourth is your own. The star has a faint canonical name in Furling — *Mh-Layi-Olen*, 'the patient one' — though the Furlings rarely speak it; they save the name for the moments that matter."*

- **Planet I — Mh-Lai Prime** *(TERRESTRIAL; canonical Furling homeworld)*: *"Mh-Lai. Capital of the Furling Persuader Council. Furred-everywhere interior architecture; canopy cities woven into the planet's mountain ridges; the canonical Mh-Lai Station orbits at L1 with the Hearth. Population: ~2.3 billion Furlings. Climate: warm-temperate; long autumn-and-spring; brief sharp summers; rich-resource planet. The Council chambers are in the Persuader Council Hall on the third continent."* **Post-Fall scanner reveal differs** — see `the-fall-of-mh-lai.md`; the canonical text reads *"Mh-Lai. Consumed. The Council chambers are gone. Hearth-of-Iron docked at L1 carries the survivors."*
- **Planet II — Furlmart** *(ROCKY moon-class; tutorial location)*: *"Furlmart. Mh-Lai's third moon. Mineral-rich cargo-handling depot; Furlmart Cooperative runs the docks. ## The 10 Rainbow Worlds

> Per [rainbow-worlds-arc.md](rainbow-worlds-arc.md), the 10 Rainbow Worlds form an arrow pointing at the dimensional-crossing into the Andromeda Galaxy. SC2 archaeologists 250kya later misread the arrow as "Galactic Core"; we know better. **One Rainbow World is Alpha Andromedae** (canonical diegetic wink).

Each Rainbow World canonically shows the same **chromatic-iridescent surface signature** to scanners — concentric color bands shifting through the spectrum as the planet rotates. SC2 calls them "rainbow worlds" for this exact visual. In our slice they are *un-Resonated* until the Steward seeds them with the Rainbow Resonator.

### 🌈 Rainbow World #1 — *Aurora-Threshold* (canonically the first on the arrow)

**Scanner reveals**: *"Rainbow World #1. Chromatic-iridescent surface signature. Concentric color bands shifting through the spectrum as the planet rotates. **The arrow begins here.** Unresonated; the Furling Rainbow Resonator module is required to seed this world. SC2 archaeologists 250kya later will catalog this as one of the canonical ten Rainbow Worlds without knowing why they exist."*

### 🌈 Rainbow World #2-9 — *(canonical seven middle-arrow worlds)*

**Scanner reveals (generic per planet)**: *"Rainbow World [N]. Chromatic-iridescent signature. Unresonated. Required for the Rainbow Worlds arrow-completion. The Furling Rainbow Resonator module's seed pulses align this planet's dimensional-substrate with the crossing-vector. SC2 archaeologists will catalog its location precisely 250kya later but will lack the canonical context."*

### 🌈 Rainbow World #10 — *Alpha Andromedae* (canonical diegetic wink)

**Scanner reveals**: *"Rainbow World #10. **Alpha Andromedae.** The arrow-tip world. Chromatic-iridescent signature like the others, but here the canonical *Andromeda promise* is faintly visible at the geometric resolution — the dimensional substrate of THIS planet *partially aligns with the destination galaxy's substrate*. The Furlings consider this the canonical *signpost* — pointed at where they are going. **SC2 archaeologists 250kya later will catalog this as a star in the constellation Andromeda; they will not realize the slice's Furlings chose it deliberately as the arrow-tip.**"*

## Authoring backlog (Tier 3 — Earth + SC2 mainstream homeworlds)

Per Aaron's brief, planets canonically in SC2/SC3 should get aged-by-250kya lore. **Tier 3 backlog** for follow-up authoring passes:

- **Earth (Sol III)** — canonically *empty of sentient life* in our era; proto-humans are pre-Neolithic hunter-gatherers ~250kya before SC2's Captain Zelnick is born; SC2 archaeology canonically misses this era because pre-Neolithic humans leave no archaeological signature
- **Alpha Centauri** — canonically explored by SC2 era; in our slice, *just a system*; the Furlings have catalogued it as low-priority
- **Major SC2 species homeworlds** that already exist in our slice (Burvix Caster star = Burvixese homeworld; Gamma Vulpeculae already covered; Beta Corvi already covered; Delta Crateris = Mycon canonical SC2 location — backdate as biot-cradle world)
- **The Sa-Matra prototype site** — canonically the Defender-faction's build-site for the prototype; the Sa-Matra Prototype that fires at the Final Conflict is *built somewhere* — that location deserves lore
- **The Burv Caster system** — Burvixese homeworld where the Caster fires
- **Earth's Moon** — canonically the Furling Scout sealed dormant on the Moonbase per the canonical SC2 callforward in `furling-artifacts-and-callforwards.md`

These Tier 3 entries can be authored in a follow-up pass without blocking the slice.
