# The Selvenne — *The Long-Memories* (planet-scale coral hive-mind; experiential archive)

> nervous system.* The Selvenne don't store data; they store qualia.**

## The Concept

The Selvenne are a planet-scale collective intelligence. The species *is*
a coral reef — a single distributed organism covering most of the
seabed of an ocean world (canonically **Vellumar** in the slice
cluster). Each polyp is ~5cm; each polyp holds approximately 1
Furling year of *experiential memory* — not facts, not data, but
the *qualia* of a lived moment. The reef as a whole has continuous
self-memory back ~50 million years.

What makes them the slice's *Long-Memories*: when an intelligent
visitor *physically touches a polyp*, the polyp **plays back** its
stored experience into the visitor's nervous system. For 3-15
seconds the visitor *lives* an ancient memory — the feeling of a
prior-cycle aurorae aurora through Chenjesu-equivalent eyes, the
moment of a now-extinct species' first-contact ritual, the
helplessness of a previous-cycle Culling witnessed from a
shallow-water shore.

This is not data transfer. It is **experiential transfer**. The visitor
remembers it as if it happened to them. **The Selvenne are the slice's
emotional-archive species.** Where the Hider faction stores cognitive-
signature telemetry (empirical), the Selvenne store *what it felt like
to be those species* (subjective).

The Furlings collaborate with them to ensure every species that dies
in the Culling has at least one Selvenne polyp that *remembers* them
from the inside.

## Body Plan (the reef as species)

- **Form**: vast underwater coral reef structures across the Vellumar
  seabed. Various reef-architectures — fan-coral, brain-coral, tower-
  coral — each with distinct polyp morphology and memory specialty.
  Total reef coverage ~38% of Vellumar's seabed (rest is sand, kelp,
  volcanic vents).
- **Individual polyp**: ~5cm tall, soft-bodied, with retractable
  tentacle-cilia for water filtering and nutrient capture. Each polyp
  is bioluminescent at night — the reef glows in slow chromatic waves
  as memories migrate between polyps.
- **Memory architecture**: each polyp holds ~1 year of qualia. Polyps
  *exchange* memories continuously via chemical-cognition signaling
  through the water — every memory in the reef is *eventually*
  experienced by every polyp. (Slowly. On geological timescales.)
- **Lifespan**: individual polyp ~20 years. When a polyp dies, its
  stored memories disperse into the reef before the body decays. No
  memory is lost. The reef has continuous memory back ~50 million
  years.
- **Reproduction**: standard coral budding. New polyps inherit a
  randomly-chosen partial memory-pattern from the parent polyp,
  giving them an *experiential disposition* at birth without any
  specific memory content.

## Voice & Communication

- **The reef speaks as a chorus**. Many polyps vibrate water in unison
  to produce sound. To an underwater visitor it sounds like *every*
  polyp speaking at once in a layered harmonic chord. To a surface-
  dweller listening through Furling acoustic-translator equipment, the
  voice is rendered as a *layered chord of many overlapping voices,
  each carrying a slightly different emotional tone.*
- **Pronouns**: first-person plural ("we who remember," "the reef")
  always. The species does not have a concept of singular self.
- **Tone**: slow, *feels-rich*. The Selvenne communicate emotional
  texture more than facts. A Selvenne dialog about the Burvixese
  Caster activation is more like a *grief-tinted chord* than a
  report — the words convey the catastrophe, the harmonic timbre
  conveys what it felt like to remember.
- **Memory-playback**: when a polyp transmits experiential memory to
  a visitor (via touch), the *visitor's own voice* hears the memory.
  The experience is in first person.
- **Sample memory** (one a Steward might experience by touching a
  particular polyp):
  > *(You are submerged in warm magenta atmosphere. Your body is
  > buoyant. Your name is something like "Drift-Of-The-Dawn-Storm."
  > You are a Slylandro Observer from 3.4 million years ago, in a
  > prior cycle. The sky brightens — you sense something at the
  > edge of thought, a wrongness, a pressure where there should be
  > empty sky. You drift-think slower, hoping to slip beneath. The
  > pressure passes. You survive. You remember this for the reef
  > to hold.)*

## Faction Alignment

**Precursor (Stay-Go), with logistical complications.** The Selvenne
are *philosophically* aligned with the Migration (they want their
memories preserved, the Furlings can preserve them, Andromeda is
safe) — but they are **a coral reef**. Migration logistics are
non-trivial.

The Furlings' plan: physically lift large sections of the Vellumar
seabed in shielded water-tanks aboard heavy migration vessels. The
reef sections will be deposited on a similar ocean world in
Andromeda. **The migration of the Selvenne is the single largest
logistical operation in the Migration timetable.** It takes more
ships than any other species, more time, more energy, more risk —
and is canonically successful at the cost of *some* reef damage.

**Terminal: Migrated** (with cosmetic ~5% memory loss in transit;
the reef itself records this loss in subsequent millennia).

## In the Slice

**Encounter location**: Vellumar (ocean world in slice cluster).
The Steward descends in a Furling submarine (loaned for the
encounter) to the principal reef-architecture, the **Brain-Coral
Sanctum**.

**Encounter shape**: the Steward lands on a submarine platform
adjacent to the Sanctum; the reef's chorus begins (a vast layered
welcome that takes minutes); a host-polyp emerges from the
substrate and *extends* a tentacle-cilium toward the Steward's
exposed hand. Touching it triggers the first memory-playback.

**What they want**: experiential contributions. The Steward's own
lived memories of slice encounters — the moment of the Distress
Beacon screening, the contemplation-cove at Taalo's Stone, the
Veils Falling ceremony, the Burvixese Caster activation. The
Steward contributes by *touching a recipient polyp* and *thinking
clearly about the memory* for ~30 seconds. The polyp records.

**What we want**:
- **Lived experiences of past species** (selected by the Selvenne
  based on the Steward's emotional readiness — they won't play a
  prior-cycle extinction memory to a Steward who hasn't yet
  faced the slice's own catastrophes)
- **Bio-Archive entries**: each memory played to the Steward
  becomes an Archive entry — *"You remember being a Slylandro of
  the prior cycle"* etc.
- **A unique reward**: an **Echo Crystal** — a small piece of the
  reef the Steward can carry. Touching the Echo Crystal lets the
  Steward re-experience any memory the reef has shared, on demand,
  for the rest of the slice.

**Quest** `selvenne_memory_archive` in `tools/quest_inventory.csv`:
branches contribute-faithfully (standard) / withhold (the reef
senses the omission; the chord-grief darkens further visits) /
Cleanser-pressure (Council asks whether the reef's experiential
broadcast might attract the Others — the Selvenne respond by
playing the Steward a memory of *being a prior-cycle Cleanser*;
the Steward feels what it was like; the request retracts).

## Ship — None

The Selvenne are sessile coral. They have no voidcraft. They are
transported by Furling migration vessels; they cannot themselves
travel. In Super-Melee they cannot appear.

## Authoring Notes

- **Their primary canonical NPC**: **Choir-Of-The-East-Reef** — the
  reef section the Steward dialogs with first. Like the Stelloth,
  the "name" applies to the *collective* — there isn't an individual
  polyp the Steward speaks with, but the chorus has a designation.
- **Memory-playback design constraint**: the slice should write 5-8
  canonical memories the Selvenne *can* play to the Steward.
  Suggested memories:
  1. A prior-cycle Slylandro surviving the previous Culling by
     drift-quieting (canonical confirmation that the doctrine has
     worked before)
  2. A prior-cycle Selvenne ancestor reef-section being touched by
     an unknown alien (the Steward feels what it is to be the reef
     during contact)
  3. A Furling Migration of a previous cycle that *failed* — a
     deep ancestral horror
  4. A moment from the Furling Hider faction's founding (deep-past
     Furling history, raw)
  5. A prior-cycle Mrokon-equivalent species firing their own
     Hammer-Of-Refusal (canonical thread: defiance is recurrent)
- **Bio-Archive integration**: each memory the Steward experiences
  adds an "Others — prior cycles" Archive entry to the player's
  log. This works perfectly with the existing Archive design and
  the Chenjesu prior-cycle canon (it's the same prior cycle the
  Chenjesu remember, but lived from *inside* the species who lived
  through it).
- **Cross-canon with Chenjesu**: the Selvenne and Chenjesu compare
  their prior-cycle memories. The Chenjesu remember as *witnesses*
  (rooted, slow, factual); the Selvenne remember as *participants*
  (qualia, subjective, emotional). The two species canonically
  exchange data — Furling Hider archive contains both.
