# The Migration Timetable

> Canonical phasing of the Furling Migration to Andromeda. Authored
> 2026-05-17 to fill the slice-canon gap: the slice's epilogue branches
> and *Migrated* terminal statuses repeatedly assume *some* sequence
> exists; this doc spells it out. Cross-references: the 18-species
> roster in [project_species_taxonomy_8plus8.md](../../memory/project_species_taxonomy_8plus8.md);
> per-species Migration outcomes in `tools/quest_inventory.csv`.

## Overview

The Migration is not a single event — it is a **multi-decade
phased relocation** of every Precursor-aligned species in the
slice cluster, plus the partial-migrant contingents from
Homesteader species who managed to evacuate. The phasing is
coordinated by the Furling Migration Council with **Kovellim
guidance** (they've done this seven times before; they know
the order).

**Hard deadline**: when the Others arrive in our cluster, the
Migration window closes. Species who have not departed by then
are committed to whatever Homesteader doctrine they chose
(Cloaked / Pre-sentient / Hidden / Eliminated / Pending).

**Crossing destination**: the Andromeda Galaxy (M31), reached
via the Rainbow Worlds dimensional crossing. The crossing is
canonically *difficult* — and the difficulty IS the buffer
against the Others (who, per Furling Hider research, have not
been detected in Andromeda).

## The Six Phases

### Phase 1 — Preparation (slice tutorial through Beat 4)

**Status**: starting; runs continuously throughout the slice
until the Culling.

- Furling Migration Council finalizes which species get lifts,
  on what timeline, with what resources
- Engineering for special-case species ramps up:
  - Selvenne tank-ships (water-tank reef-section transport)
  - Karavem perch-city evacuation cradles
  - Mmrnmhrm cognitive-patch supply chain
  - Slylandro atmospheric-lift design (if any Slylandro
    migrate — most go Cloaked instead)
- Stewards (the player) deployed to species to negotiate,
  gather data, and begin first-contact + ongoing diplomacy
- Furling Hider faction's archive grows continuously (Bio-
  Archive entries accumulate per the slice's quest outcomes)
- Council faction-standing votes happen throughout (Persuader
  / Cleanser / Defender / Denier / Hider alignments shift
  based on Steward choices)

### Phase 2 — Vanguard Migration (slice Beat 5 — early-mid)

**Status**: first species depart.

- **Kovellim Crossing-Frigates** depart first as Migration
  vanguard; their job is to *navigate ahead*, map the
  dimensional crossing route in real-time, and signal back to
  the cluster. Ovala-Eight-Crossings leads.
- **Karavem Aria-Skiffs** depart shortly after — light bodies,
  easy lifts, Migrating en route to pre-scouted Andromeda
  canyon-worlds. (The Kovellim have *promised* the Karavem
  specific resettlement canyons from their prior-cycle
  knowledge.)
- **Arilou** depart via their own path — Quasi-Space exit, not
  through the Rainbow Worlds crossing. Their *Hidden* terminal
  status reflects this alternate route. (The Steward's Sage's
  Gift quest is the Arilou's slice-canonical departure.)
- **Furling Persuader vanguard ships** depart alongside the
  Kovellim — Furling Persuader-faction families with multi-
  generational Migration plans go first to establish footholds.

### Phase 3 — Logistical Migration (slice Beat 6 — mid)

**Status**: the heaviest lifts.

- **Selvenne reef-section transport** begins — Furling-built
  shielded water-tank vessels physically lift sections of
  Vellumar's seabed; the largest single logistical operation
  in the Migration timetable. Takes more ships, more time,
  more energy than any other species combined. Some reef-
  damage expected; canonically ~5% memory loss occurs in
  transit, which the reef itself records in subsequent
  millennia.
- **Stelloth Three-Voice Arc** departs from its super-giant
  trading post, carrying the *artifact archive* it has
  collected from slice species — Lemmkin papers, Burvixese
  audio recordings, Utwig pre-doctrine voice samples,
  Mrokon Hammer-Round telemetry, Taalo Shield-fragments
  (depending on the Steward's slice choices).
- **Melnorme** depart from their super-giants. The schism
  between leaver-and-stay-behind Drahn-loyalists plays out
  *during* this phase; canonically **Drahn itself is destroyed
  by the Others during this window**, traumatizing the
  surviving Melnorme into their canonical SC2-era permanent-
  nomad status.

### Phase 3.5 — **The Fall of Mh-Lai** (mid-slice forced-acceleration event)

**Status**: catastrophe; everything compresses.

The Others arrive **6 months earlier than the Hider faction had
estimated** — they followed a Hider-faction research probe back to
its origin (Mh-Lai). The Furling Council is in session at Mh-Lai
when the dimensional signature crosses the cluster halo. The
Steward is somewhere else in the cluster and cannot reach Mh-Lai
in time to do more than choose how much they witness.

**Mh-Lai falls.** The Furling home is consumed. The Council
chamber's data archives go dark with their occupants. The Migration
vessels in standby orbit pull away carrying what they could —
typically ~30% of the Council if the Steward led an evacuation, far
less otherwise. The Persuader-faction commander (Halia) lives,
dies, or is politically captured by the Cleansers based on player
choices.

**Aftermath effects on the Migration timetable**:
- **The Migration accelerates.** Phase 4 (Last-Wave) effectively
  *begins now* — every remaining Precursor-aligned species must
  evacuate as quickly as possible. Quests with longer timelines
  compress.
- **The Migration flagship Hearth-of-Iron becomes the new home base**
  — all Mh-Lai-only services (Trade, Customization, Bio-Archive,
  tier-1+ install-gate) transfer to Hearth-of-Iron. Mev-Tar Lwen-Tar
  (Yelena's mother) moves the Unzervalt tooling there.
- **The slice's emotional register shifts permanently.** From this
  point on, the Steward leads from grief.

See [the-fall-of-mh-lai.md](the-fall-of-mh-lai.md) for the full
scene canon + quest FSM in `tools/quest_inventory.csv`.

### Phase 4 — Last-Wave Migration (slice Beat 7 — late-mid through climax)

**Status**: final departures.

- **Androsynth Refugees** depart — Coel Tessar's contingent
  crosses with the Migration; the **Distress Beacon** travels
  with them and is preserved as the canonical Furling Archive
  evidence of the Others' decursion attack
- **Mmrnmhrm Sentinels** depart after accepting the Bio-
  Architect-derived neural-substrate patch (in the standard
  quest branch). They will continue defending their First-
  Makers' empty homeworld during the crossing, then settle in
  Andromeda. The Sentinel-Logic-Cohort-Forty-Seven-Theta is
  among them.
- **Burvixese migrant contingent** (only if the Steward
  advocated pre-Caster evacuation): a small group reaches
  Andromeda before the Caster fires. Their diaspora is the
  Burvixese's only future.
- **Utwig migrant contingent** (only if the Steward advocated
  to pre-doctrine youth): unmasked Utwig who refused the Veils
  Falling depart with the Migration.
- **Lemmkin migrant contingent** (only if the Steward
  advocated to "the quiet ones"): Trill and ~12 archivists
  cross with the Migration, carrying the most complete
  Lemmkin archive to Andromeda.
- **Mrokon Operator migrant contingent** (only if the Steward
  arranged a lift in the partial-evac branch): young
  Operators not yet bonded to their first puppet — they cross
  with the Migration and may someday fire a second Hammer-Of-
  Refusal in the neighboring galaxy.
- **Remaining Furling Migration Council** departs in this phase
- Stewards (player) depart at the latest possible moment if
  they wish to be present for the Culling beats

### Phase 5 — Final Acts on the Stay Side (slice Beat 7-8)

**Status**: the species who chose to stay execute their
doctrines as the Others approach. The Migration is in transit;
this is what happens behind it.

- **Taalo Shield** activation (per the Steward's help-level)
- **Burvixese Caster** harmonic activation (their last-bid
  doctrine ignites; the Others arrive moth-to-flame)
- **Utwig Veils Falling** ceremony completes (cognitive load
  drops them below threshold; doctrine works comically)
- **Lemmkin archive distribution** completes (their pre-Culling
  research dispersal — every cluster gets a Lemmkin archive copy)
- **Mrokon Hammer-Of-Refusal** fires (every Operator on Mrokon's
  Stand synchronizes; tens of thousands of kinetic-impact rounds
  strike the Others simultaneously; the Others are *marked*)
- **Thinn edge-align** doctrine activates (ribbon-bodies align;
  status *Pending*)
- **Slylandro Cloaking Satellite** (if installed) becomes
  permanent; they survive the Others' passage
- **Chenjesu** remain rooted; below threshold by biology;
  survive
- **Furling Defenders** make their final stand with the
  Sa-Matra super-weapon if it's completed; canonically the
  Sa-Matra is *not enough* and most Defenders die; the
  surviving few are absorbed into SC2-era proto-Ur-Quan
  conscript culture (deep callforward)

### Phase 5.5 — **The Final Conflict** (Sa-Matra Standoff)

**Status**: the slice's climactic battle.

The Migration fleet completes formation at the Rainbow Worlds
arrow-tip. The dimensional crossing is moments from opening.
**Admiral Drev-Tok Velt-Mar's Homesteader coalition** — Furling
Defenders + Sa-Matra Prototype + Mrokon Hammer-Ship remnants +
Burvixese Broadcaster-modified vessels + Thinn troupe holdouts +
Cleanser hardliners — emerges from the cluster-edge between the
Migration and the crossing point.

The negotiation attempt:
- **Diplomatic rare** (Council-sanctioned Hijacked Vessel + Persuader ≥ 10 + Halia alive free): Drev-Tok sees the marked Others' Vessel and converts; doctrine reversed; he joins the Migration
- **Persuader-symbolic** (Persuader ≥ 8 + Halia alive free): one volley exchanged for honor; Drev-Tok stays in galaxy with honor guard
- **Combat victory** (default): super-melee fleet-vs-fleet; 3-phase Sa-Matra Prototype boss fight; Drev-Tok dies with his ship; Migration completes

See [the-final-conflict.md](the-final-conflict.md) for the full scene canon + quest FSM in `tools/quest_inventory.csv`.

### Phase 6 — The Culling (slice's climactic beat)

**Status**: the Others arrive.

- All Homesteader doctrines are tested in real-time
- Witnessing-Stewards are present for the species's last
  moments (per branch outcomes)
- The Migration vessels are well outside the cluster by now,
  approaching the Rainbow Worlds crossing
- The slice's climactic narrative beats happen here — the
  Steward witnesses, mourns, departs

### Phase 7 — Epilogue (Andromeda arrival)

**Status**: the Migration vessels arrive in Andromeda; resettlement
begins.

- Kovellim-scouted resettlement sites accept each species:
  - Karavem to canyon-worlds with the right acoustics
  - Selvenne to ocean-worlds with similar reef-conditions
  - Furlings to Furling-suitable terrestrial worlds
  - Androsynth alongside Furling enclaves (they're already
    used to clone-human / Furling cohabitation)
  - Mmrnmhrm to a defensible system (their doctrine continues —
    they will defend the new Furling homeworld now)
- The Stelloth distribute their artifact archive to the
  Andromeda-based galactic culture; Lemmkin archives are read;
  Burvixese recordings are studied; the slice's species
  *continue* in memory even when they couldn't continue in body
- The Kovellim record this as their **eighth crossing**.
  Ovala becomes Ovala-Nine-Crossings; her cloak gains its
  ninth thread; her daughter begins weaving the family
  genealogy entry
- Far-future: when the Others arrive in Andromeda (which they
  will, eventually, per the cycle), the Migration plans for a
  *ninth* — and the Kovellim are still around to lead it.

## Migration Lift Types (canonical vessel classes)

| Lift Type | Owner | Capacity | Used by |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Furling Migration Lift** | Furling Persuader Council | ~10,000 bipedal-humanoid bodies + cargo | Most species; biological default |
| **Kovellim Crossing-Frigate** | Kovellim | ~500 + vessel-as-vanguard role | Kovellim themselves; navigation vanguard |
| **Stelloth Three-Voice Arc** | Stelloth chord-fleet | ~200 chords + the artifact archive | Stelloth + their cultural cargo |
| **Selvenne Tank-Ship** *(Furling-built specialty)* | Furling Hider Council | ~1 reef-section per ship | Selvenne (multiple tank-ships per reef) |
| **Karavem Aria-Skiff** *(small private vessels)* | Karavem | ~10 individuals + provisions | Karavem; light and elegant |
| **Mmrnmhrm Sentinel-Loop Cradle** *(self-built)* | Mmrnmhrm | one Sentinel-cohort each | Mmrnmhrm self-transport |
| **Quasi-Space portal** (Sage's gift) | Arilou (via the Sage) | Arilou-class only | Arilou separate route |
| **Hijacked Others' Vessel** *(slice-rare; one-off if quest completes)* | Furling Defender renegade or Steward-specific | TBD | Special hijack quest content |

## How to apply (future sessions)

- When a species's quest outcome is *Migrated*, the timetable above gives the canonical phase they depart in
- When a species's outcome is *MixedEliminatedMigrated*, the migrant contingent rides the relevant phase's lift; the majority dies in Phase 5/6
- When asked "when does X migrate?": consult the phase tables above
- When asked "how do they migrate?": consult the lift-type table
- When designing slice-epilogue cinematics: the phases give natural cinematic beats — vanguard departure (hopeful), logistical heavy-lifts (anxious), last-wave (urgent), the Culling (tragic), arrival (bittersweet)
- The hard deadline (Others' arrival) is the slice's narrative pressure cooker; the Migration timetable's pace is set by *fear of missing the deadline*
