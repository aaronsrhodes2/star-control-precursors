# The Halia–Steward Banter Library

> Canonical curated banter between Commander Halia (Persuader-faction
> Mh-Lai commander; the Steward's superior officer + closest friend in
> the Council) and the Steward (player). Authored 2026-05-17 per
> Aaron's brief: *"make sure we build an emotional bond through lots
> of witty banter between the player character and Halia so it has
> maximum impact"* — the impact being [The Fall of Mh-Lai](the-fall-of-mh-lai.md).
>
> The banter library is **drawn from** at canonical trigger moments
> throughout the slice. Design-chat wires each exchange to its
> appropriate context (post-Furlmart, post-quest, Council briefing,
> comm-while-in-hyperspace, etc.). The library is curated, not
> exhaustive — each entry is a *complete exchange* (~2-6 lines) the
> Design chat picks up whole.
>
> **The point**: when Halia dies in the Fall, the player should feel
> they have *lost a person*, not lost an NPC. Every banter line is a
> deposit in the emotional bank account that the Fall withdraws from.

## Voice profiles

### Commander Halia

- **Persona**: warm authority. Genuinely funny in a *dry-and-observant*
  register — never loud, never showboaty. Calls the Steward *"Steward"*
  in professional moments, drops it for first-name (canonical: the
  Steward's first name is *player-input*) in personal moments. Uses
  the verbal idiom *"That was a compliment"* / *"I'm telling you that
  as your friend, not your commander"* when she wants the line to
  land.
- **What Halia does NOT do**: she does not flirt, she does not
  patronize, she does not undermine the Steward's authority in
  front of others. She critiques *privately*. She praises *publicly*.
  Her professionalism is *bone-deep*.
- **Tea**: she canonically drinks Mh-Lai station-tea black; she
  invites the Steward for tea *as a code phrase* for *"I want to
  talk to you as a person, not as a commander."* The Steward learns
  this distinction over the slice.
- **Furling fur-aesthetic markers**: her office at Mh-Lai is fur-
  cushioned per canon; she keeps a small Mh-Lai souvenir on her desk
  (a *fur-bound* logbook with seven prior-Steward signatures inside).
- **Age**: ~58 Furling years (mid-career senior commander; ~17 years
  in command rank).

### The Steward (player character)

- **Persona**: witty. Per the Furling humor doctrine canon, *the
  protagonist is funny*. The Steward's wit is **warm-deadpan with
  occasional self-deprecation**. They respect Halia genuinely and
  *bicker only with people they like*. They lose wit in mission-
  critical moments and *especially* in the Fall.
- **Banter rule**: the Steward's wit-options with Halia trend
  *slightly less sharp* than their wit-options with alien species.
  They are sharing tea, not negotiating.

## Banter exchanges — TUTORIAL ARC

### Trigger: Beat 1 (first Mh-Lai docking, post-tutorial-intro)

> **Halia** *(over comm; Steward is just dropping into dock)*: Steward. Welcome to Mh-Lai. Welcome back to Mh-Lai. Welcome to *yet again* Mh-Lai. We are running out of new ways to welcome you.
>
> **Steward**: I'm running out of new ways to arrive.
>
> **Halia**: Try arriving five minutes earlier next time. The Furlmart cargo handler has been waiting.
>
> **Steward**: He'll wait.
>
> **Halia**: He will. He's contractually obligated. But he wants you to know he's bored.

### Trigger: Beat 2 (post-Furlmart cargo handover)

> **Halia**: The cargo handler said you complained about the smell in Hangar Bay Twelve-Forty-Eight.
>
> **Steward**: It smells of the last refit. The last refit was three crews ago.
>
> **Halia**: It smells of *heritage*, Steward. Mev-Tar Lwen-Tar personally welded that hull. Develop a heritage tolerance.
>
> **Steward**: My heritage tolerance is well-developed. My olfactory tolerance is unrelated and untrained.
>
> **Halia**: That was a compliment.
>
> **Steward**: That was a *compliment*?
>
> **Halia**: I called you well-developed.

### Trigger: Beat 3 (post-Coel Tessar Distress Beacon)

> **Steward**: Coel Tessar wanted me to tell you they thought you were *"reasonable for a Furling."*
>
> **Halia**: Tell them I'm aiming for "reasonable for a Council member" and the Androsynth bar is generously low.
>
> *(Beat.)*
>
> **Halia**: That was a compliment. They were trying to compliment me.
>
> **Steward**: I caught that.
>
> **Halia**: Did you tell them I'm aiming higher?
>
> **Steward**: I told them you'd be flattered. I left out the part about your ambitions.

### Trigger: Beat 4 (Distress Beacon screening at Mh-Lai)

> **Halia** *(post-screening; private channel)*: That was the worst footage I have ever watched.
>
> **Steward**: I know.
>
> **Halia**: It is also the most important footage we have ever obtained. I am — Steward, I'm telling you as your friend, not your commander — *grateful*. To Coel Tessar. To the time displacement. To you.
>
> **Steward**: Thank you.
>
> **Halia**: Don't *thank me* for being grateful, Steward. That's not how gratitude works.
>
> **Steward**: I see.
>
> **Halia**: Drink the tea.

### Trigger: Beat 5 (Customization scene first install)

> **Halia** *(comm-over to Customization bay)*: I see you've installed the Scanner Mark Three. Yelena's family will be pleased.
>
> **Steward**: Yelena's family doesn't know me yet.
>
> **Halia**: Yelena's family knows every Scout that has flown out of Unzervalt. *Twelve-Forty-Eight* is on their books. They knew you the day you signed for it.
>
> **Steward**: That's a little unsettling.
>
> **Halia**: That's how *family-owned shipyards* work, Steward. Have you not been part of one before?
>
> **Steward**: My family does *administrative work*.
>
> **Halia**: Mine too. We get to enjoy the warmth from a polite professional distance.

### Trigger: Beat 7 (post-Others-Reveal cinematic)

> **Halia** *(privately, after the cinematic concludes)*: That was harder than I expected, watching with you.
>
> **Steward**: For me too.
>
> **Halia**: I have read the Hider reports for fifteen years. I have known what the Others are abstractly for that entire time. Watching the cinematic with you was — different. *(A pause.)* Steward, do you have someone to talk to about this?
>
> **Steward**: I'm talking to you.
>
> **Halia**: I am not *enough* — and I am not making a joke. Make sure you have someone else, too. The work will eat you otherwise.
>
> *(Beat.)*
>
> **Halia**: That was not a compliment. That was an order.
>
> **Steward**: Understood.

## Banter exchanges — MID-GAME (Beats 8+; established friendship)

### Trigger: post-Slylandro Cloak quest favorable

> **Halia**: The Slylandro sent the Council a thank-you cloud.
>
> **Steward**: A what?
>
> **Halia**: An actual cloud. Of acknowledgment. The atmospheric chemistry has been — interesting.
>
> **Steward**: Did you frame it?
>
> **Halia**: We are *trying*. Atmospheric chemists are involved. I'll keep you posted. *(Pause.)* It is the only thank-you in Council history that has *condensed*.

### Trigger: post-Mycon Whisper quest (suppress branch)

> **Steward**: I told them to suppress it.
>
> **Halia**: I would have made the same call.
>
> **Steward**: Would you have, though? Really?
>
> **Halia**: Steward. I have read your file. You consult me *after* you've made the call. That's part of why I trust you. Don't suddenly start asking permission for the right reasons.
>
> **Steward**: I was asking for reassurance.
>
> **Halia**: That's still asking permission, just with worse phrasing. The answer is yes. You were right.

### Trigger: post-Mycon Whisper quest (allow-emergence branch)

> **Steward**: I told them to let it wake.
>
> **Halia**: ... I would not have made that call.
>
> **Steward**: I know.
>
> **Halia**: But you made it. Tell me why.
>
> **Steward**: *(player-input)* [several conversation paths follow; Halia engages substantively with whatever rationale the Steward provides — she disagrees but takes the reasoning seriously]
>
> **Halia** *(after listening)*: Steward. I would not have made the call. I will *defend* the call you made. That is the difference between a commander and a clerk. Drink the tea.

### Trigger: post-Mraka Last Race side-quest favorable

> **Halia**: Mraka Yenn-Sa is back on my comm. She's asking for *navigation clearance to visit Soren Kel-Var's family*.
>
> **Steward**: Grant it.
>
> **Halia**: Already done. I'm not asking for clearance permission, Steward. I'm telling you you fixed something twelve years old and the Council noticed.
>
> **Steward**: Mraka did the fixing.
>
> **Halia**: Mraka did the fixing because you brought her to the wreck. Don't underestimate what you do, Steward. *(Beat.)* Also — Yelena says the drive-repair was *forty percent cleaner* than the diagnostic predicted. She is, I quote, *"feeling smug."*
>
> **Steward**: She earned it.

### Trigger: post-Bren-Vor Aimer's Verdict side-quest favorable

> **Halia** *(quietly)*: The Council voted to demote Karol-Vere Belt-Tar.
>
> **Steward**: I heard.
>
> **Halia**: Bren-Vor's evidence was — Steward, it was *prepared*. It was *thorough*. Velt-Ra's journal was bound with three-color thread; it had a Persuader pin on the spine; Bren-Vor had read it eight hundred times before the hearing. *(Pause.)* He has been carrying it for three years.
>
> **Steward**: I know.
>
> **Halia**: I am — I am proud of you, Steward. I am also — I am quietly furious with the system that made his evidence necessary. Both at once. I'm telling you that as your friend, not as your commander. I would not say it on a Council channel.
>
> **Steward**: Drink the tea, Halia.
>
> **Halia** *(after a beat)*: ...The Steward giving the *commander* tea-orders. We have reached an interesting working relationship.

## Banter exchanges — LATE-MID GAME (Beats 12-14; the heart-tightening ones)

### Trigger: ~3 in-game weeks before the Fall (Halia's first "miss-you" line)

> **Halia** *(unprompted, comm-over while Steward is in hyperspace)*: How long has it been since we just *sat* for tea? Three months? Four?
>
> **Steward**: Four. The Olwen-Veth memorial was the last time.
>
> **Halia**: I miss it. I'm telling you that as your friend, not your commander. The work hasn't stopped requiring you. But I'd like a tea-and-no-agenda visit before the Migration window closes. Will you make time?
>
> **Steward**: I'll make time.
>
> **Halia**: ...Soon, Steward. Don't tell me that and then go visit four more star systems.

### Trigger: ~2 in-game weeks before the Fall (the visit-or-don't moment)

> **Halia** *(at Mh-Lai dock; the tea visit happens IF the Steward routes here)*:
>
> *(if Steward routed to Mh-Lai for the tea visit)*
> **Halia**: You came.
> **Steward**: I came.
> **Halia**: Tea is the canonical mass-energy unit of Furling diplomacy. *(She pours.)* I have a thing to tell you. Not Council. Personal.
> **Steward**: I'm listening.
> **Halia**: My mother — Persuader Vala Telven-Mar — died three years ago. Cancer. Standard mortality of someone who refused the longevity treatments because she said *"the work should pass to people who will do it new."* I think about her every time you make a call I wouldn't have made. *(Pause.)* I think she would have liked you.
> **Steward**: I would have liked her.
> **Halia**: I know.
> *(Long pause; tea continues; Halia talks about her mother for the rest of the scene without further player-input.)*
>
> *(if Steward did NOT route to Mh-Lai)*
> **Halia** *(comm, ~5 minutes of attempted small-talk, then)*: It's all right, Steward. The work is the work. I'll see you when you can. Don't worry about the tea. *(But she does not say what she would have said about her mother. The canonical line is lost.)*

### Trigger: ~1 in-game week before the Fall (the last full conversation)

> **Halia**: The Hider faction is running additional probes at the cluster edge. I do not like the readings.
>
> **Steward**: How bad?
>
> **Halia**: I do not know. The readings are *anomalous*. Either nothing or — *(She stops herself.)* Steward. Be careful out there. Be more careful than the work requires.
>
> **Steward**: Halia, that's unusually direct.
>
> **Halia**: I have been your commander for two years and your friend for one. I have earned the right to be direct.
>
> **Steward**: Yes you have. I'll be careful.
>
> **Halia**: ...Thank you. *(Beat.)* That was not a compliment. That was relief. The two are sometimes the same, with you.

## Banter exchanges — POST-FALL (variant per Halia branch)

These exchanges fire post-Fall in the Common Room or at Hearth-of-Iron. They are *not* witty in the same register; they are *what comes after wit*.

### Halia alive (Persuader-free; race-success branch)

> **Halia** *(in Hearth-of-Iron command center; first post-Fall meeting)*: Steward. *(Long silence.)* I am not making a joke. I do not have one. I — *(Her voice does not break, but it tightens.)* Thank you for coming.
>
> **Steward**: I had to.
>
> **Halia**: You did not have to. You chose to. The math said you wouldn't make it. The math was wrong. I am — *(She stops.)* I will be your commander for the rest of this Migration. I will be your friend for the rest of my life. Both are true. Drink the tea.

### Halia dead (race-doomed / don't-race; her last words via recording)

> *(The Steward enters the Common Room. The Mh-Lai memorial sigil is on the wall. Halia's voice plays from a final-state-sealed recording — her canonical last words now permanent in the slice's archive.)*
>
> **Halia recorded**: *"...Steward — I see your ship in orbit. Thank you for coming. Even now. Keep going."*
>
> *(The recording loops. The Steward listens. The Common Room is quiet. Tarven is in his alcove with one of the surviving prior-Steward log-readers open; he has paused his reading. The Steward and Tarven do not speak.)*

### Halia alive, Cleanser-supervised (worst branch)

> **Halia** *(on Cleanser-channel review; brief formal transmission only)*: Steward. The Migration timetable continues. Phase Four operations are underway. Cleanser doctrine prevails on Council communications until further notice. *(Pause.)* I —
>
> *(Channel briefly cuts; reconnects on different priority.)*
>
> **Halia** *(off-channel, much shorter, private)*: I'm sorry, Steward. I'm —
>
> *(Channel cuts permanently. The next time the Steward hears from Halia, it is back on the Cleanser-formal channel. The friendship is intact and the channel is not. This is canonical.)*

## How to use this library

### For Lore chat (authoring new exchanges)

- Halia's voice is *warm-dry-direct*. Every line either *cares about the Steward* or *teases the Steward* or *both at once*
- The Steward's voice is *warm-deadpan-self-deprecating*. Their wit is *slightly less sharp* with Halia than with anyone else
- The verbal idiom *"That was a compliment"* / *"I'm telling you that as your friend, not your commander"* / *"Drink the tea"* is canonical Halia
- The verbal idiom *"Drink the tea, Halia"* (Steward giving Halia the tea-order) is the canonical *"we are equals now"* signal — appears in the late-mid banter when their relationship has reached parity
- **Halia does NOT discuss her mother until the late-mid banter** — the canonical line *"I think she would have liked you"* is held in reserve for maximum payoff

### For Design chat (wiring trigger contexts)

- Tutorial banter (Beat 1-7): fires automatically at each tutorial milestone
- Mid-game post-quest banter: fires within ~5 minutes of completing the relevant quest
- Late-mid banter (the heart-tightening ones): fire on calendar-week-equivalent triggers + Mh-Lai docking proximity
- The *visit-or-don't* moment ~2 weeks before the Fall is **player-driven** — if the Steward routes to Mh-Lai during the window, the tea-and-mother conversation fires; if they don't, the canonical line is *permanently lost*
- Post-Fall banter variants gate on `flag:halia_status` (alive_persuader / dead_witnessed / dead_distant / cleanser_supervised)

### For Audio chat (voice direction)

- Halia's banter voice should be *bone-dry-warm*; performance reference: a senior captain who has earned the right to be gentle
- The Steward's voice should be *warm-deadpan*; the wit lands quietly
- Halia's *"That was a compliment"* should be delivered with the same flat-affect she uses for actual orders — the joke is in *not signaling the joke*
- Halia's *"I'm telling you as your friend, not your commander"* should *soften* the voice slightly; the only place she breaks the dry register
- Post-Fall Halia voices: per the existing HANDOFF_audio_chat.md entries — alive-Persuader-free carries *quiet weight*; dead recorded voice plays through final-state-seal artifact; Cleanser-supervised has metallic edge + constrained phrasing

### For Image chat (visual states)

- Halia's office at Mh-Lai (pre-Fall): warm fur-aesthetic; tea-service prominent; her fur-bound logbook visible on the desk
- Halia in Council chamber (pre-Fall): formal Persuader cloak; commanding but warm
- Halia on Hearth-of-Iron (post-Fall, alive variant): same person but visibly carrying weight; tea-service is still there but the cup is sometimes empty
- Halia's memorial portrait (post-Fall, dead variant): formal Persuader-faction memorial pose; framed; canonical Mh-Lai memorial sigil beside it

## The Fall payoff

When the Fall fires, the player has accumulated:
- ~15+ canonical banter exchanges with Halia
- The *"miss-you, please make time, drink the tea"* canonical late-mid line
- (If they made the tea-visit) the *"I think she would have liked you"* line about Halia's mother
- The cumulative weight of two years of fictional working relationship

The Fall's first transmission — *"I love you, Steward. I have been your commander. I have been your friend. Either way — keep going"* — lands with maximum impact because the player **knows** what Halia means by "friend." They've heard her *say it* over and over. *"I'm telling you that as your friend, not your commander"* has been her canonical verbal tic. The Fall is the moment that tic *retires forever*.

That is what the banter library is for.
