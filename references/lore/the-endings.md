# The Endings — Slice Resolution Tier System

> Authored 2026-05-17 per Aaron's brief: 6 canonical ending tiers
> from **Best** to **Disastrous**, gated on ally count + crewmate count
> + side-quest completion. The slice has multiple endings; the player's
> cumulative choices across the slice determine which one fires.
>
> The endings are the slice's *true* terminal beat — even The Final
> Conflict's outcome branches feed into the endings system. The
> Crossing-Opens cinematic plays for the *good* endings; the
> *Unsuccessful* and *Disastrous* endings have their own distinct
> cinematic and music packages.

## The Six Endings

### 🌟 Best — *"The Quiet Resolution, Honored Fully"*

**Conditions**:
- **ALL allies** — 16/16 slice species reach non-Eliminated terminal status (every Precursor species Migrated; every Homesteader species Cloaked / Pre-sentient / Hidden / MixedEliminatedMigrated-with-large-diaspora / Pending-favorable)
- **ALL crewmates** — 5/5 named crew aboard (the weapons-officer slot counts whether filled by Bren-Vor OR Forward; both qualify)
- **ALL side-quests** — every crew side-quest resolved favorably; the Hijack quest completed Council-sanctioned; The Final Conflict resolved diplomatically OR combat-victory with minimal Migration-fleet losses

**Tone**: triumphant-but-earned. The slice's purest payoff.

**Cinematic**: Migration crossing opens; the full fleet enters in canonical order; every named NPC is alive or canonically-honored; the Steward enters last on a bridge full of friends; Halia stands beside them (if alive) or her memorial sigil glows on the bridge (if dead — only race-success or canonical honor-protocol branches qualify for Best); the Common Room bond-objects are *all visible* — the most accumulated state possible; in Andromeda, the canonical resettlement is shown: Kovellim records this as their 9th crossing; Karavem perch-cities in new canyons; Selvenne reef intact; the Lemmkin archive being unpacked; Stelloth artifact-archive accepted by the post-Migration galactic culture; the Mrokon memorial inscribed (their dead are *named in Andromeda*); Sevreth's Child humming peacefully in Mira-Rou's medbay; Forward + his 2-Thinn diaspora arguing cheerfully about doctrine in the Common Room.

**Final canonical line** *(Steward narration, slice's last text)*: *"The Quiet Resolution. The bookkeeping is the dignity. The dignity is the work. We continue."*

### 🥇 Great — *"The Quiet Resolution, Honored"*

**Conditions**:
- **ALL allies** — 16/16 species saved
- **ALL crewmates** — 5/5 aboard
- *Side-quests partial* — some crew side-quests skipped or declined

**Tone**: triumphant-but-incomplete. The slice's high-payoff outcome with some personal threads unfinished.

**Cinematic**: same Migration crossing structure as Best, but the Common Room bond-object accumulation is partial; some crew side-quest payoffs visibly missing; the canonical Andromeda resettlement happens but some crew arcs are *unfinished* (e.g., if Mraka's Last Race wasn't completed, her Soren-reconciliation is canonically absent from the epilogue).

**Final canonical line**: *"The work was good. Some of it was not done. We continue."*

### 🥈 Good — *"The Quiet Resolution, Partially Honored"*

**Conditions**:
- **ALL allies** — 16/16 species saved
- **SOME crewmates** — 1-4 crew aboard

**Tone**: bittersweet success. The galaxy is saved but the Steward's *personal* community is partial.

**Cinematic**: Migration crossing opens; full alien-species fleet enters; but the Common Room's named-crew alcoves are partially empty — the canonical visual of *"the home you arrive with is not the home you could have had."* Andromeda resettlement happens but the Steward is canonically *lonelier* than the Best/Great endings.

**Final canonical line**: *"The galaxy was the work. The galaxy made it. The home was also the work. The home is smaller than I hoped."*

### ⚖️ Successful, at a cost — *"The Quiet Resolution, Roughly"*

**Conditions**:
- **SOME allies** — 8-15 species saved (at least half)
- **SOME crewmates** — 1-4 crew aboard

**Tone**: pragmatic-bittersweet. The Migration completes but with visible casualties; the dignity is qualified.

**Cinematic**: the Migration crossing opens but the fleet is visibly *thinner* — some species's expected vessels never arrived; the Selvenne reef may have lost sections in transit; the Karavem perch-city slots assigned for Andromeda have empty entries; canonical *"some of us did not make it"* register; the Steward enters last on a bridge that is professional-not-warm.

**Final canonical line**: *"The work was the work. We did what we could. Some did not arrive. The dignity is in not pretending otherwise."*

### 💀 Unsuccessful — *"The Steward Escapes; The Galaxy Is Consumed"*

**Conditions**:
- **FEW allies** (1-7 species saved) *OR* **FEW crewmates** (0-1 crew aboard)

**Tone**: tragic + survivor's-guilt. The Steward lives but the galaxy does not.

**Canonical sequence**:

The Migration fleet attempts to assemble at the Rainbow Worlds but is too sparse + too disorganized to complete formation in time. Drev-Tok's coalition is unopposed at the arrow-tip; without enough Migration-fleet support, the Steward cannot even engage the Final Conflict properly. The Others arrive *during* the assembly.

**The Steward escapes alone** through the crossing — Time Drive at maximum charge; one ship through the dimensional fold; the corridor closes behind them. They emerge in Andromeda. They are *the only Furling there*.

**The Others, satisfied with the easy meal of an unprotected galaxy, decide they like the taste.** They do not move on to the next galaxy as they have in prior cycles. **They stay. They consume the galaxy entirely — every species, every world, every cognitive signature, every echo of the Furling civilization. They will consume this galaxy *forever*, treating it as a permanent feeding-ground.** No species ever evolves to sapience in this galaxy again, because the Others maintain it as their permanent garden.

The SC2-era canon-impact: in this branch, the SC2-era galaxy *does not exist*. Captain Zelnick is never born. The Slylandro never become the SC2-era Cloaked species. Earth is consumed in the cycle's deep past. The Migration was the *only* path, and it failed; this galaxy's future is *eaten*.

**Final canonical line** *(Steward narration, alone in Andromeda)*: *"I am the only one. The galaxy is the kitchen now. I will tell Andromeda what we were. I will hope no one comes back."*

### ☠️ Disastrous — *"The Treaty Signed In Steward Blood"*

**Conditions**:
- **NO allies** — 0 species saved (every species Eliminated)
- **NO crewmates** — 0 crew aboard

**Tone**: absurdist-horror with Furling humor doctrine breaking down. The slice's darkest possible outcome, played as *dark comedy*.

**Canonical sequence**:

The Steward arrives at the Rainbow Worlds arrow-tip *alone*. No Migration fleet has assembled. No allies arrive. No crew aboard. The Others arrive on schedule.

The Steward, having spent two years executing the Quiet Resolution mandate so badly that *every* species reached the Eliminated terminal status, has lost the ability to recognize the situation. They open a Council-channel broadcast — the one Halia trained them to use — and *attempt to negotiate*.

**The Others do not negotiate.** They never have. Their canon is consumption, not diplomacy. But the Steward, alone, does not know this. They draft a treaty unilaterally — *"the Galaxy of [name] hereby cedes all sovereignty, all sentient and pre-sentient species, all worlds, all cognitive signatures, and all future evolutionary trajectories to the Others, in perpetuity, in exchange for the Steward's safe passage and the right to be remembered."* They transmit it.

**The Others approach the Steward's ship.** They consume it. They consume the Steward. They use the Steward's blood — canonically and literally — to *imprint the treaty onto the substrate of the galaxy itself*. The galaxy is now, *by Steward decree and Others' acceptance*, *officially surrendered*. The treaty is binding.

**The Others eat the galaxy at leisure**, treating the surrender-treaty as their canonical legal claim. The slice's narrator concludes:

> *"You led negotiations to hand over the galaxy to the Others, for all time, and then they ate you and used your blood to sign the treaty.*
>
> *Good job."*

The screen fades to black. No music. No Migration. No Andromeda. No Steward. Just the canonical wry-bitter Furling-humor-doctrine punchline acknowledging the player's catastrophic failure.

**Note**: this ending is canonically reachable only via *extreme* Cleanser-aligned play — every species cleansed; every crew rejected; every alliance refused. It is the slice's *intentional* punishment ending for players who pursue the *worst-possible-outcome strategy*. Aaron's framing: *"Good job."*

## Side-quest completion tracking (for Best/Great differentiation)

The slice tracks side-quest completion via these flags (suggested for Design):
- 5 crew recruitment quests (`recruited_<role>=True`)
- 5-6 crew side-quests (`<role>_sq_complete=True`)
- 16 species side-quests (each with their own complete-flag)
- The Hijack quest (`hijack_council_sanctioned=True` OR `others_vessel_destroyed=True`)
- The Final Conflict (`diplomatic_resolution=True` OR `combat_victory=True`)

**Best** requires ≥ 95% of all flags True with favorable values.
**Great** requires ≥ 85% with the same.
**Good** requires ≥ 70%.
**Successful, at a cost** requires ≥ 50%.

Design can tune the thresholds during playtest; these are the canonical *intent* numbers.

## Ending-evaluation timing

The ending fires:
- **Best / Great / Good / Successful, at a cost**: AFTER The Final Conflict resolves AND the Crossing-Opens cinematic plays; the ending-cinematic immediately follows
- **Unsuccessful**: when the Migration timeline reaches its hard deadline (Others' arrival in cluster) AND the Steward has FEW allies/crew — the Final Conflict either never triggers (no fleet to defend) or the player skips it; the Steward escape-cinematic fires
- **Disastrous**: when the same hard deadline is reached AND the Steward has 0 allies/0 crew — the negotiation cinematic fires; the dark-comedy punchline plays

## SC2-era canon-impact per ending

| Ending | SC2-era result |
|---|---|
| Best | SC2-era is canon — the Migration successful, the Furlings remembered, the prior-cycle species lineage continues; Captain Zelnick's era exists as canonical |
| Great | Same as Best |
| Good | Same as Best (the galaxy's future continues; crew arcs were just incomplete) |
| Successful, at a cost | SC2-era canon is preserved but with *fewer surviving artifacts*; some SC2-era references to Migration species are *partial* or *unsourced* |
| Unsuccessful | **SC2-era canon does not exist.** The galaxy is consumed forever; no species evolves; Earth is consumed in the deep past; Zelnick is never born. The slice retroactively erases the SC2 future. |
| Disastrous | Same as Unsuccessful PLUS the canonical Steward-treaty-signed-in-blood becomes the **canonical Others' legal claim** to the galaxy as a permanent feeding-ground. |

## How the endings frame Drev-Tok in retrospect

In the favorable endings (Best/Great/Good), Drev-Tok was *wrong* — the Migration worked; the species who couldn't follow were given dignified resolutions; the marking-technology proved out. In the Unsuccessful ending, Drev-Tok was *right* — the Migration failed; the galaxy is consumed; he died for nothing OR he died defending what was eaten anyway. The Disastrous ending makes Drev-Tok *retroactively correct in his grievance and tragically irrelevant* — he died fighting for a galaxy the Steward then surrendered.

**This is the slice's deepest moral structure**: the ending tier *retroactively redefines* whether the Persuader doctrine or the Defender doctrine was correct. The player learns *only at the end* which faction's history-record will be canonical.

| Best | Warm gold-cream + Andromeda-promise indigo | Crossing-Opens triumphal cue (slice's only major music) + warm crew-themes unified | *"We continue."* |
