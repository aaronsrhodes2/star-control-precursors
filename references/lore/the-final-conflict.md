# The Final Conflict — Sa-Matra Standoff at the Rainbow Worlds Arrow-Tip

> The slice's climactic battle. Authored 2026-05-17 per Aaron's brief:
> *"the remaining fleet of the Precursors of all the migrating species
> arrive at the point of the arrow of rainbow worlds to depart, but
> they are greeted by the Homesteaders led by your arch nemesis leader
> of the Homesteaders. Confrontation is a giant super-melee between
> the two fleets."*
>
> **Phase 5/6 of the [Migration Timetable](migration-timetable.md)** —
> the slice's last major beat before Phase 7 (Andromeda arrival). The
> Migration fleet has gathered at the arrow-tip; the dimensional
> crossing is moments from opening; and the arch-nemesis has assembled
> *the opposition*.

## Canonical premise

The Migration fleet has assembled at the geometric arrow-tip of the
Rainbow Worlds — the dimensional weak-point that opens the path to
Andromeda. Every Precursor-aligned species's vessels are in formation:

- **Furling Migration Lifts** (Persuader-faction main fleet; ~340 vessels)
- **Hearth-of-Iron** (Persuader flagship; Halia commands if alive)
- **Kovellim Crossing-Frigates** (vanguard; ~8 vessels; Ovala-Eight-Crossings leads dimensional-navigation)
- **Karavem Aria-Skiffs** (~120 vessels; singing in low harmonics)
- **Selvenne Tank-Ships** (~30 vessels; the largest single logistical operation; reef sections aboard)
- **Stelloth Three-Voice Arc** (artifact archive aboard)
- **Mmrnmhrm Sentinel-Loop Cradles** (with the cognitive patch applied)
- **Androsynth Refugee Fighters** (Coel Tessar's contingent)
- **Migrant contingents** from any Homesteader species the Steward
  rescued — Slylandro (if Migrated branch), Burvixese (if pre-Caster
  evac advocated), Utwig youth (if advocated), Lemmkin "quiet ones"
  (if advocated), Mrokon Operators (if partial-evac), Thinn diaspora
  (if Forward's confrontation-branch favorable)
- **The Steward's Furling Scout** (or the Hijacked Others' Vessel if
  acquired, which the fleet treats with deep ambivalence)

The Rainbow Resonator is in position. The Rainbow Worlds are in
geometric alignment. Kovellim navigation is computing the final fold-
vector for the crossing.

**And then the arch-nemesis arrives.**

## The Arch-Nemesis — Admiral Drev-Tok Velt-Mar

> **Canonical NPC: Admiral Drev-Tok Velt-Mar** — Furling Defender
> Commander; lifelong veteran of three Defender operations (canonically
> *"Three Hammers"* in his clan-suffix oral tradition); lead architect
> of the **Sa-Matra Prototype** super-weapon. ~70 Furling years old.
> **Has been a voice on Council channels throughout the slice** —
> never met in person before this scene — opposing every Persuader-
> aligned choice the Steward made. The Steward will recognize his
> voice immediately.

**Drev-Tok's doctrine**: the Migration is **preemptive surrender**.
The Furlings have the Sa-Matra prototype; they have Mrokon's mark-
the-Others technology (canonical, if Mrokon Hammer fired full); they
have prior-cycle Kovellim knowledge of dimensional-substrate weapons;
they have *everything they need to fight*. Leaving the galaxy is, in
his canonical phrase, *"abandoning the species who cannot follow."*

He believes:
- The Persuaders are cowards
- The Migration is a Furling betrayal of every Homesteader species
- The Sa-Matra Prototype, *firing in full Furling fleet coordination*, can mark
  the Others permanently — the Mrokon Hammer-Of-Refusal proved the
  Others *can be touched*; that single fact validates his entire
  doctrine
- If the Migration leaves, the Furlings will be remembered in the
  galactic record as *the ones who ran while the Others ate everyone
  else*

He is **wrong about the Sa-Matra working at scale**. The Furling Hider
faction has measured this; the Sa-Matra can mark *one* Vessel,
maybe two. The Others arrive in *thousands*. But Drev-Tok believes,
because believing is the only thing left to a Defender who has lost
his cohort to three prior operations and refuses to lose again.

He is also **genuinely furious with the Steward personally**. Every
Persuader success the Steward enabled — every species saved via
Cloak, every Migration-aligned Council vote, every Mh-Lai-channel
broadcast in favor of the Migration timetable — Drev-Tok has heard.
He has *known* the Steward's voice for two years. Now he has *seen*
their ship coming through the Rainbow Worlds arrow-tip.

He is *waiting* for them.

## Drev-Tok's coalition (the Homesteader fleet)

- **Furling Defender main force** — the Sa-Matra Prototype + ~80 Defender
  vessels (Defender Vessels per existing canon); Drev-Tok commands
- **Mrokon Hammer-Ships** — if the Mrokon Hammer-Of-Refusal fired-full
  branch was the Steward's choice, ~12 surviving Mrokon Hammer-Ships
  (Operators who weren't on the Hammer-firing roster) join Drev-Tok;
  canonical: *"if our Hammer marked the predator, we will mark the
  cowards too — but only mark; we will not kill Furlings"* (the Mrokon
  are warriors, not murderers; their fire is *symbolic* rather than
  lethal)
- **Burvixese Broadcaster-modified vessels** — if Burvixese sabotaged
  (Cleanser pre-emption) branch, a few remnant Broadcasters were
  retrofitted by Defender engineers; symbolic presence
- **Thinn troupe** — if Forward's confrontation branch favorable AND
  the troupe didn't migrate, a handful of troupe-members are present
  in their own ships, **edge-on of course** — visible from the
  Migration fleet's side as *faint chromatic slipstreams* curling
  around the battle space
- **Hardline Cleanser contingent** (if `cleanser_acceleration` was the
  Fall branch) — the Cleansers who captured Halia have joined
  Drev-Tok's coalition for the final stand; Halia is canonically
  aboard a Cleanser-supervised vessel HERE
- **Some surviving Slylandro** (if the Steward chose Eliminated for
  Slylandro) — bitter — small contingent in atmospheric-modified
  vessels (rare; bad-slice-outcomes only)

The coalition is *visually motley* — Furling Defender warship design
PLUS various species's vessels in formation. The Defenders are the
backbone; the others are *symbols of grievance*.

## The Scene — five beats

### Beat 1 — Arrival + Hail

The Migration fleet completes formation at the arrow-tip. Kovellim
nav computes 4 minutes 12 seconds to dimensional-fold readiness.
**Drev-Tok's fleet emerges from the cluster-edge** — a coordinated
warp-arrival that puts them between the Migration fleet and the
arrow-tip. They are *not running*; they are *standing in the way*.

Drev-Tok hails. **Channel-wide broadcast** — every vessel in both
fleets hears him.

> **Admiral Drev-Tok Velt-Mar, Sa-Matra Prototype command bridge,
> open Council-channel**:
>
> *"Steward. I have been waiting to meet you in person. *(A pause.)*
> Two years on the Council channels arguing against your every
> Migration vote. Two years watching you save Homesteader species
> while telling the Furlings to leave the rest behind. Two years of
> the Persuaders calling me 'difficult' and 'unhelpful' and 'a voice
> that does not understand the timetable.'*
>
> *I understand the timetable. I have always understood the
> timetable. The timetable is **preemptive surrender**. The timetable
> is **abandoning the species who cannot follow**. The timetable is
> **handing the Others a quiet galaxy** because *we did not have
> the will to face them*.*
>
> *Steward. I have the Sa-Matra Prototype. I have allies. I have
> the Mrokon's proof that the predator can be marked. I am not
> letting your fleet through this gate without a fight.*
>
> *Stand aside. Return to the cluster. Fight with us.*
>
> *Or come through me."*

### Beat 2 — The Negotiation Attempt

The Steward gets ONE chance to negotiate. The dialog FSM offers
several lines, gated on faction-standing and prior choices. Most
options FAIL — Drev-Tok's position is fundamental and he's been
waiting 2 years to hear it from the Steward in person, not to be
talked out of it.

**The negotiation outcomes**:

- **Default failure**: Drev-Tok rejects every argument; the super-
  melee begins
- **Very rare diplomatic success**: requires ALL of —
  - Persuader standing ≥ 10
  - The Distress Beacon has been delivered to Furling Hider archive
  - Halia is alive AND free (not Cleanser-supervised)
  - The Steward has the **Hijacked Others' Vessel** (Council-sanctioned
    branch)
  - At least 5 Homesteader species successfully Cloaked / Pre-sentient
  - The Steward speaks the canonical line: *"Admiral. Look at my ship."*
  → Drev-Tok sees the Others' Vessel. He sees the Mrokon marking-
  technology *already integrated* into a captured Vessel hull.
  Canonical line from Drev-Tok: *"...You did it. You **marked them
  back**. The Persuader path was the predator-marking path all along.
  *(Long silence.)* My doctrine was wrong. I will stand aside."*
  The Defender fleet retreats. Drev-Tok joins the Migration with
  Defender doctrine reframed as *"we marked them; that is enough;
  we will continue in Andromeda."*
- **Persuader-only success**: requires Persuader standing ≥ 8 + Halia
  alive AND free → partial diplomatic outcome — Drev-Tok agrees to a
  *symbolic clash* (one volley exchanged for honor; no real casualties
  on either side); the Migration proceeds; Drev-Tok stays in this
  galaxy with a smaller honor-guard. Bittersweet outcome.

### Beat 3 — The Super-Melee (if negotiation fails)

**Fleet-vs-fleet engagement** at the arrow-tip. The slice's largest
combat scene by an order of magnitude.

The Migration fleet has more ships but most are *non-combat* (Migration
Lifts, Selvenne Tank-Ships, Karavem Aria-Skiffs, Stelloth Three-Voice
Arc). The Defender fleet has fewer ships but most are *warships*.

The player commands their own ship (Furling Scout or Hijacked
Others' Vessel) plus directs the fleet via secondary commands:
- **Vanguard**: Kovellim Crossing-Frigates absorb damage with their
  Cycle-Fold special — they teleport back to defensive positions when
  hit hard
- **Glass cannons**: Androsynth Refugee Fighters + Thinn Blade (if
  Forward recruited; otherwise no Thinn ships)
- **Heavy line**: Furling Migration Lifts forming a *protective shell*
  around the Selvenne Tank-Ships (the Selvenne are the slowest +
  most-fragile-and-can't-fight)
- **Sniper**: Mrokon Hammer-Ship if Mrokon migrant Operators present;
  one Hammer-Round available for the Sa-Matra Prototype directly
- **Support**: Karavem Aria-Skiffs use Counterpoint Stitch to reverse
  Defender thrust vectors; Stelloth Three-Voice Arc binds Defender
  drive systems with Contract-Resonance Lance

**The Sa-Matra Prototype is the boss**. Multi-stage:
- Phase 1: heavy energy-lance volleys at Migration-fleet flagships
- Phase 2: *(at 50% hull)* the Prototype splits into a formation of
  3 sub-vessels (canonical Sa-Matra multi-vessel mode)
- Phase 3: *(at 25% hull)* the Prototype unifies for one final volley
  at the dimensional-crossing arrow-tip itself — Drev-Tok's last
  attempt is to *destroy the crossing* so no one Migrates

The player must:
- Survive Phase 1 (defensive piloting)
- Disrupt the Phase 2 formation (target the central coordination vessel)
- Stop the Phase 3 dimensional-crossing-shot in time

**The Hijacked Others' Vessel**, if equipped, is *uniquely capable*
in this fight — its Decursion weapon can briefly stutter the Sa-Matra's
firing solutions; this gives the Migration fleet a major edge.

**Crew active abilities pay off here** (their slice-long buildup):
- **Mraka's Evasive Burst** — escape Sa-Matra energy-lance volleys
- **Bren-Vor's Precision Focus** OR **Forward's Perpendicular Aim** —
  one-shot the central coordination vessel in Phase 2
- **Yelena's Field Overhaul** — one full repair during the fight (the
  ship cannot survive Phase 3 without it)
- **Mira-Rou's Bio-Signature Dampening** — reduces the Sa-Matra's
  lock-on duration on the player's ship
- **Tarven's Deep Archive Scan** — reveals the Sa-Matra's targeting
  pattern, allowing pre-positioning

Players who built bond with crew through side-quests are *substantially*
better-equipped for this fight. Players who skipped the side-quests
get a much harder version.

### Beat 4 — Drev-Tok's Last Stand

Regardless of negotiation success, there is a **canonical moment of
respect** when the Sa-Matra Prototype is defeated (combat path) OR
when Drev-Tok stands aside (diplomatic path).

**Combat path — Drev-Tok's final transmission**:

> *"Steward. *(His voice is broken but composed.)* You were the
> better tactician. I was wrong about the timetable. I was wrong
> about you. *(A pause.)* My Defender cohort died in three operations
> over twenty years. I have, today, joined them. Lead the Migration.
> Honor the species we leave behind. Honor the species we carry
> with us. Goodbye, Steward."*
>
> *(His Sa-Matra Prototype goes silent. He does not eject. He goes
> down with his ship.)*

**Diplomatic path — Drev-Tok's stand-aside transmission**:

> *"Steward. The galaxy will remember us — both of us — differently
> than I expected. Go. Pass through. We will fight from this side
> with the marking-technology you gave us. We have honor now. Go."*

### Beat 5 — The Crossing Opens

The Migration fleet completes formation. The Rainbow Resonator fires.
The Rainbow Worlds align. **The dimensional crossing opens** — a
shimmering iridescent corridor through which Andromeda is faintly
visible at the far end.

The Migration fleet enters the corridor in order:
- Kovellim Crossing-Frigates first (canonical — they always lead)
- Karavem Aria-Skiffs second (they sing the crossing-passage canonically)
- Selvenne Tank-Ships third (slowest; protected by Furling Lifts)
- Furling Migration Lifts main mass
- Migrant-contingent ships from rescued Homesteader species
- **The Steward's ship LAST** — Halia's canonical Persuader-Commander
  honor; the Steward enters the crossing last so they can *witness
  every other species cross safely*

(If Halia is alive: she stands beside the Steward on the bridge.
If she's dead: her memorial sigil is on the bridge.)

**The slice ends here.** Phase 7 (Andromeda arrival) is canonically
post-slice content.

## Trigger conditions (Design contract)

Suggested gating:
- `flag:mhlai_destroyed=True` (Fall has happened)
- `flag:rainbow_resonator_equipped=True` (Steward has the climactic module)
- `flag:rainbow_worlds_seeded >= 8` (8 of 10 Rainbow Worlds have been
  seeded with Resonator pulses; canonical "alignment threshold")
- `flag:migration_fleet_assembled=True` (Hearth-of-Iron has called all
  Migration vessels into formation)

When all conditions met → cinematic auto-fires; the Final Conflict
begins; the slice's climax is engaged.

## Outcomes (slice terminal states)

| Outcome | Conditions | Slice epilogue tone |
|---|---|---|
| **Diplomatic success (rare)** | Persuader ≥ 10 + multiple specific quest flags | Best ending — Drev-Tok joins the Migration with honor; the Defender doctrine evolves; the Migration completes with all factions reconciled |
| **Persuader-symbolic-clash** | Persuader ≥ 8 + Halia alive free | Bittersweet — Drev-Tok stays in galaxy with honor guard; the Migration completes |
| **Combat victory** | Default success | Canonical — Drev-Tok dies; the Migration completes with the Steward as canonical Persuader leader; Defender faction mourns Drev-Tok |
| **Combat narrow** | High losses (Selvenne reef section partially lost, Karavem casualties high) | Sad — Migration completes but at heavy cost; some Andromeda species smaller than planned |
| **Combat defeat (game over)** | Player loses combat | Slice game-over — Time Drive auto-engages; combat retries (per canonical Time Drive mechanic) |

## How this scene serves the slice

- **Concentrates faction politics** — every Persuader/Cleanser/Defender
  choice the player made over 2 years pays off in fleet composition,
  Drev-Tok's coalition makeup, and negotiation availability
- **Caps the Hammer-Of-Refusal / Hijacked Vessel arcs** — those late-
  slice content surfaces *matter* here; the Vessel is the only way to
  unlock diplomatic success; the Hammer's "we marked the predator"
  proof is canonically the cornerstone of Drev-Tok's reversibility
- **Pays off crew bonds** — players who built bonds get easier combat;
  players who skipped get the canonical hard fight
- **Pays off the Halia arc** — Halia present on the bridge if alive
  free; Halia memorial sigil if dead; Halia on Drev-Tok's coalition
  if Cleanser-supervised (the DEVASTATING variant — the Steward must
  fight their friend's controlled-vessel)
- **Closes the slice on a canonical Persuader note** — the Migration
  proceeds; the galaxy is partially saved; the dimensional crossing
  to Andromeda opens
- **Sets up Phase 7 epilogue** — Andromeda arrival is post-slice

## Canon revision 2026-05-18 — *simpler than the 2026-05-17 draft*

Aaron's spec, verbatim: *"At the last goalpost for the game, we are
going to meet at the tip of the arrow of the rainbow worlds and
depart, but when we arrive, the Homesteaders fleet is there, lead by
our arch nemesis who has convinced them that the precursors leaving
is what would bring destruction. No matter how we try to wiggle out
of it or negotiate, we end in conflict. Super-melee, they get all of
their ships, one of each, and we get all of our ships, one of each
friendly species we helped with their quest. If we win, we get to go
see the ending, otherwise, re-load last save (our time drive takes
us back before the conflict starts)."*

This **supersedes** the 2026-05-17 draft's three-branch resolution
(diplomatic-rare / diplomatic-symbolic / combat). The simplification:

- **Negotiation always fails**. The Steward can try (canonically 2-3
  attempts shown for character beats), but Drev-Tok rejects each
  one. There is no diplomatic-victory branch.
- **Combat is forced**. Super-melee fleet-vs-fleet.
- **Fleet composition is mechanical, not branch-dependent**:
  - *Drev-Tok's fleet* = **one of each canonical Homesteader-aligned
    combat ship class** (fixed; no variation based on slice flags).
  - *Steward's fleet* = **Furling Scout PLUS one ship per friendly
    species the player helped** (alliance-flag-driven; varies with
    slice progress).
- **Win path**: transition to the ending (per canonical Endings
  evaluation per the 6-tier system in `the-endings.md`).
- **Loss path**: the Furling **Time Drive** rewinds the player to
  the moment just before the conflict triggered. The player can
  try again with whatever fleet they had — *no game-over*, *no
  permadeath of allies in this specific encounter* (Time Drive
  honors the canonical "combat is not rewindable mid-fight, but
  losses pre-fight ARE" rule from `furling-tech-mechanics.md`).
  Functionally: the loss path is *retry until you win*.

### Implementation contract (Design lane)

Per the above, the engine work:

1. **Trigger predicate** `should_fire_final_conflict(game)` checks the
   minimum slice-end gates:
   - `mhlai_destroyed=True` (post-Fall)
   - At least N alliance flags set (the Steward has done their job)
   - `rainbow_resonator_equipped=True` (the player has the artifact
     that lets the Migration cross)
   - NOT `final_conflict_resolved=True` (single-fire)
2. **Auto-fire on hyperspace entry** — same pattern as `should_fire_fall`
3. **Pre-conflict snapshot** — store relevant game state (fleet,
   credits, cargo, flags) on entry. The Time Drive restore-on-loss
   path uses this.
4. **`FinalConflictScene`** — orchestrates: brief Drev-Tok dialogue
   (negotiation always rejected) → spawns `FleetCombatScene` with
   the composed fleets → handles result.
5. **Drev-Tok fleet** — canonical fixed list (see implementation:
   `src/scz/content/final_conflict.py`).
6. **Steward fleet** — composed from alliance flags at call time.
   `Furling Scout` is always present (slot 0). Each ally flag adds
   one canonical ship.
7. **Win** → set `final_conflict_resolved=True` + transition to
   the Endings evaluation (currently a stub scene; full
   implementation per `the-endings.md` is a separate dispatch).
8. **Loss** → restore from the pre-conflict snapshot + place the
   player back in hyperspace just outside the Rainbow Worlds
   trigger zone. They can re-engage by re-entering the trigger
   conditions.
