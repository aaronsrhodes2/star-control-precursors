> Authored 2026-05-17 per Aaron's brief: *"make sure each species we

> from us or need us to do something, or we need to deal with them

## "The Quiet Resolution" — canonical mandate name

The Furling Steward's mandate has been referred to in prior docs as
*"the Migration timetable"* and *"the win condition"* and *"handling
every sentient species before the Others arrive."* These all point at
the same canonical activity, but the slice needs a *name* for it that
the Steward and the Council can actually say aloud.

**The canonical name is: *The Quiet Resolution.***

It is canonical in three senses simultaneously:

1. **Quiet** — the central canon: every species the Furlings encounter
   in the slice needs to be brought below the Others' detection
   threshold (or removed from the cluster entirely) BEFORE the Others
   arrive. The galaxy must become *quiet*.
2. **Resolution** — every species's case must be *resolved* (terminal
   status set: Migrated / Cloaked / Hidden / Pre-sentient / Eliminated
   / Pending). The Furling Council's bookkeeping must close. No
   loose ends.
3. **Resolution (the literary sense)** — the Furling Steward's
   *narrative* must reach a satisfying end-state. The Quiet Resolution
   is the dignified-tragedy framing the slice asks the player to
   accept. *"We could not save everyone. We made the work clean.
   That is the dignity."*

The Furling Persuader faction adopted *"The Quiet Resolution"* as the
mandate's official Council-channel name about 18 months before slice
start (canonical decision by the Persuader Senior Council; ratified
unanimously). Halia uses it. The Steward uses it. Drev-Tok uses it
*dismissively* (canonical Defender position: *"The Quiet Resolution
is preemptive surrender dressed in language"*).

### How "The Quiet Resolution" is referenced in canon

- **Halia's first-tutorial Council briefing** would canonically open
  with: *"Steward. Your mandate is The Quiet Resolution. Every
  species we encounter must be brought to a terminal state — saved,
  hidden, or honestly mourned — before the Others arrive. The
  bookkeeping is the dignity."*
- **Drev-Tok's Final Conflict opening** would canonically include:
  *"Your Quiet Resolution is what brought us here, Steward. You
  resolved species into terminal statuses; I have been *not*
  resolved into one for two years, and here I am."*
- The Steward's wit-options can reference *"The Quiet Resolution"*
  casually — *"Add it to the Quiet Resolution paperwork"* etc.

## The Side-Quest Mandate

**Every slice species we meet has at least one canonical side-quest**
encoding either:

- **(a) "They want something from us"** — the species has a need we can
  fulfill (most common pattern; the Slylandro/Mycon/Androsynth/Mmrnmhrm
  pattern)
- **(b) "We need them to do something"** — we have a need they can
  fulfill (the Stelloth/Karavem/Kovellim trade pattern)
- **(c) "They are resisting our mandate"** — they refuse to be
  resolved cleanly; the Steward must persuade, override, or honor
  their refusal (the Taalo Shield / Burvixese Caster / Utwig Veils /
  Mrokon Hammer pattern; also the Final Conflict)

**Every successful side-quest grants at least one tangible reward**
from the canonical reward list:

1. **A crew member** (recruitable NPC)
2. **A module blueprint** (a schematic that unlocks a Mh-Lai-purchasable module per [economy-and-trade-loops.md](economy-and-trade-loops.md))
3. **An artifact** (a Bio-Archive-tracked item the slice records — often a flag-state item)
4. **A sensor** (a sensor-slot module)
5. **A lander** (a special-case lander variant; canonically reserved for major rewards)
6. **A lander upgrade** (a lander-system module — sub-system distinct from ship-mod modules per the Melnorme niche canon)

The full reward inventory per species follows.

## NEW lander-upgrade reward category (canon clarification)

Per the [economy-and-trade-loops.md](economy-and-trade-loops.md)
canon, **lander upgrades are a distinct sub-system from ship modules**.
They are normally sold by the Melnorme for BIO-cargo and are field-
installable (no Mh-Lai install gate). However, **species-quest reward
grants of lander upgrades are exempt from the Melnorme-only rule** —
they're earned through narrative work, not trade.

The new species-granted lander upgrades follow the same slot-mechanic
suggestion as the existing Melnorme lander-hardening backlog (per
HANDOFF_design_chat.md): a new `lander_armor` slot, parallel to
`sensor`, with these new entries available as quest-grant modules:

- **`LANDER_CHENJESU_CRYSTALLINE_PLATING`** (Chenjesu quest reward):
  +50% surface_hazard_resistance; canonical: the Chenjesu's crystalline
  resonance applied to lander hulls; gentle bioluminescent shimmer
  visible while landed
- **`LANDER_MROKON_PUPPET_ARMOR`** (Mrokon quest reward, full-support
  branch only): +30% lander_max_hp; +25% surface_hazard_resistance;
  canonical: Mrokon puppet-armor metallurgy applied to lander chassis;
  bears the canonical scribed-ledger pattern (small visible kill-tally
  marks added per surface mission)
- **`LANDER_BURVIXESE_AMPLIFIER`** (Burvixese quest reward):
  +35% surface_sensor_range; +20% deposit-detection-fidelity; canonical:
  cognitive-amplification circuitry derived from the Burv Caster's
  smaller broadcaster nodes, miniaturized for lander use
- **`LANDER_TAALO_SILICATE_HARDENING`** (Taalo quest reward, full-help
  branch only): +40% surface_hazard_resistance against lava/lightning/
  geological hazards; canonical: silicate-substrate plating derived
  from Taalo Shield-fragment material; pale-violet glow-tracery visible
  on the lander

These four lander-upgrades collectively cover **4 of the 8
Homesteader species quests**, ensuring the lander-upgrade reward
category is *meaningfully* represented across the slice — and they
canonically continue the dignity of doomed species into the Migration
(the lander upgrades are Furling-engineered using materials/technology
from species that did not survive).

## How to apply (future sessions)

- When asked *"does X species have a side-quest?"*: check the audit
  table above. Every slice species has at least one.
- When asked *"what does X species grant?"*: check the audit table.
- When designing a NEW species (post-roster-target; unlikely): the
  species MUST have a side-quest with at least one tangible reward
  from the canonical 6-category list.
- When player feedback says *"the rewards feel uneven"*: reference
  the audit table; the current intent is that EVERY species has a
  meaningful tangible reward, and the 4 Homesteader-quest lander-
  upgrade additions resolved the last visible gap.
