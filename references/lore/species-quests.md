## Species Quests (one per species, bidirectional)

> Every slice species has one canonical quest: **they want a concrete thing from us, we want a concrete thing from them.** This is the species' narrative center of mass — the encounter that turns first contact into a relationship. Branching outcomes feed [win-condition-and-methods.md](win-condition-and-methods.md)'s terminal-status framework. Schema in [species-design-schema.md §6](species-design-schema.md).

> **Canonical FSM data — `tools/quest_inventory.csv`** (built from `tools/build_quest_inventory.py`). Authored 2026-05-17. Each quest below has a full state-and-choice tree in the spreadsheet — initial encounter, info branches, transactional ask, terminal outcomes mapped to Win-condition statuses. This narrative doc summarizes the *shape* of each quest; the spreadsheet carries the *dialog content and side-effect plumbing* that Design-chat wires into `src/scz/dialog/characters.py`. **When making changes to a quest's branching:** edit the ROWS in `tools/build_quest_inventory.py`, regenerate the CSV, and update this doc's summary to match.

The list below is the slice authoring target. Detail per-quest grows as we author it; for the design pass, what matters is the *shape* — "this is the trade" — not the full dialog FSM.

## Slylandro Observers — *The Cloaking Satellite*

- **They want**: continuance. The Slylandro can't lift their own gas-bag bodies out of the atmosphere. Their only viable terminal status is *Cloaked* (Homesteader path) or *Migrated* (Precursor path, requires building them a hyperspace-capable evacuation lift).
- **We want**: the **Hyperspace-Echo Sensor Pattern** — when the Slylandro drift-think in the upper troposphere, their pulse patterns interfere with hyperspace echoes in a measurable way. Recording the interference gives the Furling Steward an Other-detection sensor module a generation ahead of the current one.
- **Bridge**: the cloak works *because* it uses the same interference pattern the Slylandro generate naturally — installing a satellite is essentially turning their atmosphere into a permanent quiet-field. The Slylandro must consent (they're sentient evacuees, not livestock) and they must teach you the pattern. Refusal of consent leaves *Eliminate* on the table for the Cleanser faction.
- **Bidirectional reward**: their cloak satellite goes up; your scanner module installs.
- **Branches**:
  - Persuader/Hide path: cloak satellite installed → terminal **Cloaked**
  - Precursor path: build them a lift → terminal **Migrated** (expensive, late slice)
  - Cleanser path: refuse to help → Cleanser faction enforces **Eliminated**
  - Defender path: leave them be → **Pre-sentient** (lore-tagged false; they're already sentient, so this is a delusion that ends in **Eliminated** by the Others arriving)

## Proto-Ur-Quan + Proto-Qor-Ah — *The Uplift Dilemma*

- **They want**: nothing from us — they're pre-sapient mid-uplift. The "want" is the Mycon-managed continued nudging that's already happening; the question is whether the Steward *continues* or *stops* it.
- **We want**: clarity on what happens next. The Council is split. Persuaders argue for completing the uplift to sapience so we can persuade them to Migrate; Cleansers argue for cleansing the pre-sapient stock before they cross the detection threshold; Defenders argue to halt-and-leave.
- **Bridge**: the player runs an observation arc — three encounters showing the proto-Ur-Quan establishing dominance hierarchy, the proto-Qor-Ah escalating purification ritual, and a glimpse of what the merged sapient form would look like. The quest's *deliverable* is the Steward's recommendation to Council.
- **Bidirectional reward**: the recommendation drives the slice's biggest moral choice; the Council reaction grants either **Persuader**, **Cleanser**, or **Defender** faction standing.
- **Branches**:
  - Continue uplift → both species mature → become Migration cases (later slice content; for slice, this just sets up the SC2-era domination canon)
  - Stop uplift → terminal **Pre-sentient** (slice-clean, SC2 canon-clean)
  - Cleanse → terminal **Eliminated** (Cleanser standing +; Persuader / Defender standing −−)

## Mycon Biots — *The Deep Child Whisper*

- **They want**: continued service. The Mycon were created by the Furlings as terraformers and are happy at it. They ask for **terraforming directives** to keep the work flowing.
- **We want**: the **Mantle-Resonance Bio-Architect** module — a Mycon-derived organ that grows the Steward's lander tractor-beam radius and accelerates lander replication, by repurposing the same mantle-stir biology the Mycon use to plant seeds. This is a permanent ship upgrade with real combat-AI relevance (faster lander cycle = faster mineral haul = faster repair).
- **Bridge**: the deal is good — until the Steward notices the FIRST_WHISPER state in their dialog. A subset of the biot mass is beginning to develop sentience through the "Deep Child" emergence. If the player accepts the bio-architect module and ignores the whispers, the slice climax includes a Them-Corrupted biot combat encounter (the Deep Child's awakening attracts the Others). If the player reports the whispers to Council early, the bio-architect is still delivered but a suppression directive accompanies it — terminal **Pre-sentient** preserved.
- **Bidirectional reward**: Bio-Architect module installed; Mycon terraforming directives delivered.
- **Branches**:
  - Suppress whispers → **Pre-sentient** (Persuader / Defender +)
  - Allow emergence → new sentient species → must be handled like another Slylandro/Arilou (cloak / migrate / etc.)
  - Cleanse the heretical biot subset only → **Pre-sentient** for rest, but Cleanser standing rises and Persuader standing falls
  - Allow + ignore → Them-corrupted combat encounter; biots end **Eliminated** by the Others on arrival regardless

## Arilou Lalee'lay — *The Sage's Gift* *(already implemented through Sage dialog)*

- **They want**: the Council's *decision* on the Migration timetable. The Arilou are leaving for Quasi-Space regardless; they want a clean handoff so they aren't blamed for the leftover problem.
- **We want**: **Quasi-Space access** (portal spawner + portal map) — already implemented as the Sage's gift in `dialog/characters.py:arilou_sage()`, side-effect `_grant_quasispace_portal`.
- **Bridge**: the Sage delivers the gift unconditionally because they pity the Steward, but at the cost of an obligation — the Sage expects the Steward to *speak for* the Arilou view at Council. (Council scene is later content; for the slice, this is a flag set on the player profile.)
- **Bidirectional reward**: portal spawner gained; the Steward owes the Sage a speech.
- **Branches**:
  - Speak for the Arilou at Council → terminal **Hidden** (Arilou voluntary exile, Arilou standing high)
  - Refuse to advocate → terminal **Hidden** anyway (they leave regardless), but Arilou standing low — affects future encounters
  - Council overrides Arilou advocacy with Cleanser doctrine → **Eliminated** of any holdouts (rare and ugly)

## Androsynth Refugees — *The Distress Beacon*

- **They want**: medical aid — they arrived with dimensional-shear injuries from the decursion attack that displaced them. The Steward can deliver a **shear-repair fab pattern** from the lander fabricator + Bio-Architect work.
- **We want**: the **Androsynth Distress Beacon** — an unimpeachable recording of the Others' decursion attack on their original timeline (their *future*, our era's some-millennia-later). This is the slice's primary "proof of the Others" artifact, used in every later Persuader dialog to convince other species to evacuate.
- **Bridge**: Coel Tessar (the canonical refugee leader) shares the beacon as soon as the Furling Steward proves they can stabilize her crew. The shear-repair lands the wounded; the beacon lands in the Furling Archive.
- **Bidirectional reward**: refugees stabilized → terminal **Migrated** (they cross with the Migration); Distress Beacon in the Bio-Archive's Others section.
- **Branches**:
  - Standard delivery → both halves complete cleanly
  - Steward declines to help (rare) → refugees die; their beacon is recovered from the wreck (still gained), but Persuader standing collapses; Cleanser standing rises (some Cleansers wanted them silenced anyway)
  - Steward fails the fab roll → can retry; never gates the slice

## Mmrnmhrm Sentinels — *The Archive Excerpt* *(optional)*

- **They want**: a **cognitive upgrade** — they've been working unchanged for 3-8 million years and the Sentinel collective has reached the limit of its self-modification ability. The Furling Steward can apply a Bio-Architect-derived neural-substrate patch that re-opens self-improvement.
- **We want**: the **Mmrnmhrm Archive Excerpt** — testimony that *defense doesn't work*. Their entire existence has been "defend the Homeworld"; they outlived their First-Makers, who lost. Used by Defender-faction Furlings to argue *for* Migration ("the Mmrnmhrm proved the alternative").
- **Bridge**: the Mmrnmhrm don't dramatize this — they offer the archive entry as transactional, deliver the patch as transactional. Quiet wry awareness of the absurdity is in their voice.
- **Bidirectional reward**: cognitive patch applied (their continued upgrade-cycle); Archive Excerpt in the Bio-Archive.
- **Branches**:
  - Standard delivery → Defender-faction standing falls (their argument is undermined), Persuader rises
  - Steward declines the patch but takes the Excerpt → small Persuader cost; Defender boost
  - Cleanser arc: a Cleanser Furling argues the Mmrnmhrm should be destroyed before they cross the sentience threshold themselves. The player can advocate for or against; choice affects terminal status
- **Terminal status (depending on path)**: **Migrated**, **Pre-sentient**, or **Eliminated** (Cleanser path)

## Chenjesu Crystalline Collective — *The Resonance Record* *(optional)*

- **They want**: nothing. They are rooted, patient, and unconcerned with departure — their crystalline substrate is invisible to the Others and they have lived through a prior Culling already. *They will remain.*
- **We want**: the **Resonance Record** — testimony, slowly delivered, of the *last* Culling. Millions of years ago. Witnessed from a rooted position. The slice's most cosmologically significant reveal: the Others come in cycles, the Furlings are not the first to face this, and rooted patience may be the *one* defense that works.
- **Bridge**: the Chenjesu offer the record in slow present-tense utterances. They ask nothing in return because they need nothing. The Furling Steward can offer a gift of mineral nutrients to accelerate their slow growth — accepted with no change in obligation, in true Chenjesu form.
- **Bidirectional reward**: Resonance Record in the Bio-Archive (and unlocked as evidence in Council dialog); the Chenjesu remain rooted in good condition.
- **Branches**:
  - Standard contact → terminal **Pre-sentient** (below detection threshold, no action needed); Defender standing rises (their argument validated by Chenjesu precedent)
  - Cleanser argues they're sentient and should be eliminated before transition → big moral fight; the slice's cleanest test of the Persuader/Cleanser axis
  - Player chooses to *mobilize* the Chenjesu (heretical hubris) → outside slice scope; flag for full game

## Taalo — *The Shield That Will Not Hold* *(side-quest; help-or-not, but they die either way)*

> **Canon note** (revised 2026-05-17, third pass): the Taalo are SC2-canonical silicon-based sentients. **The species *is* a single mountain range** on a high-silicate planet; individual Taalo are mobile fragments of that mountain. **They cannot leave** — their metabolism is tied to the local planetary ecosystem; relocation kills them. ~5,000-year deep friendship with the Furlings. In our slice they are **building the Taalo Shield** — a planetary-scale anti-Others defensive barrier — believing (correctly) that they cannot migrate and (incorrectly) that the Shield might hold. **The Shield does not work.** The Taalo are **consumed by the Others in the Culling regardless of any Steward intervention.** The Steward's choice is *how* to bear witness and *what survives them as a SC2-era artifact*. See [species-sheets.md §9 Taalo](species-sheets.md) for the full sheet; this entry is the quest summary.

- **They want**: **Furling engineering help with the Taalo Shield**. The Taalo geometers are extraordinarily thorough but limited by their geological tempo; Furling rapid-engineering can compress decades of their work into seasons. They ask for cognitive-pattern modeling of the Others' detection apparatus, field-generator schematics, mantle-deep energy-routing, antimatter handling for the activation-burst, whatever Hider-faction tooling the Steward can route to them. They are humble askers — they are not certain the help will be enough; they thank in advance for the trying.
- **We want**: the Bio-Archive record of the work + the witnessing. The Hider faction will pay for the Shield-construction telemetry (unique data on a planetary-scale anti-Others defense built, activated, and *failed* in real-time). The Persuader faction will pay for the *experience of having borne witness* — the post-Migration galaxy's epilogue is shaped by which Stewards were present for which species' last moments.
- **Bridge**: the Furlings have known the Taalo for ~5,000 years. The Steward is canonically **the fourth Steward to visit this particular ridge** across the friendship's history. The Shield project is the slice's slowest-paced crisis — Taalo time, not Steward time. The Steward visits *repeatedly* across the slice's run, contributing what they can each time, watching the friendship deepen at the end. The encounter is the slice's longest-paced dialog beat by design: walk the lower slope past Shield generator-nodes; sit in the contemplation-cove; hear slow Taalo and weighty mountain-thoughts; contribute; optionally exchange mineral samples (the Taalo place them where they think the Shield will fail first); long farewell.
- **Bidirectional reward**: Hider-faction Bio-Archive entry (Shield-construction telemetry); Persuader-faction Bio-Archive entry (witnessing record); a **Shield-fragment** carried to Andromeda (small piece of the inert Shield substrate; the SC2-era recoverable artifact is what this fragment will eventually be); Taalo standing rises across all Furling factions; **no module, no resource, no ship**
- **Branches** *(all end with Taalo eliminated; differences are in how complete the SC2 artifact is)*:
  - **Help fully — repeated visits, every contribution accepted** → Shield **finished and activated** late in slice → Others arrive → Shield does *nothing* → Taalo consumed within hours. The Steward witnesses. **Finished Shield's inert remains survive intact**. 230kya later SC2 archaeologists recover an intact device with its full psionic-nullifier side-effect property. **SC2's canonical anti-Dnyarri tool is at maximum effectiveness only if our Steward helped.** Persuader ++, Hider ++
  - **Help partially — occasional visits, some contributions** → Shield **partially complete** at activation → still fails → Taalo consumed. **Partial-Shield remains**; SC2 archaeologists recover a partially-functional device; Dnyarri-counter works but less reliably
  - **Don't help — visit but contribute nothing, or skip entirely** → Shield **never finished** → Taalo consumed mid-construction; Shield-infrastructure stands abandoned and inert on the empty planet → SC2 archaeologists recover a non-functional device. **Captain Zelnick's eventual victory over the Talking Pet becomes much harder or impossible.** A real downstream cost; the in-slice epilogue notes the five-thousand-year friendship was unattended at the end
  - **Sabotage / Cleanser pre-emptive euthanasia** → a Cleanser argues the Shield-activation broadcast may itself flag the cluster to the Others, demanding pre-emptive Taalo euthanasia. If the Steward complies → Taalo **Eliminated early**; Shield destroyed pre-activation; no SC2 artifact at all; **Furling Council formally censures the Steward** (Cleanser doctrine has a hard floor at erasing a substrate-consciousness). The worst possible outcome for both the Taalo *and* SC2-era humanity

## Burvixese — *The Caster Reckoning* *(Be-Loud doctrine; mid-game catastrophic-witness event)*

> **Canon note**: in our era, the Burvixese have committed to a survival doctrine the Furling Council advised against. They are building the **Burv Caster** — a planetary-scale cognitive broadcaster — believing that if their intelligence-signature is unmistakably *significant*, the Others will recognize them as peers rather than prey, or pass over them as too costly to harvest. It will not work. The Others eat the loud first. See [species-sheets.md §10 Burvixese](species-sheets.md) and [furling-artifacts-and-callforwards.md "The Burvixese and the Caster"](furling-artifacts-and-callforwards.md).

- **They want (early-slice)**: a Council witness for the Caster's harmonic activation. They are *proud* of the work; they invite the Steward as one would invite a colleague to a launch. Engineering tour of the array. Demonstrations of the carrier-band. *Confidence.*
- **We want**: cognitive-signature telemetry from before and after the activation. The Furling **Hider faction** badly wants this data — successful or failed, the cognitive-amplification doctrine is unprecedented and the measurements matter for the long-term cognitive-dampening research thread (parallel-but-opposite to the Taalo silicon-substrate research). The Hider archive will gain its sharpest Be-Loud-doesn't-work evidence from this beat.
- **Bridge**: the Burvixese welcome the Steward enthusiastically. They believe they will succeed. The Steward's choice is whether to (a) witness silently, (b) advocate against — convince some Burvixese to migrate as insurance, (c) sabotage (Cleanser pressure: the harmonic activation will broadcast at an amplitude that *flags* the cluster to the Others; some Cleansers argue the Burvixese should be euthanized pre-activation for everyone else's safety)
- **Bidirectional reward**: cognitive-signature telemetry → Hider archive entry; permanent Persuader standing shift if the Steward attempts pre-activation evacuation; **no module, no resource** — the witnessing and the migrant-rescue ARE the reward
- **Branches**:
  - **Witness silently** → harmonic activation proceeds → the Others arrive within minutes → most Burvixese die at the Caster site → a small surviving contingent (whoever happened to be off-world during activation) migrates to Andromeda → terminal status MIXED: **Eliminated** (majority) + **Migrated** (small contingent). The Caster site is recovered intact by Furling researchers; smaller broadcaster nodes scattered across the galaxy survive as the SC2-canonical Burv Broadcasters
  - **Advocate pre-activation evacuation** → small migrant contingent grows (~30% of population manages to be off-world or aboard Furling lift during activation); harmonic activation still happens for the doctrinal majority → still **Eliminated** + **Migrated** mixed, but Migrated proportion is much larger; Persuader standing rises significantly; the slice epilogue tracks the Andromeda diaspora warmly
  - **Sabotage / Cleanser-pressure pre-emptive euthanasia** → Burvixese **Eliminated** entirely before activation; the Caster is dismantled, not destroyed; cluster cognitive-signature flare averted; Cleanser standing up sharply; Persuader/Hider standing down sharply; SC2-canonical Burv Broadcasters never get deployed (lore hole — SC2 archaeologists find the Burvix Caster system empty AND no broadcaster network across the galaxy)
  - **Skip entirely** → activation proceeds without Council witness; same Eliminated-majority/Migrated-minority outcome; Bio-Archive lacks the telemetry; the slice records that the Caster lit up, then went quiet, and the cluster mourned

## Utwig — *The Veils Falling* *(Homesteader-by-devolution; mid-game witnessing event)*

> **Canon note**: the Utwig are SC2's depressed mask-wearers, retroactively explained. In the Furling era they have *just* become sentient and choose to *deliberately devolve themselves* by adopting mask-and-ceremony culture as a cognitive-dampening doctrine. It is the slice's clean example of below-threshold survival via *chosen culture* (distinct from Mmrnmhrm/Chenjesu's biology-based immunity and the Slylandro's Furling-tech cloak). See [species-sheets.md §11](species-sheets.md) + [furling-artifacts-and-callforwards.md "The Utwig and the Ultron"](furling-artifacts-and-callforwards.md). SC3-era "the Precursors devolved into animals" is canonically a misreading: it was the Utwig.

- **They want**: a Council witness for the **Veils Falling** ceremony — the formal mass-adoption event where the entire Utwig population dons the masks of the doctrine. The pre-doctrine Utwig leaders have decided. They have invited Furling Council members from neighboring clusters. The Steward is asked, as the cluster's Council representative, to be one of the named witnesses in the formal ledger.
- **We want**: the Bio-Archive record of the Veils Falling itself, including cognitive-signature telemetry from before and after the doctrine takes hold. The Utwig are the **first species in galactic history** to attempt cognitive-dampening as a *cultural* strategy rather than via biology or external tech. The Council needs to know: does it work? Measurable? At what observable cost? The slice's Hider faction in particular has been waiting for this evidence.
- **Bridge**: the Utwig welcome the Steward unconditionally. They will not argue the doctrine; they have decided. There is no Convince path. There is no Migration path for the majority (they have already refused). What the Steward can do is *advocate quietly* among the pre-doctrine youth — the unmasked Utwig who have not yet committed — and a small migrant contingent may join the Precursors instead of the doctrine. The Cleanser path is also available and is *especially fraught here*: the cognitive-transition moment of the Veils Falling emits a brief *flare* that some Cleansers argue could attract the Others, justifying pre-emptive euthanasia.
- **Bidirectional reward**: Veils Falling Bio-Archive entry (Artifacts category); a preserved *unmasked* Utwig voice-recording — the only one in the galaxy after the doctrine completes; cognitive-signature telemetry useful for Hider-faction research. **No combat reward, no module, no resource.** The slice's third witnessing-only quest alongside the Taalo (Silicon Witnesses) and the Burvixese (Caster Reckoning). The Steward earns Persuader and Hider standing in roughly equal measure
- **Branches**:
  - **Attend + witness silently** → terminal **Pre-sentient** (the doctrine works; the Utwig survive the Culling locked into ceremony); Persuader standing +, Hider standing ++ (their thesis is validated)
  - **Attend + advocate to pre-doctrine youth** → mixed outcome: the majority terminal **Pre-sentient**; a small migrant contingent terminal **Migrated**; Persuader standing ++; SC2-era diaspora hook (the migrant Utwig found a colony in the neighboring galaxy that may someday return)
  - **Sabotage / Cleanser-pressure euthanasia** → Utwig **Eliminated** before the doctrine completes; Cleanser standing ++, Persuader standing −−, Hider standing −−; SC2-era lore consequence: no Utwig, no Ultron tragedy, no SC3 "Precursors devolved" misreading 250,000 years later. The galaxy is *quieter* in SC2 era because of your choice; the Mask Eats World lore-hole is canon
  - **Decline the witness invitation** → the doctrine still proceeds without a Council witness; Utwig still **Pre-sentient**; but the Bio-Archive record is partial, the Hider faction does not get its measurement, and the slice's epilogue notes that the Utwig went quiet *without anyone present to record what worked*. Small Persuader standing penalty for refusing a fellow sapient's last clear-cognition request

## Melnorme Trade-Network — *The Trader's Manifest* *(multi-beat recruitment; Persuader path; comedic register)*

> **Canon note**: per [project_melnorme.md](../../memory/project_melnorme.md) and the existing in-engine handler in `src/scz/content/star_arrival.py`, the Melnorme are sentient super-giant traders in our era (NOT proto). They orbit super-giant stars; they trade tech and info for BIO cargo; they side with the Precursors *in principle* but have not committed institutionally. Individual traders intercept the Steward at any super-giant for the standard trade dialog (already implemented). This quest is the *species-level* commitment — convincing the Melnorme trade-network as a whole to migrate.

- **They want**: proof. The Melnorme do not commit on word — they want *receipts*. Three of them, layered: (1) proof the Others are real (the Androsynth Distress Beacon, played for them on the homeworld Council's screen), (2) proof the Steward can do business correctly (the shelving test at the High Trader's Super-Mart), and (3) proof the Migration is more than a Furling delusion (at least one OTHER non-Furling species committed). All three before they sign on.
- **We want**: the entire Melnorme trade-network committed to Andromeda. The Melnorme are not numerous as individuals but they are *logistically central* — their tradeships move bio-cargo, schematics, sensor data across the cluster. Without them, the Migration loses its supply chain. Their reward delivery also brings: a **Melnorme Trade-Network Sensor** (tier-2 sensor stub — surfaces all super-giant trade-routes + per-system bio-cargo predictions; final deltas TODO_LORE) and a **Melnorme Science-Trade Schematic** (redeemed at Mh-Lai's Schematic Vault to unlock a future Melnorme-derived module; final identity TODO_LORE).
- **Bridge**: the existing Melnorme trade dialog (auto-launches at any super-giant) gains a new top-level option: *"Will you migrate with us?"* The trader, provided the Steward already has the Distress Beacon in their archive, directs them to the Melnorme homeworld at one specific super-giant — **Alpha Vulpeculae** (3594, 2661 in the precursor-era universe data; current invented canon; chosen for narrative resonance with the Androsynth, whose Vulpeculae Engineering Detachment lived two hundred and fifty thousand years downstream of the same coordinates). From there, the 4-beat recruitment plays out.
- **Bidirectional reward**: Melnorme network commits → terminal **Migrated**; Steward gets sensor module + schematic.
- **Tonal register**: transactional, gallows-comedic. The Melnorme's relentless commercial-doctrine sensibility — applied to *existential* matters — is the source of the comedy. The shelving test (Beat 3) is the canonical example. The humor never softens the stakes; it underscores them. *The Council that asks whether you took fresh or stale bio-substrate is the same Council deciding whether their species evacuates a galaxy.*
- **Branches**:
  - **Full path** → all four gates met → terminal **Migrated**. Persuader standing ++. Trade-Network Sensor + Science-Trade Schematic in inventory.
  - **No Beacon yet (Beat 1 gate)** → the trader politely refuses to direct the Steward home: *"Without proof, the Elders will not see you. We do not waste the Elders' time. Return when your manifest is heavier."* Quest gated behind Androsynth Beacon recovery (Tutorial Beat 4 / Coel Tessar encounter).
  - **Failed shelving test (Beat 3)** → comic stall. The elder grumbles, the Steward goes back to the cold-aisle, the elder asks the same question again. Recoverable; first miss is *expected* (the joke needs the player to fail once). Persistent miss-after-being-told would be a fail state, but for slice MVP one auto-retry is enough.
  - **No second-species witness yet (Beat 4 gate)** → Council defers: *"You have shown us what is coming. You have shown us you can do business. You have not shown us a movement. Bring us one species not your own that has chosen to ride with you, and we will speak again."* Quest re-opens automatically the moment any non-Androsynth, non-Furling species lands a `<species>_terminal_status = "Migrated"` flag.
  - **Refused engagement entirely** → Melnorme stay nomadic → terminal **Eliminated** when the Others arrive. The distributed Melnorme tradeships are the easiest possible target for an Other looking for sentience-density; they are eaten *first* in the deep-space corridors between super-giants. The slice's epilogue notes that "*the Steward did not bring the manifest. The Elders did not see it. The trader-pods went out of contact one by one.*"

### Beats

1. **Trader Intercept** *(at any MELNORME_PROTO super-giant; prereq: `has_distress_beacon`)*. The standard Melnorme trade dialog gains a new top-level option: *"Will you migrate with us?"* The trader — provided the Beacon flag is set — directs the Steward to the homeworld system at Alpha Vulpeculae. Without the Beacon, the trader politely refuses (*"Without proof, the Elders will not see you."*) and the option re-presents on the next intercept once the Beacon is recovered. **Side-effects**: `met_melnorme_trader_recruitment`, `learned_melnorme_homeworld`.

2. **Homeworld Council** *(at Alpha Vulpeculae)*. New arrival-handler branch in `src/scz/content/star_arrival.py` — when the Steward enters Alpha Vulpeculae *and* `learned_melnorme_homeworld` is set, the standard Melnorme trader is replaced by `melnorme_council()` (new dialog character). The Council greets formally and references the Androsynth rumours: *"Our traders bring us footage from the Vulpeculae deeps that we cannot verify. The dead colony is on the same heading as our spice-route. We are — concerned. We do not pretend otherwise."* The Steward offers the Beacon. The recording plays for them, on their own screens — the cities counting backward, the eight million who were not, the half-second of light from somewhere that was no longer there. The Council reacts visibly. *"This is — this is not a rumour. This is the price of standing still."* **Side-effects**: `met_melnorme_council`, `melnorme_saw_beacon`.

3. **High Trader's Super-Mart** *(unlocked by Beat 2)*. Council directs the Steward to the High Trader's Super-Mart at the homeworld — *"where all things can be bought, and where worthiness is measured by what you choose."* The Super-Mart is a Melnorme-side equivalent of Mh-Lai Station's customization bay: **the Steward can install / uninstall ship modules here** (canonical: parallels Mh-Lai's tier-1+ install gate; the Super-Mart bypasses Furling-shipyard tolerance with a flavor justification that Melnorme techs handle any-substrate work fluently, *for a price* — a Melnorme commercial rate that converts Furling credits to bio-cargo for the install; trade-economy layer for the Design + Lore chats to nail down). The first time the Steward enters, an elder approaches and asks them to perform the shelving test: fetch a specific bio-substrate item from the cold-aisle shelves. The player picks an item and returns. The elder asks: *"Did you take from the front of the shelf, or the back?"*
   - **First attempt, 50/50**: if the player picks front-of-shelf → comic dismissal (*"Front-of-shelf is the older stock, rotated forward to clear inventory. You have selected the Melnorme equivalent of yesterday's bread. We will give you the test again. Return to the cold-aisle."*). The Steward goes back. *Now they know the trick.*
   - **Second attempt**: the player picks back-of-shelf → *"Passable. Lucky once, deliberate twice — the second is what we needed to see. You may speak with the Council again."*
