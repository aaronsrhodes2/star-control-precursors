# aspect: 1:1
# notes: top-down combat sprite of the Planar Blade — the Planar's
# unique two-dimensional ship. Its trick is that it is literally
# PLANAR — zero thickness along its perpendicular axis. From above
# (the gameplay camera) it should look like a vivid iridescent ribbon
# stretched out — but with the implicit suggestion that EDGE-ON it
# would VANISH. Front-facing immunity via Edge-Align special.

A top-down orthographic view of a Planar Blade starship. Shape:
a long narrow ribbon-bodied ship, ~6:1 aspect, oriented vertically
in the frame (nose to top), tapered to a fine point at both ends.
The hull is a single thin sheet of iridescent material — deep
teal in the center shifting to royal violet at one edge and
gold-leaf yellow at the other, like an oil-slick on water or a
hummingbird feather, the colors shifting in bands along the
length. Faint refractive shimmer suggests the planar nature of
the body. Two small thruster nodes glow soft cyan at the rear
end, casting a thin vapor-wake. At the center-of-mass: a single
small dark optical pip — the Planar individual piloting it. No
"hull thickness" markings — the ship should look flat as a knife
blade from this top-down view. Background: deep starfield, near
black. Style: stylized 1990s sci-fi game art, painterly with
crisp silhouette, vivid saturated iridescence. The ship must read
INSTANTLY as a flat blade, not as a slender SC2-style ship.
