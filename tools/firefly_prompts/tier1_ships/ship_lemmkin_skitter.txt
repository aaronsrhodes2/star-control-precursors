A top-down orthographic view of a Lemmkin Skitter starship.
Shape: a compact ~2:1 ovoid hull with a sharply tapered nose and a
long fluffy thruster-shroud at the rear (visually echoing the
species' fluffy tail — a clear deliberate diegetic homage). Hull
plates are mismatched scraps of russet-orange, cream, and burnished
copper riveted together in a friendly patchwork (improvised, but
not crude — careful craftsmanship by curious paws). Twin forward
BURST-SCATTER PROBE cannon barrels protrude from the nose like
oversized chestnut-shaped emitters with brass coils. A small
hatched panel under the tail-shroud — the TAIL-DROP package
ejector — glows soft amber, with a brass-rimmed iris waiting to
open. Hull viewports are ROUND PORTHOLE-WINDOWS arranged in a
cheerful row down the dorsal centerline (a Lemmkin Curator visible
peering out of the bridge porthole — round black eyes, bright
with interest, NO fear). A short stubby thruster fin on each side
glows soft cyan-violet. Style: stylized 1990s sci-fi game art,
painterly with crisp silhouette, vivid saturated colors, deep
black background. Tone: GLASS CANNON, FAST, OBVIOUSLY-FRAGILE,
clearly built by a species that thought "what if our spaceship had
a TAIL?" — endearing, brave, doomed.
