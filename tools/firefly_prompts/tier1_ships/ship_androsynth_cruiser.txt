faithful to the SC2-canonical Androsynth Guardian silhouette.
SHAPE: a VERTICAL SPINDLE — a stack of 4 to 5 dark armored
disc-segments threaded on a central longitudinal spine, like a
vertebral column. Each disc is a flattened armored ring of
gunmetal-grey to near-black plating with deep panel-line detail.

each end of the spine (one at the top, one at the bottom), and
smaller ruby-red orb-glints peek out from between the disc
segments where the spine is exposed. The orbs are glowing softly
from within — internal plasma or warp-core light. A small
slender antenna-like protrusion rises from the topmost orb. The
hull palette is matte dark grey to black with red as the only
accent; no blue, no cyan. Stylistic note: the silhouette must
read instantly as "stacked dark discs on a spine with red bulbs
at the ends" — this is the NON-NEGOTIABLE canonical Guardian
shape. Background: pure deep-black starfield, no horizon, no
nebula. Lighting: dramatic single key-light from upper-left
catching the disc-edges and glinting on the red orbs.

silhouette): a single small warm-amber circular hatch glows on
one of the middle disc segments — the time-displaced refugees'

not war".

Style: stylized 1990s sci-fi game art rendered at modern
