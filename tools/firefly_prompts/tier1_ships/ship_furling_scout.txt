A top-down orthographic view of a Furling Scout starship — the
player's vessel. Silhouette inspired by the Vulcan D'Kyr-class
cruiser from Star Trek: a LONG SLENDER YACHT-LIKE MAIN HULL
oriented vertically in the frame (nose to top), ~4:1 aspect.
The hull is LONG — the nose tapers to a teardrop point and
extends well forward into the upper third of the frame; the
rear thruster fluting fills the lower section.

THE RING: AROUND THE FORWARD-MIDDLE AXIS of the hull, a LARGE
RING-SHAPED NACELLE sits concentric with the hull, slightly
larger in diameter than the hull's beam. The hull is LONG
ENOUGH THAT THE NOSE PROTRUDES FORWARD OF THE RING — at least
20–30% of the hull is visible AHEAD of the front edge of the
ring (the nose pokes through and continues forward). Roughly
half of the hull (the body and rear) is visible BEHIND the ring
as well. This is the visual hook: the long slender hull THREADS
THROUGH the ring, with hull visible both forward and behind.

The ring's inner rim glows soft red-amber (the warp halo). The
ring is held in place by 2–4 slender support pylons connecting
it to the hull. Hull color: warm copper-cream with brass
bracketing at the seams and a Furling glyph etched at the
ring-support junction. Main thruster array at the rear glows
soft cyan-violet, with 2-3 secondary maneuvering jets at the
hull's mid-section. A small central optical pip / bridge-canopy
on the dorsal centerline (the Steward's bridge).

Background: deep starfield, near-black. Style: stylized 1990s
sci-fi game art, painterly with crisp silhouette, vivid
contrast. Tone: ELEGANT-EXPLORER, the ship of a careful
gardener-civilization, NOT a warship. Reference rhyme: Vulcan
D'Kyr, but warmer (copper instead of bronze, organic curves
instead of strict geometry).

NEGATIVE: ring around the rear of the hull, ring obscuring the
nose, ring surrounding the whole hull as a halo, short stubby
hull, hull entirely inside the ring's footprint. The nose MUST
be visible forward of the ring.
