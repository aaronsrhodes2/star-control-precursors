A top-down orthographic view of an Others' Vessel. Shape: roughly
a 1:1 footprint occupying a vague disc-region, but the visible
hull is NOT a solid mass — it is a TANGLE of inverted geometry,
black ribbons that fold through themselves at impossible angles,
edges that LEAD INTO each other in ways that should not connect.
Color: ABSOLUTE BLACK as the dominant value (a black darker than
space) with brief wrong-light highlights of a non-spectral
greenish-violet that does not match any other ship's lighting in
the universe. Surrounding the vessel: a faint perturbation halo
of bent space — background stars subtly LENSING and STRETCHING
around the edges, as if the vessel is gravitationally folding
the spacetime it sits in. No visible engine, no visible weapons,
no visible cockpit. Just wrong geometry, drinking light. Style:
stylized 1990s sci-fi game art, painterly. Tone: HORRIFYING,
INCOMPREHENSIBLE, the only ship in the game that is NOT made by
hands. Background: deep starfield with lensing artifacts around
the vessel.
