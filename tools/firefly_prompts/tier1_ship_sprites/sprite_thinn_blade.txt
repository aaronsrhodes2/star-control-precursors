Top-down 2D game sprite of a THINN BLADE starship (formerly
called "Planar Blade").

SHAPE: a LONG NARROW RIBBON-BODIED ship, ~6:1 aspect,
oriented vertically in the frame (nose to top), tapered to
a fine point at BOTH ends. The hull is a single thin sheet
of iridescent material — DEEP TEAL in the center shifting
to ROYAL VIOLET at one long edge and GOLD-LEAF YELLOW at
the other, like an oil-slick on water or a hummingbird
feather. Faint refractive shimmer suggests the planar
nature of the body — the ship has ZERO thickness along its
perpendicular axis. From this top-down view, the ship looks
flat as a knife blade.

WEAPON: a small forward LATERAL-CUT EMITTER at the top tip
of the ribbon — a tiny dark optical pip behind a thin
opening, hinting at a slim coherent slicing beam that
propagates along the ribbon's plane. The Thinn's defensive
trick (Edge-Align dodging) is implied by the ship's
flatness; no special weapon ornament is needed.

COLOR: iridescent teal-violet-gold ribbon shifting in bands
along its length. Two small thruster nodes glow soft cyan at
the rear, casting a thin vapor-wake. At the center-of-mass:
a single small dark optical pip — the Thinn individual
piloting.

TOP-DOWN ORTHOGRAPHIC VIEW: camera directly above. Subject
centered, vertically oriented, ~6:1 aspect.

BACKGROUND: solid pure black (#000000), edge to edge. NO
starfield, NO clouds, NO horizon, NO nebula, NO ground.

STYLE: stylized 1990s sci-fi game sprite at modern fidelity.
Crisp readable silhouette. Tone: ELEGANT, FLAT, the ship
must read INSTANTLY as a 2D ribbon, not as a slender SC2-
style ship.

NEGATIVE: starfield, stars, nebula, perspective view,
three-quarter view, side profile, ground, horizon, painterly
background bleed, three-dimensional hull, thick body,
visible side panels, wings.
