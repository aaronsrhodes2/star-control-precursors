Top-down 2D game sprite of a LEMMKIN SKITTER starship.

SHAPE: compact ~2:1 OVOID HULL with a sharply tapered nose
at the top and a long fluffy thruster-shroud at the rear
(visually echoing the Lemmkin species' fluffy tail — a
clear deliberate diegetic homage). The fluffy tail-shroud
trails behind the ship like a brush. Hull made of mismatched
scrap plates rivetted into a cheerful patchwork. A row of
round porthole windows down the dorsal centerline (small
black ovals along the hull's spine).

WEAPONS: TWIN FORWARD BURST-SCATTER PROBE CANNONS — two
chestnut-shaped emitter barrels protruding from the nose,
wrapped in brass coils, with small amber muzzle glows. Below
the tail-shroud, a small hatched panel — the TAIL-DROP MINE
ejector — glowing soft amber through a brass-rimmed iris
(the mine-package release point).

COLOR: bright russet-orange and cream hull plates riveted
together with burnished copper trim. Small cyan-violet
thruster glow visible through the fluffy tail-shroud. Brass
accents at every joint.

TOP-DOWN ORTHOGRAPHIC VIEW: camera directly above. Subject
centered, ~70% of frame.

BACKGROUND: solid pure black (#000000), edge to edge. NO
starfield, NO clouds, NO horizon, NO nebula, NO ground.

STYLE: stylized 1990s sci-fi game sprite at modern fidelity.
Crisp readable silhouette. Tone: GLASS CANNON, FAST,
OBVIOUSLY-IMPROVISED, friendly fearless curiosity — a ship
built by a species that thought "what if our spaceship had
a TAIL?"

NEGATIVE: starfield, stars, nebula, perspective view,
three-quarter view, side profile, ground, horizon, painterly
background bleed, sleek warship aesthetic, military uniformity.
