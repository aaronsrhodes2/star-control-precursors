Top-down 2D game sprite of a FURLING SCOUT starship. Nose
pointing toward the top of the frame.

SHAPE: long slender yacht-like hull oriented vertically, ~4:1
aspect, with a tapered teardrop nose at top. Around the FORWARD
third of the hull: a LARGE RING-SHAPED NACELLE concentric with
the hull, slightly wider than the hull beam. THE HULL EXTENDS
FORWARD OF THE RING — the nose protrudes past the front edge
of the ring. Half the hull is also visible BEHIND the ring.
2 to 4 slender pylons connect the ring to the hull.

WEAPON: forward-mounted FURLING BEAM EMITTER at the very tip of
the nose — a small recessed lens behind a brass aperture,
glowing warm gold (matches the beam's color). One single
forward-arc emitter, no turrets.

COLOR: warm copper-cream hull with brass bracketing at the
seams; ring inner-rim glows soft red-amber; main thrusters at
the rear glow soft cyan-violet. Small Furling glyph etched at
the ring-pylon junction.

TOP-DOWN ORTHOGRAPHIC VIEW: camera directly above. Symmetric
across the vertical axis. Subject centered, ~70% of frame.

BACKGROUND: solid pure black (#000000), edge to edge. NO
starfield, NO clouds, NO horizon, NO nebula, NO ground.

STYLE: stylized 1990s sci-fi game sprite at modern fidelity.
Crisp readable silhouette. Painterly surface detail tight
inside the silhouette. Tone: ELEGANT-EXPLORER, the careful
gardener-civilization's ship — NOT a warship.

NEGATIVE: starfield, stars, nebula, atmospheric haze,
perspective view, three-quarter view, side profile, ground,
horizon, painterly background bleed, ring obscuring the nose,
short stubby hull, hull entirely inside the ring footprint.
