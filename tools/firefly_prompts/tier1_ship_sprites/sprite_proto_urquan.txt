Top-down 2D game sprite of a PROTO-UR-QUAN WARSHIP.

SHAPE: HEAVY AGGRESSIVE WARSHIP, ~1.7:1 aspect, oriented
vertically with a brutal armored prow at the top. Broad
across the beam. Thick segmented armor plates with dark
panel lines suggesting a primitive-but-effective build (mid-
uplift; the species has weapons but their refinement is
crude). Twin heavy thruster nacelles at the rear flanking a
broad keel.

WEAPONS: at the very prow, a CRUSHING CLAW CLUSTER — an
arrangement of 3 to 5 dark-bronze crusher-pincers arrayed in

Crushing-Claw Burst projector). Each pincer has visible
hydraulic-style musculature, like a primitive shellfish-claw
mechanism scaled up to warship size. A faint deep-blue glow
emanates from the central bore (matches the projectile
color). The ship's MASS itself is its Ram Charge weapon —
the prow is reinforced with a heavy battering ram plate.

COLOR: deep dark-blue hull (Proto-Ur-Quan species color
indicating this is the pre-Ur-Quan lineage) with bronze
crusher-pincers and faint blue weapon-glow. Brass detailing
at structural seams.

TOP-DOWN ORTHOGRAPHIC VIEW: camera directly above. Symmetric.
Subject centered, ~75% of frame.

BACKGROUND: solid pure black (#000000), edge to edge. NO
starfield, NO clouds, NO horizon, NO nebula, NO ground.

STYLE: stylized 1990s sci-fi game sprite at modern fidelity.

species that will become the Ur-Quan — but here, mid-uplift,
