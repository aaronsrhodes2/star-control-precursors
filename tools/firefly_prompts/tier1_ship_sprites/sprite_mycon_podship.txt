Top-down 2D game ship sprite of a MYCON PODSHIP.

SHAPE: a bulbous ORGANIC POD-SHIP, *grown* not built. Round
asymmetric body shaped like an irregular seed pod. Fibrous
surface texture with deep grooves. Bio-luminescent veins of
soft pink-red pulse across the hull. A central PLASMOID-
EMISSION APERTURE faces forward — visible as a dark mouth
ringed with bright bioluminescent glow. Around the perimeter:
defensive POD-SPIKES (~6) jutting outward, also bioluminescent
at their tips.

COLOR: dark purple-red hull base (RGB ~200,90,130 — like
dried blood with a hint of fungal pink). Bright magenta-red
glow at apertures and vein tips. Surface texture suggests
chitin-and-flesh hybrid.

TOP-DOWN ORTHOGRAPHIC VIEW: camera directly above. Plasmoid
aperture facing TOP of frame. Subject centered, ~70% of
frame. Slightly asymmetric is OK — it's organic.

BACKGROUND: solid pure black (#000000), edge to edge. NO
starfield, NO nebula, NO horizon.

STYLE: stylized 1990s sci-fi game sprite at modern fidelity.
The ship reads as ALIVE, not built. Mycon-canon (SC2) look
but in our era — not yet fully awakened to sentience but the
biots are organizing into vessels.

NEGATIVE: starfield, stars, nebula, perspective view,
three-quarter view, side profile, ground, horizon, metallic
hull plates, geometric symmetry, manufactured aesthetic.
