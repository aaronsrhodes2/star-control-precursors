# notes: Sprite for Sentry Drone 47-Theta (unionized) — the canonical
# tutorial enemy. Small, slow, can only turn left (canon labor-drone
# limitation). Weapon: weak small-bore single gun (2 dmg). Should look
# like a clearly-improvised, labor-class drone — NOT a warship.

Top-down 2D game sprite of a SENTRY DRONE 47-THETA.

SHAPE: small COMPACT UTILITY DRONE — ~1:1 footprint, roughly
hexagonal hull with rounded corners. Industrial fabricator
finish, not refined. The drone has a clearly-improvised look,
like a converted cargo-handling unit pressed into a sentry
role rather than a purpose-built combatant. A small short-
range thruster sits on each of the 6 hex faces (omni-thrust
for a labor drone), faintly glowing soft white.

WEAPON: a SMALL FORWARD GUN mounted on one of the hex faces
— a single short barrel sticking out from a hastily-welded
mount. The gun looks LIGHT-DUTY: a 2-damage labor-grade
weapon, NOT a warship cannon. A small yellow-amber muzzle
glow.

DETAIL: a small unionized-labor BADGE sigil etched onto the
hull's dorsal centerline — a tiny diegetic wink for players
who notice (the drone is canonically a union member who
fights only because Local 47 mandates a single grievance
duel). The badge is a small flat circular emblem in faded
yellow paint.

COLOR: industrial light-grey and cool-grey hull with faded
yellow caution-stripe markings. Brass-and-rust accents at
the structural welds.

TOP-DOWN ORTHOGRAPHIC VIEW: camera directly above. Subject
centered, ~50% of frame (small drone — leave plenty of
black around it).

BACKGROUND: solid pure black (#000000), edge to edge. NO
starfield, NO clouds, NO horizon, NO nebula, NO ground.

STYLE: stylized 1990s sci-fi game sprite at modern fidelity.
Crisp readable silhouette. Tone: IMPROVISED, LABOR-CLASS,
SLIGHTLY-COMICAL, the only ship in the game with a union
card.

NEGATIVE: starfield, stars, nebula, perspective view,
three-quarter view, side profile, ground, horizon, painterly
background bleed, sleek warship aesthetic, heavy military
armor, intimidating silhouette.
