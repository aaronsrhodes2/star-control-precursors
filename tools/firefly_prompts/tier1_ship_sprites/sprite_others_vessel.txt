Top-down 2D game sprite of an OTHERS' VESSEL.

SHAPE: roughly 1:1 footprint occupying a vague disc-region,
but the visible hull is NOT a solid mass — it is a TANGLE of
INVERTED GEOMETRY: black ribbons that fold through themselves
at impossible angles, edges that LEAD INTO each other in ways
that should not connect. The silhouette reads as a knotted
black wireframe orb that the eye cannot trace fully. No
identifiable forward / rear / dorsal axis — but the overall
mass is centered and disc-like.

WEAPON: no visible muzzle, no visible cannon, no visible
emitter. The DECURSION weapon does not project from a barrel —
it propagates as a localized temporal-dimensional disturbance
from the entire vessel. Visual hint: occasional bright wrong-
light flickers of NON-SPECTRAL GREENISH-VIOLET pulsing through
the inverted geometry, suggesting weaponized wrongness
percolating from the hull itself.

COLOR: ABSOLUTE BLACK as the dominant value (darker than
space). Wrong-light highlights of a non-spectral greenish-
violet that doesn't match any other lighting in the universe.
Background-stars (where touched by the vessel's halo) subtly
LENS and STRETCH around its edges — visible space-folding
artifacts even though the background is otherwise pure black.

TOP-DOWN ORTHOGRAPHIC VIEW: camera directly above. Subject
centered, ~70% of frame.

BACKGROUND: solid pure black (#000000), edge to edge. NO
starfield, NO clouds, NO horizon, NO nebula, NO ground —
EXCEPT for faint lensing distortion artifacts immediately
around the vessel's outline (its gravitational fold-effect).

STYLE: stylized 1990s sci-fi game sprite at modern fidelity.
Tone: HORRIFYING, INCOMPREHENSIBLE, the only ship in the
game NOT made by hands.

NEGATIVE: solid hull, recognizable cockpit, recognizable
cannon, friendly silhouette, warm colors, balanced/symmetric
ornament, organic shapes, conventional spaceship, motion
blur, perspective view, three-quarter view, side profile.
