Top-down 2D game sprite of a PROTO-QOR-AH MARAUDER starship.

SHAPE: a BLADE-FORM hull — slim, aggressive, ~2.5:1 aspect,
oriented vertically with a sharp piercing nose at the top.
The body is a tapered ivory-and-black blade with sharp
edges. Surrounding the central hull: a HALO OF 4-6
DEPLOYABLE CUTTING RIB-SEGMENTS arrayed radially in a 360°
ring just outside the ship's silhouette, like a circular
saw frozen mid-swing. The blade-segments are extended
outward in this view (the Spin Blade is canonically
deployed when in combat).

WEAPON: the SPIN BLADE halo IS the weapon — its purpose is
to chew anything close. Each blade-segment has a sharpened
leading edge with a faint amber-orange glow at the tip. No
forward turrets; the whole ship is the weapon.

COLOR: cream-ivory hull plates with deep-black structural
accents and bronze internal hardware visible through cutaway
panels. The blade-segments are darker bronze-black with
amber edge-glow. A single small viewport amidships (the Qor-
Ah pilot's bubble) gleams red.

TOP-DOWN ORTHOGRAPHIC VIEW: camera directly above. Symmetric
across vertical axis. Subject centered, ~75% of frame
(the spin-blade halo fills the wider radius).

BACKGROUND: solid pure black (#000000), edge to edge. NO
starfield, NO clouds, NO horizon, NO nebula, NO ground.

STYLE: stylized 1990s sci-fi game sprite at modern fidelity.
Crisp readable silhouette. Tone: FANATIC, LETHAL-PURE,
SUICIDAL-AGGRESSIVE, a ship that exists ONLY to kill.

NEGATIVE: starfield, stars, nebula, perspective view,
three-quarter view, side profile, ground, horizon, painterly
background bleed, friendly aesthetic, defensive armor focus,
shielded hull.
