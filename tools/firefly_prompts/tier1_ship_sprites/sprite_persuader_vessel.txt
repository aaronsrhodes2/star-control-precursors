Top-down 2D game sprite of a PERSUADER VESSEL starship.

SHAPE: an ORICHALCUM-BRONZE ARMILLARY SPHERE — concentric
rings nested around a central oval pod. Three or four
rotating bronze hoops at different angles enclose a small
elongated central body. Brass spikes / antennae radiating
outward from key intersection points around the rim
(diplomatic broadcast spires). Roughly circular footprint
from above. From this top-down view, the rings read as
concentric ovals around the central pod.

WEAPON: forward-facing DIPLOMATIC SUASION EMITTER at the
"north" pole of the rings — a small brass aperture with a
warm amber glow (matches the Persuader Beam's gentle amber
color). The Dialog Amplifier is implied by additional
small antenna-spires around the rim.

COLOR: rich orichalcum / warm bronze with brass accents and
a small central golden glow at the pod's core. Faint amber
halo from the dialog-broadcast field.

TOP-DOWN ORTHOGRAPHIC VIEW: camera directly above. Subject
centered, ~70% of frame.

BACKGROUND: solid pure black (#000000), edge to edge. NO
starfield, NO clouds, NO horizon, NO nebula, NO ground.

STYLE: stylized 1990s sci-fi game sprite at modern fidelity.
Crisp readable silhouette. Painterly surface detail tight
inside the silhouette. Tone: DIPLOMATIC, ORNATE, RITUAL,
the ship of a faction that thinks language is a weapon.

NEGATIVE: starfield, stars, nebula, atmospheric haze,
