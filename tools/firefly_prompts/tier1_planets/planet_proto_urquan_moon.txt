# aspect: 1:1
# notes: planet sphere for the proto-Ur-Quan tidal-locked moon. The
# moon never rotates relative to its sun, so it has a permanent
# DAY-side and permanent NIGHT-side with a habitable terminator-band
# in between. The proto-Ur-Quan and the proto-Qor-Ah live in the
# terminator-band twilight.

A planet sphere viewed from medium orbit, filling the frame. A
tidal-locked moon, ~Earth-Moon-sized, orbiting a yellow dwarf
sun (off-frame, upper-left). The world is sharply divided into
three vertical bands: a SCORCHED DAY-side filling the left half
(red-brown desert cracked plains, white salt deposits, dry
beyond habitability), a NARROW HABITABLE TERMINATOR-BAND down
the center (cool blue lakes, dark vegetation in dusk-green and
purple, rivers running across the band like veins), and a
FROZEN NIGHT-side filling the right half (ice-blue glaciers
under starlight, with the rare amber pinprick of a settlement-
fire here and there). The proto-Ur-Quan and proto-Qor-Ah live
in the habitable strip — visible as scattered dark pinpricks of
encampment clustering near the terminator lakes. A thin
atmosphere refracts at the terminator boundary. No artificial
infrastructure visible (this is still the pre-language uplift
era). Style: stylized 1990s sci-fi game asset, painterly, crisp
circular silhouette. Tone: A WORLD THAT FORCES ITS INHABITANTS
INTO A SLIVER — the harsh environment is half the reason the
proto-Ur-Quan turned out dominance-oriented.
