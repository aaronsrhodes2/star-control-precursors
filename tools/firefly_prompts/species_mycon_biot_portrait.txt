A close-up portrait of a "Mycon Biot" — a Star-Control-universe
fungal terraforming worker engineered by an ancient civilization.
A bulbous mass of pale ochre and warm-grey fungal tissue, the size
of a torso, asymmetric and slowly pulsing. From its sides extrude
3-5 thin motile spore-tendrils that float as if in liquid, each
tipped with a tiny chitinous sensor-bud. At its center, a soft
internal glow — a bioluminescent centroid in cool teal-cyan — is
just beginning to manifest, suggesting a nascent consciousness
("the Deep Child") waking up inside the tool. Visible fine spore-
dust drifts around the figure, gold-flecked motes against the dark.
Lighting: warm low-angle illumination, deep shadow above, set
against a near-black volcanic mantle-vent backdrop with a faint
glow of magma below. Style: stylized 1990s sci-fi game portrait,
painterly, crisp silhouette. The biot should look strange and
biological but not horror — it is a worker, not a monster, in this
era. Subtle uncanny quality from the central glow.
