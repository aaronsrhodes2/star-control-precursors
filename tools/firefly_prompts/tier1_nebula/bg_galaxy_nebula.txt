# aspect: 3:4 (or 4:5 — match the original 3200x4258 poster ~3:4)
# notes: nebula-only galaxy backdrop. Generate using sc2-starmap.png as
# a Reference Image so Firefly anchors the color palette to the
# original, but EVERYTHING except the nebular gas is stripped:
#   - NO star points
#   - NO control-zone borders or color overlays
#   - NO species labels, constellation names, or titles
#   - NO constellation lines
# This becomes the L1 layer in the new hyperspace map; stars are
# rendered procedurally on top by StarRenderer.

A vast top-down galactic-scale nebular backdrop, painterly, vertical
orientation roughly 3:4. Deep cosmic black base. Across it: large,
slow swirls of dust and ionized gas in the Star Control 2 palette —
deep teal, rose-magenta, indigo, amber-gold, dusty cyan. The clouds
should feel like spiral-arm material, with brighter "core" regions
toward the center-right and progressively dimmer outer regions
toward the edges. Subtle organic flowing structures suggesting where
ancient stellar nurseries might have collapsed.

ABSOLUTELY EXCLUDE: star points of any kind (no white dots, no glints,
no glow centers), constellation lines, control-zone color blobs or
borders, species emblems, text labels, planet sprites, ship sprites,
UI overlays, frame borders. The image should look like a high-altitude
photograph of an INTERSTELLAR MEDIUM with no stars in it — gas only.

Style: stylized 1990s sci-fi game backdrop, painterly, beautiful but
ATMOSPHERIC not busy. The goal is a backdrop that ~500 procedurally-
drawn stars will look INHABITED inside, like jewels scattered across
a quilt of nebular gas.
