A vertical alien planet-surface backdrop, viewed straight-on as if
the camera stood at the avatar's eye level. Foreground: a level
weathered-stone surface with subtle alien lichen growth in pale
teal and gold, no figures. Mid-ground: stylized geological forms —
twisted basalt spires, low ridges. Background: an alien sky tinted
deep amber-orange near the horizon transitioning to deep violet at
zenith, two small distant moons visible. A faint dust-haze sits
just above the horizon. NO CHARACTERS, NO STRUCTURES, NO SHIPS —
completely empty stage ready to host an avatar composited on top
at runtime. Lighting: low alien sun creates long soft shadows
toward camera, warm key with cool ambient fill. Style: stylized
1990s sci-fi game backdrop, painterly, crisp at portrait-panel
display size (~420x600 pixels). The composition should leave the
middle vertical clear for an avatar figure.
