A bust portrait of a "Planar" alien — a member of a two-dimensional
sentient species from a Star-Control-universe game. The figure is
literally flat: a vertical ribbon-thin body with zero width, like a
sheet of stained glass standing on edge. The body is iridescent —
shifting through deep teal, royal violet, and gold-leaf yellow
depending on viewing angle, the way an oil slick or hummingbird
feather shifts. The face area shows a constellation of small
dark optical points arranged like punctuation along the ribbon's
upper edge. From the side it would be invisible; we view it at a
slight 3/4 angle so the planar shape reads clearly. Wisps of
"slipstream" — the disturbed air of the medium passing AROUND the
zero-thickness body — show as faint vapor trails on either side.
The arms (also planar) extend in graceful flat curves like the
edges of leaves. Background: a deep black void with a few stars,
plus a vivid splash of magenta-aurora behind the figure that the
ribbon-body partially eclipses, accentuating its impossible
thinness. Style: stylized 1990s sci-fi game portrait, painterly,
genuinely strange. Should feel alien in a way SC2 fans haven't
quite seen before — beauty plus uncanny dimensional wrongness.
