# aspect: 1:1
# notes: heraldic emblem for the Denier Furling faction — Homesteaders
# who refuse to accept the Others exist at all. They insist the rifts
# are natural phenomena. The "this is fine" faction. Should look
# willfully serene, almost academic — the calm of the deeply mistaken.

A heraldic faction insignia centered on a deep teal-blue field. The
emblem: a stylized open scholarly eye with a thin diagonal slash
across it (the Denier's signature: I have looked and I have decided
NOT to see). Behind the eye, a circular ring of arranged
constellation-points in pale silver, suggesting an orderly cosmos.
Below: a Furling-cuneiform scroll in subtle silver. Palette: deep
teal-blue, pale silver, ivory accents. Crisp graphic style — should
read clearly even at icon size in a UI corner. Centered composition,
symmetrical, no text in English. Style: stylized 1990s sci-fi game
faction heraldry, painterly but graphic. The insignia should read
as SERENE, ACADEMIC, and quietly WRONG.
