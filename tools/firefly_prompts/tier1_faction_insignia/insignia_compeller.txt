# notes: REROLLED 2026-05-17 — first attempt used human hands and a
# confusingly-oriented shield. Compeller heraldry should NOT use human
# anatomy and should NOT echo the Defender shield motif. Compellers
# are the pragmatist Furling sub-faction that uses sedation, deception,

NO TEXT, NO LABELS, NO WORDS, NO LETTERS, NO HUMAN ANATOMY. A
heraldic faction insignia centered on a dark slate-gray field.
The emblem: a stylized warm-amber DOORWAY rendered in flat
graphic style (an arched portal cut clean through a slate plate),
with a SHEPHERD'S CROOK (a long graceful curved-hook staff)
extended through the doorway from the foreground side, its hook
gently curling around a small abstract circular FORM (an
idealized stand-in for "the one being brought through") and
drawing it forward toward the doorway. The staff is held by NO
visible hand or limb — it floats as a pure heraldic symbol. The
circular form should NOT be a face or figure — just a featureless
disc with a soft glow, like a soul-token. Limited palette: warm
copper, soft amber, deep mahogany accents, white highlights —
warmer than the Defender's slate-and-bronze palette. Crisp flat
graphic style — should read clearly even at icon size in a UI
corner. Centered composition, symmetrical, no creatures, no
hands, no human anatomy, no text in any language. Style:
stylized 1990s sci-fi game faction heraldry, painterly but
graphic. Tone: FIRM COMPASSION, GUIDED PASSAGE, not coercion.
