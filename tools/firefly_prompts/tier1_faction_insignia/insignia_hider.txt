NO TEXT, NO LABELS, NO WORDS, NO LETTERS, NO HUMAN HANDS, NO HUMAN
ANATOMY, NO FINGERS. A heraldic faction insignia centered on a
deep midnight-blue field. The emblem: a horizontally-curved
CONCEALMENT-VEIL — like a low arched canopy or a stylized
parabolic shield — rendered in cool silver-on-black, arching
OVER a small glowing crystalline orb in cool blue-white at the
emblem's center (the cognition being sheltered). Light-rays
strike the veil from above and BEND AROUND the orb, traced as
five thin spectrum-bending lines that curve over the canopy and
exit at its sides without ever reaching the orb. Below the
canopy, a horizontal Furling-cuneiform inscription scroll in
subtle silver (pure heraldic glyph; no English). Palette:
midnight blue, soft silver, glowing cool blue-white center, hint
of spectrum-violet in the bending rays. Crisp graphic style —
should read clearly even at icon size in a UI corner. Centered
composition, symmetrical, NO anatomy, NO hands, NO faces, NO
creatures, NO text in any language. Style: stylized 1990s sci-fi
game faction heraldry, painterly but graphic. Tone: PROTECTIVE,
QUIET, REVERENT — the visual argument is "light bent away from
the thinking thing, so the thing cannot be seen."
