A gorgeous 2D game sprite of a SUN-LIKE YELLOW STAR, viewed from a
great distance. Brilliant yellow-white core with visible solar
granulation texture. Warm golden corona radiating outward in long
delicate streamers. A few visible solar prominences arcing off the
limb. The star feels familiar — a G-class main-sequence body, the
color of our Sol seen from interplanetary distance.

COLOR: brilliant CREAM-WHITE center (RGB ~255,250,220), surrounding
WARM-GOLD corona (RGB ~255,230,120), shifting to amber-orange near
prominence arcs, fading to deep black at the perimeter.

VIEW: head-on, sphere-disc centered, ~70% of frame diameter. Pure
black starless background outside the corona.

STYLE: painterly with photoreal stellar texture. Visible surface
granulation. Crisp, gorgeous, suitable as a hero element in a sci-fi
space game. NO stars in the background, NO planets, NO orbiting
bodies. Just the star, alone, in the void.
