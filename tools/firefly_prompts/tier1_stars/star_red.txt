A gorgeous 2D game sprite of a RED STAR, viewed from a great
distance. Deep red core with visible surface granulation and prominent
dark sunspots. Crimson corona radiating outward in long delicate
streamers. Several dramatic solar prominences arcing off the limb in
glowing rust-orange. The star feels ANCIENT — a slow-burning M-class
red body, late in its life cycle.

COLOR: dark CRIMSON center (RGB ~220,80,80), surrounding RUST-RED
corona (RGB ~255,110,100), with darker burgundy prominences and
near-black sunspots, fading to deep black at the perimeter.

VIEW: head-on, sphere-disc centered, ~70% of frame diameter. Pure
black starless background outside the corona.

STYLE: painterly with photoreal stellar texture. Visible surface
granulation, sunspots, dramatic prominences. Crisp, gorgeous,
suitable as a hero element in a sci-fi space game. NO stars in the
background, NO planets, NO orbiting bodies. Just the star, alone, in
the void.
