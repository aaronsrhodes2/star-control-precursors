A gorgeous DEEP-SPACE STARFIELD backdrop for a 2D top-down space
combat game. View: looking out into deep interstellar space. Hundreds
of stars at varying brightness and color (mostly white-cream with
occasional warm-amber and cool-blue accent stars). 4-6 BRIGHT
HERO STARS scattered across the frame, each with a soft diffraction
spike. Subtle faint NEBULAR HAZE at the corners — barely visible
soft purple and dusty teal cloud tints, mostly transparent. The
overall feeling is DEEP, QUIET, COSMIC — a peaceful void in which
two ships are about to fight.

COMPOSITION: starfield evenly distributed but not uniform — natural
clusters and quiet patches. Brightest stars sized small but visible.
Background is mostly very-dark-navy black, NOT pure black — a tiny
amount of ambient star-glow makes it feel like real deep space.

VIEW: 16:10 aspect ratio, fills entire frame. Suitable as a tiled
background that ships render over the top of.

STYLE: painterly with photoreal star fidelity. Hubble-like.
Atmospheric and immersive. NO ships, NO planets, NO ground, NO
horizon, NO motion lines, NO text, NO HUD elements. Just gorgeous
deep space.
