A gorgeous 2D game sprite of an ORANGE GIANT STAR, viewed from a
great distance. Warm orange-amber core with visible surface
granulation and a few large dark sunspots. Soft orange-red corona
radiating outward in long delicate streamers. Several visible solar
prominences arcing off the limb. The star feels OLDER and COOLER
than our Sun — a K-class body in middle age.

COLOR: warm AMBER center (RGB ~255,200,120), surrounding ORANGE-RED
corona (RGB ~255,180,100), with darker rust-amber prominences,
fading to deep black at the perimeter.

VIEW: head-on, sphere-disc centered, ~70% of frame diameter. Pure
black starless background outside the corona.

STYLE: painterly with photoreal stellar texture. Visible surface
granulation and sunspots. Crisp, gorgeous, suitable as a hero
element in a sci-fi space game. NO stars in the background, NO
planets, NO orbiting bodies. Just the star, alone, in the void.
