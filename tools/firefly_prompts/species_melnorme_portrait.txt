A close-up bust portrait of a "Melnorme Trade Master" — a Star
Control universe alien merchant. A rounded, bulbous, orange
exoskeletal pod the size of a head and shoulders, with a single
enormous luminous deep-blue cyclopean eye dominating the center.
The eye has a faint vertical-slit iris. Below the eye, a small
horizontal mouth-slit shows several small ivory teeth, conveying
a mercantile half-smile. The pod's surface is segmented orange
chitin with darker reddish creases, like an arthropod carapace.
A faint magenta/plasma-violet aura surrounds the figure — the
ionization signature of the gas-cloud energy-being piloting from
within. Lighting: dramatic warm rim-light from the right (a nearby
super-giant star), deep shadow on the left, set against a near-
black void with a few distant stars. Style: stylized 1990s game
portrait art, painterly with crisp silhouette, faithful to the
original Star Control II Melnorme silhouette but rendered at modern
fidelity. The character should look formal, mercantile, slightly
inscrutable. No human features; pure alien.
