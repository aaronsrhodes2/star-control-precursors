A wide cinematic still over the surface of Vulpeculae III, mid-Others-
attack. Above, the BLACK SPACE-TIME TUNNELS hang in the sky like
inverted whirlpools, drinking. From the city below — towers, suburban
domes, freighter spaceports — bright STREAMS OF LIGHT are rising in
slow spirals up to the tunnels: each stream is the cognitive trace of
an individual being pulled OUT of its body and drained INTO the wound
in space. The lights are different colors, different intensities,
showing the variety of the minds being taken — gold for elders, white
for children, blue-green for adults. Below them, the city itself is
intact: the buildings stand, no fires, no rubble. Tiny bodies lie
peacefully in the streets as their cognition is taken — the Others
do not kill the body, they EAT THE MIND, and the body simply ceases
to think. The horror is in the QUIET. Style: stylized 1990s sci-fi
game cutscene art, painterly. Tone: SOUL-CONSUMPTION, devastatingly
quiet, the most psychologically terrifying shot in the slice. No
HUD, no UI, no text. No visible gore or violence.
