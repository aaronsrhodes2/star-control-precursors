Cloaking Satellite. A roughly spherical crystalline lattice structure

brown gas giant, partially silhouetted against rolling methane
clouds. Lighting: cool blue inner glow + warm gas-giant ambient.

satellite should read as PROTECTIVE — elegant, intricate, given
