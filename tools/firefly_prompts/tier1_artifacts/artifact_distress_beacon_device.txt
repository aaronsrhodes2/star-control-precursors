Distress Beacon. A roughly cubical device about a meter on a side,
hull plates dented and stained, mounted on a tripod of mismatched
landing legs. Bristling with antennas of various lengths, several
exposed circuit boards under cracked covers, a central glowing
amber-yellow emitter lens pointing outward. Faded geometric Androsynth
clone-iconography stenciled in white on one panel: a stylized
clone-helix and the broken-chain symbol. Pulsing soft amber light
from the central lens. Floor beneath it shows scoring from the
moment of its dimensional displacement. Background: deep matte
black with a few cooling vapor wisps. Lighting: warm amber from
the device + cool rim light. Style: stylized 1990s sci-fi game
artifact-card art, painterly. The device should read as
DESPERATE — improvised, last-ditch — but still functioning.
