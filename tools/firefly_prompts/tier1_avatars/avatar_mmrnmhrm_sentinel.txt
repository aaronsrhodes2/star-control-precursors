A full-body avatar painting of a Mmrnmhrm Sentinel, an ancient
self-modifying robot. Humanoid silhouette but DELIBERATELY
ASYMMETRIC, ~2.0m tall. ONLY TWO LEGS — bipedal, no extra limbs.
The body is a PATCHWORK of mismatched repairs accumulated over
millions of years: torso plating in three different alloys
welded together (brushed titanium, tarnished copper-bronze,
matte gunmetal-gray), seams visible. ARMS ARE DIFFERENT FROM
EACH OTHER: left arm is shorter, factory-original, ending in a
THREE-FINGER manipulator with delicate articulation; right arm
is longer, clearly a later retrofit, ending in a FIVE-CLAW heavy
gripper. Shoulders held at different heights. One leg has a
visible knee-joint replacement (newer alloy bolted on); the
other is original equipment. Head: a single CENTRAL optical-
array dome glowing deep blue, set slightly OFF-CENTER on the
neck stem (the result of a head-grafting repair). NO face — just
sensors, seams, and a strip of subtle Mmrnmhrm geometric glyphs
etched into the chest in cool platinum (one glyph clearly added
later, brighter than its neighbors). Pose: standing in a quiet
three-quarter, asymmetric: weight on the left foot, right arm
hanging lower than the left, head tilted in computation.
Background: SOLID MATTE DARK NAVY (#0a0e1a), edge-to-edge.
Lighting: even soft three-point with cool blue rim from the head
dome, faint copper rim from the older patches. Style: stylized
1990s sci-fi game character portrait, painterly, melancholy,
ancient. Tone: ONE ROBOT, NEVER FIXED, JUST CARRIED FORWARD —
the asymmetry tells the millennia.

ARTICULATION BRIEF (rig: robot_humanoid + asymmetric):
- HEAD: separable, off-center on neck stem
- ARMS L/R: deliberately mismatched (short vs long, fingers vs claws)
- TORSO: three-alloy patchwork; clear seam lines for chip animation
- LEGS: bipedal, asymmetric knee mods
- Avoid: more than two legs, symmetric body, uniform finish
