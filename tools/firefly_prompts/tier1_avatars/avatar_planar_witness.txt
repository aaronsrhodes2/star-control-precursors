A full-body avatar painting of a Planar individual. Body plan: a
single rectangular PEARLESCENT GLASS SHEET, roughly 1.5m tall by
1m wide, floating in a slightly tilted (~15 degrees off-vertical)
orientation in midair — no support, no contact with the ground,
weightless. The sheet is essentially flat and roughly square-ish
with softly rounded corners. Surface is iridescent: deep teal at
the center shifting through royal violet at one edge and gold-
leaf yellow at the other, with a faint moiré-like internal
pattern that suggests the species's interiority (slow, drifting,
without a focal point). The sheet is OPAQUE to LIGHT but
TRANSLUCENT to SHADOW — soft glow inside, faint silhouettes
visible through it. NO LIMBS, NO EYES, NO FACE, NO ANATOMY. The
edges of the sheet glow softly with a one-pixel rim of pure
white-violet light. A faint vapor slipstream curls AROUND the
sheet, curving past the edge to indicate it has no thickness.
Pose: floating freely tilted in a casually-cocked angle, the
viewer sees the FLAT FACE of the sheet head-on. Background:
SOLID MATTE DARK NAVY (#0a0e1a), edge-to-edge, no scene elements.
Lighting: even soft three-point with the sheet's own pearlescent
glow as the dominant light source. Style: stylized 1990s sci-fi
game character portrait, painterly, abstract, alien. Tone: A
PERSON WHO IS A PANE OF LIGHT.

ARTICULATION BRIEF (rig: ribbon_planar — to be renamed planar_sheet):
- THE SHEET: the entire avatar, articulates by tilting / spinning
  in 3D space; cannot bend or fold
- INTERIOR PATTERN: the iridescent moiré can shift to express mood
- EDGE GLOW: brightens/dims with attention or speech
- Avoid: limbs, eyes, faces, ANY anatomical features whatsoever
