A full-body avatar painting of a Lemmkin Curator. Body plan: ~1.3 m
tall bipedal anthropomorphic squirrel. Dense russet-orange and
cream fur with a paler underbelly; a large fluffy tail (clearly
longer than the body) raised in a confident curve overhead. Head:
expressive squirrel features — black button nose, prominent
forward-facing whiskers, ROUND BLACK EYES set wide and very
ALERT, two upright tufted ears. Both eyes are bright with active
curiosity but show ZERO defensive squint — the gaze is open and
fearless in a way that is subtly wrong. Two arms with delicate
five-fingered paws — left paw holding a notebook-like archive
slab, right paw raised mid-gesture pointing at something fascinating
off-frame. Wears a leather-strap utility harness with brass-rimmed
sample tubes, an oilskin journal pouch, and a single carved acorn
pendant (clan marker). Pose: leaning forward slightly with bright
interest, weight on the front foot, tail held high — the body
language of a creature that has NEVER been told to be careful.
Background: SOLID MATTE DARK NAVY (#0a0e1a), edge-to-edge.
Lighting: even soft three-point with a faint warm amber rim from
the tail-fluff. Style: stylized 1990s sci-fi game character
portrait, painterly. Tone: ARDENT CURIOSITY, BIG SMART, NO FEAR.
