A full-body painted portrait of Elder Quor-Voh-Listening, an
Utwig shame-orator. Body plan: tall ~2.0m bipedal alien, GAUNT
AND SKINNY — long-limbed, narrow-chested, fingerbones visible
at the knuckles, ribs faintly evident under the skin. SKIN is
the dominant surface texture (NOT armor plates) — pale ashen
slate-gray with cool blue-violet undertones, smooth and slightly
translucent over the underlying anatomy, with subtle DARKER VEIN
patterns tracing down the neck and arms. A residual ridge of
darker keratin runs along the spine, the shoulders, and across
the brow — a vestigial echo of the old plate-armor, not full
coverage. HEAVY CRANIAL BROW-FOLD droops halfway over the face
(the "long shame" pose), large dark wet eyes still visible
beneath the hood — eyes are too big for the gaunt face, sad and
emotive. Wears a SEVERE BLACK ROBE in heavy fabric draped close
to the thin body, with charcoal-gray under-vestments, a wide
mourning-collar of black-on-black brocade rising at the neck,
and long open sleeves that hang past the wrists. A single carved
bone-keratin shame-mask hangs at the belt on a black leather
cord. Pose: standing tall but with shoulders slightly inward and
head bowed, weight even, hands clasped loosely at the waist —
the body language of someone who has been quiet for a very long
time. Background: SOLID MATTE DARK NAVY (#0a0e1a), edge-to-edge.
Lighting: cool moonlight key from one side, single warm amber
rim from a candle offscreen catching the cheekbone. Style:
stylized 1990s sci-fi game character portrait, painterly, GOTH-
MELANCHOLIC, theatrical, severe. Tone: DIGNIFIED SADNESS,
gaunt, ritual, a civilization that has chosen mourning as a
survival strategy.
