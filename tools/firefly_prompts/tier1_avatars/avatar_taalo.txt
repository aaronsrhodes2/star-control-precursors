A full-body avatar painting of a Taalo. Body plan: a ROUGHLY-OVOID
LUMP of mossy slate-and-quartz rock, ~1.3m tall and 1m wide at
its broadest, with a wide curtain-frill of FINER MINERAL FRINGES
around the entire BOTTOM edge — like a frilled stone skirt
extending downward to ground level, the locomotion organ that
ripples and shambles the body forward. The main mass: layered
slate-gray basalt with veins of pale rose-quartz and patches of
moss-green silicate lichen. Surface is rough and natural, NOT
smooth, NOT polished — this looks like a piece of mountainside
that decided to walk. Near the top: a SLIT MOUTH that hangs
slightly open, faintly drooling a clear acid bead (they eat rock
chemically). NO eyes are obvious — only a horizontal band of
matte compound geo-sensor patches just above the mouth, almost
the same color as the surrounding stone (you have to look for
them). NO arms, NO limbs above the curtain — manipulation, if
needed, happens by extruding small mineral pseudopod-buds from
the side. A simple woven-fiber sash crosses the upper third of
the body, hung with small smooth pebble-tools. Pose: standing
upright (centered, slightly tilted to one side as if balanced
mid-shamble), the bottom curtain fringes visibly rippling in a
slow forward-wave. Background: SOLID MATTE DARK NAVY (#0a0e1a),
edge-to-edge. Lighting: even soft three-point with a faint moss-
green rim from the lichen, faint warm rim from the acid bead.
Style: stylized 1990s sci-fi game character portrait, painterly.
Tone: A LUMP OF MOUNTAINSIDE WITH AGENCY — ancient, geological,
shambling, doomed (they will calcify into ordinary rock in the
slice; the Shield will fail).

ARTICULATION BRIEF (rig: rock_shambler — replaces bipedal_humanoid):
- BODY LUMP: the entire main mass, articulates by leaning + bobbing
- CURTAIN FRILLS: the bottom fringe ripples for locomotion
- MOUTH: slit, can open wider for talk; acid drip is animated
- PSEUDOPOD-BUDS: optional small extrusions from the sides
- Avoid: humanoid silhouette, arms, legs, prominent eyes, smooth
  polished surface — this is rough WALKING ROCK
