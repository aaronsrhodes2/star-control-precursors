A full-body avatar painting of Trade Master Vermilion, a Melnorme
trader. Body plan: a vertical cyclopean TRANSPARENT TRADE POD ~2.5
m tall on three short tripod gimbal-legs. The pod is the primary
mass — wider than any of its arms and clearly NOT humanoid in
silhouette. INSIDE the pod: a slow-swirling sentient gas cloud in
mottled saffron-orange, rust-red and deep mahogany, reorganizing
in lazy thoughtful patterns. The cloud has NO face, NO eyes, NO
visible head — it is simply weather inside glass. The pod glass
itself is reinforced with copper-bracketing top and bottom and a
single brass-rimmed speaker grille near the upper third. TWO
slender SKELETAL MECHANICAL MANIPULATOR ARMS extend from sockets
on the pod's mid-section — these arms are obviously industrial:
hydraulic-rod construction, three exposed pivot joints each,
ending in DELICATE THREE-CLAW pincers that look like a jeweler's
tweezers, NOT hands. One pincer is raised mid-gesture, the other
hangs relaxed. The pod stands on three short articulated tripod-
legs with rubberized pad-feet. NO HUMAN FORM ANYWHERE in the
composition. Background: SOLID MATTE DARK NAVY (#0a0e1a), edge-
to-edge. Lighting: even soft three-point with warm amber rim from
the internal cloud, cool cyan rim from the pod indicator lights.
Style: stylized 1990s sci-fi game character portrait, painterly.
Tone: ALIEN MACHINERY HOUSING A WEATHER-SYSTEM — not a person.
