# notes: planet sphere as seen from orbital approach. Wants to match
# the existing UQM planet-sprite convention (round disc centered,
# slight terminator shadow, no orbit ring overlay). Drahn is the
# Melnorme homeworld — yellow dwarf orbit, unusual hot-deep-mantle
# world, will be destroyed in the lore.

A planet sphere viewed from medium orbit, filling the frame. World
name: Drahn. Surface ranges from dark obsidian-rocky plains to
crystalline-encrusted highlands of pale violet quartz. Spider-
webbing across the whole surface: bright actinic-blue "lightning
rivers" — ionization channels running between mantle vents,
glowing as if etched on the planet by an arc-welder. Glowing
orange-red dots are open mantle vents emitting heat. A thin
atmosphere refracts the actinic-blue glow as a faint cyan halo
around the planet's limb. The terminator shadow falls across the
right third, with the lightning-rivers still glowing through the
nightside, brighter against the dark. Lit by a warm yellow-dwarf
sun from the upper left. Background: deep starfield. Style:
stylized 1990s sci-fi game asset, painterly, crisp circular
silhouette suitable as a sprite. Drahn should feel ALIVE — like a
planet that is itself a piece of operating machinery.
