# Denier identity: the Furling sub-faction that refuses to admit the
# Others exist. Pacifist denial. Color: pale yellow-beige (sunny,
# untroubled). Accessory: ceremonial blindfold + paper-scroll
# (denial as ritual — they read approved doctrine, refuse new data).

ONE single TALL SHAGGY FUR-LUMP alien character, standing upright,
photographed in soft museum-portrait studio lighting against a
plain dark backdrop. So densely covered in long matted fur that
you cannot count its legs — the body silhouette is a heavy
furred TEARDROP from shoulders to the floor, like a Snuffleupagus
or Cousin Itt. Two LONG furry arms hang at its sides with pale
clawed hands visible at the wrists. Two BIG triangular ears poke
up through the fur on top of its head. A small partial face is
barely visible behind a curtain of fur.

FUR COLOR (Denier-specific): pale wheaten-yellow / soft beige
matted shag — untroubled, sun-bleached, almost holiday-cheerful.
NOT deep red (Cleanser), NOT warm gold (Persuader).

ACCESSORY (Denier-specific): a CEREMONIAL CLOTH BLINDFOLD tied
across the eye-zone of the face (over the fur), pale white with
embroidered scroll-glyphs. A small paper-prayer-scroll tucked
into the throat-fur. The Deniers literally cover their own eyes
— ritualized refusal to see.

POSE: standing relaxed three-quarter, full body, head slightly
turned away from the camera as if politely declining to look.

STYLE: painterly stylized sci-fi character portrait. Reads as
PASSIVE-AGGRESSIVELY POLITE — the Denier believes if you don't
see a problem, it isn't there. Cinematic museum-portrait
lighting. NO humanoid alien face, NO bipedal silhouette, NO
visible feet, NO short fur, NO sci-fi armor.
