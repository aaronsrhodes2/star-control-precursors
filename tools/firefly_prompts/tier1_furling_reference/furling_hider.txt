ONE single TALL SHAGGY FUR-LUMP alien character, standing upright,
photographed in soft museum-portrait studio lighting against a
plain dark backdrop. So densely covered in long matted fur that
you cannot count its legs — the body silhouette is a heavy
furred TEARDROP from shoulders to the floor, like a Snuffleupagus
or Cousin Itt. Two LONG furry arms hang at its sides with dark
clawed hands visible at the wrists. Two BIG triangular ears poke
up through the fur on top of its head. A small partial face is
barely visible behind a curtain of fur.

FUR COLOR (Hider-specific): deep midnight indigo-blue and
charcoal-grey matted shag, with the outer fringe of the silhouette
fading SOFTLY into the dark backdrop — as if the character is
partially cloaked / camouflaged. The edges should look like
they're DISSOLVING into the shadow, not crisply outlined. NOT
deep red (Cleanser), NOT pale beige (Denier).

ACCESSORY (Hider-specific): a dark grey hood-like LAYERED MANTLE
of fur over the head and shoulders (an extra layer of darker fur,
like a built-in cloak) — emphasizes the hide-in-shadow theme.
A small silver fade-glyph token at the throat.

POSE: standing relaxed three-quarter, full body, head slightly
ducked, partial profile.

STYLE: painterly stylized sci-fi character portrait. Reads as
QUIET and WATCHFUL — the Hider's strategy is invisibility.
Cinematic museum-portrait lighting with strong shadow contrast.
NO humanoid alien face, NO bipedal silhouette, NO visible feet,
NO short fur, NO sci-fi armor, NO hood covering the face entirely.
