A top-down orthographic view of a small Furling lander drone in
the MOMENT OF DESTRUCTION above an alien planet surface. Same
compact rounded copper-and-cream body as the canonical lander,
same four downward-facing hover-thruster nodes — but the body
is now CRACKED OPEN along one side with a jagged dark scar of
burned-through metal, the hover-thrusters are MISFIRING (one
nozzle dark, one sputtering a stunted violet flare, the other
two giving uneven gimbal twitches), and a brief OVERLAY of
yellow-white explosion plume blooms around the breach (small, not
catastrophic — the drone is wounded, not vaporized). The tractor-
beam aperture below is OFFLINE, dark. The drone is mid-tumble —
it has rotated ~15° off horizontal. Tiny debris fragments are
flung outward. NO LEGS, NO LANDING STRUTS — still a pure
hovering form. Background: deep black with a faint hint of the
planet surface below. Style: stylized 1990s sci-fi game art,
painterly with crisp silhouette, vivid contrast.
