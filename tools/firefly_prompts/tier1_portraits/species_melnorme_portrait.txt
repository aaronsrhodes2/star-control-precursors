Control universe alien merchant. The frame shows the upper TWO-

rounded bulbous orange exoskeletal sphere with a single enormous

right (a nearby super-giant star), deep shadow on the left, set
against a near-black void with a few distant stars. Style:
stylized 1990s game portrait art, painterly with crisp silhouette,
