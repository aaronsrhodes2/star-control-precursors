A vertical full-figure painting of a single Mycon Biot — a
GIANT MUSHROOM STALK grown onto the interior floor of a Mycon
ship. The biot occupies the center of the frame as a tall,
columnar fungal stalk roughly 2m to 2.5m tall, NOT a creature-
portrait of a face. STALK ANATOMY: a thick living fungal column
in warm ochre and pale-grey-cream tones, slightly bulbous-
topped (the upper crown is a soft asymmetric dome, NOT a
clean mushroom cap), tapering toward a wider rooted base. The
upper crown carries a SOFT INTERNAL TEAL-CYAN GLOW — a
bioluminescent centroid visible through the translucent flesh,
suggesting the nascent Deep Child consciousness awakening
inside. Subtle gill-like fluting wreathes the upper third. NO
FACE, NO HUMANOID BUST, NO EYES, NO ARMS.

ROOTED TO THE SHIP FLOOR: at the biot's base, the stalk fuses
INTO the deck plating of a Mycon vessel's interior — bio-fused
flesh grading into hexagonal mycelial root-traceries that
spread visibly across the metal floor panels in a 1.5m radius
around the base, like the column was GROWN there over years.
A faint groove-pattern in the floor radiates outward (older
growth-rings).

INTERIOR SETTING: behind the biot, the dim warm-orange-lit
INTERIOR of a Mycon ship — rusted-organic bulkheads, hex-pattern
deck plating extending into the soft-focus distance, a few small
warm amber pilot-lights mounted at low height. NO planet, NO
starfield, NO horizon, NO wilderness, NO microscopic-foreground-
spore-dust. The scale reads as a HALL OF GROWN BIOTS, intimate
but not close-up — the camera frames the single column in its
ship-interior context.

Lighting: warm amber low-key from below (the ship's mantle-
vent floor lights catching the rooted base), with a cool cyan
secondary from the biot's internal glow. Style: stylized 1990s
sci-fi game art, painterly, crisp silhouette, BOTANICAL-
ARCHITECTURAL, the tool that is starting to dream.

NEGATIVE: torso-sized creature, humanoid bust portrait,
floating spore-tendrils unattached to floor, face, eyes, arms,
hands, basketball-sized small biot, planet, moon, starfield,
horizon, wilderness, exterior setting, hyphae-afro hair.
