A close-up portrait of an Utwig Elder. Body plan: armored
bipedal grazer head and upper torso. Slate-gray bone-keratin
plates cover the skull, cream throat patch, ochre flank stripes
visible at the lower edge of the frame. The defining feature:
the HEAVY CRANIAL BROW-FOLD — a thick keratin shelf above the
eyes that PHYSICALLY DROPS to bury the face under shame
response. In this portrait the brow-fold is HALF-LOWERED,
veiling the upper half of the face — only the lower eyes and
mouth are visible. Eyes: large, sad, somewhere between dignified
and devastated. The remainder of the face is in shadow under
the fold. A simple leather-and-bone shoulder mantle indicates
elder status. Behind the figure: soft-focus cliffside ritual
gathering — others of the same species, faces already FULLY
veiled (brow-fold fully down), in a slow ceremonial line, mist
behind them. Lighting: cool late-twilight key, low-angle, with
a soft amber rim from a single ritual fire offscreen. Style:
stylized 1990s sci-fi game portrait, painterly, melancholy,
dignified. Tone: A SPECIES CHOOSING TO HIDE — the long shame is
already starting, and this is a portrait of the last face we
will see before the masks come down forever.
